The 30-Day Review

Article 17 · First 30 Days English · 30 天复盘

⏱ 8 min read 🟡 Intermediate 🎯 Run the review against the scoreboard: evidence per line, asks per gap, one upward suggestion

1. Scenario 场景背景

Day 30. The 1:1 Sarah promised: "we review the day-30 line together." You remember the scoreboard she set in week one — ask-to-unblock within a day, one thing shipped end-to-end — and you have spent the week assembling evidence, not memories. Today is the first meeting where you arrive as a professional, not a passenger.

第 30 天。Sarah 承诺的 1:1："我们一起对照第 30 天那条复盘。"你还记得她第一周定下的记分板——一天内解开阻塞的提问、端到端交付一件事——而你这周一直在收集证据，而不是回忆。今天是你第一次以专业人士而非乘客的身份到会。

The professional 30-day review runs against the agreed scoreboard: evidence per line, asks per gap, and — the part almost nobody does — one upward suggestion: something the team could improve, held loosely and offered respectfully.

职业化的 30 天复盘对照既定记分板进行：每条线配证据、每个缺口配请求，以及——几乎没人做的那部分——一条向上建议：一件团队可以改进的事，保留态度、尊重地提出。

2. Dialogue 英文对话

Sarah (Manager): Day 30 — your scoreboard, your evidence. Take it away.第 30 天——你的记分板，你的证据。请开始。
You (New Joiner): Against the two lines you set. Line one: "asks that unblock within a day." Evidence: nine asks this month; seven self-served via docs or pairing; two needed a person — median response under four hours. Line two: "one thing shipped end-to-end" — the churn dashboard, live Thursday, sign-off from Elena, doc updated.对照你定的两条线。第一条："一天内解开阻塞的提问。"证据：本月 9 次提问；7 次靠文档或结对自己解决；2 次需要人——响应中位数 4 小时以内。第二条："端到端交付一件事"——流失仪表盘，周四上线、Elena 签批、文档已更新。
Sarah (Manager): Evidence, not memories — exactly as asked. My assessment matches: day-30 line, met. Now the harder half — where did the scoreboard underserve what actually happened?是证据不是回忆——完全符合要求。我的评估一致：第 30 天那条，达成。现在到更难的一半——记分板在哪一点上没能覆盖实际发生的事？
You (New Joiner): Honest gap: the scoreboard says nothing about relationships. The month\'s real unlock was the question-routing map — eleven coffees, one routing question each. I would add a line for month two: "one cross-team relationship that unblocks without me asking."诚实的缺口：记分板完全没提关系。这个月真正的解锁是问题路由图——11 杯咖啡、每人一个路由问题。我建议给第二个月加一条："一个不需要我开口就能解阻塞的跨团队关系。"
Sarah (Manager): Accepted — that line goes in. Anything the month made harder than it should have been?采纳——这条加进去。这个月有什么事比本该的更难吗？
You (New Joiner): Two. Process: the production-data ticket took nine days — my week was planned around it landing in three. A new-joiner heads-up on real SLAs would save every future joiner a replan. And one upward suggestion, held loosely: the team\'s onboarding doc has no "who to ask for what" section — my routing map took eleven coffees to build; a starter map would cost one afternoon.两件。流程：生产数据工单花了 9 天——我的周计划按 3 天做的。给新人一份真实 SLA 预告，能为每个未来新人省一次重新规划。以及一条向上建议，保留态度地提出：团队的入职文档没有"什么问题找谁"一节——我的路由图花了 11 杯咖啡才建成；一张起步地图只需一个下午。
Sarah (Manager): The upward suggestion is the part I wait for in these reviews — new eyes see the estate better than residents. Both accepted: you draft the routing-map section, and I will own the SLA heads-up note. Day-60 line, then?向上建议正是这些复盘里我等待的部分——新眼睛看园区比老居民清楚。两条都采纳：你来起草路由图一节，SLA 预告我负责。那么，第 60 天那条？
You (New Joiner): Drafted with the coffee chat\'s words: "you stop being copied on my emails — I own the analysis area and its stakeholders." Evidence by day 60: the reconciliation analysis area, one stakeholder briefing delivered, CC list halved.用咖啡时的话起草："你不再被抄送我的邮件——我拥有分析领域及其利益相关方。"第 60 天的证据：对账分析领域、一次利益相关方汇报完成、抄送名单减半。
Sarah (Manager): Measurable, and in your own words. This is the best 30-day review I have run — and the credit is the format: scoreboard, evidence, gap, suggestion. Keep this format for every review of your career.可度量，且用你自己的话。这是我主持过的最好的 30 天复盘——功劳属于格式：记分板、证据、缺口、建议。职业生涯的每次复盘都保持这个格式。

3. Key Phrases 关键句型

  • Frame Your scoreboard, your evidence. 你的记分板，你的证据。 The 30-day review is not a verdict — it is your presentation, run against rules you both agreed.
  • Evidence Nine asks; seven self-served; median response under four hours. 9 次提问；7 次自助；响应中位数 4 小时内。 Evidence is counted, not remembered — numbers with definitions beat adjectives every time.
  • Gap The scoreboard says nothing about relationships. 记分板完全没提关系。 Critiquing the scoreboard itself — the mature move: the gap is in the measurement, not just in you.
  • Upward One upward suggestion, held loosely. 一条向上建议，保留态度地提出。 The review\'s rarest slot — the team-level improvement only a new joiner can still see.
  • Draft You stop being copied on my emails. 你不再被抄送我的邮件。 The day-60 line written in the manager\'s own vocabulary — evidence attached, measurable by design.
  • Format Scoreboard, evidence, gap, suggestion. 记分板、证据、缺口、建议。 Sarah\'s verdict: the format IS the credit. Keep it for every review of your career.

4. Vocabulary 词汇表

  • Scoreboard n. 记分板 The 30/60/90 expectations from your week-one coffee — the review's measuring stick.
  • Evidence n. 证据 Counted facts: nine asks, median response, sign-off date. Not memories.
  • Upward suggestion n. 向上建议 A team-level improvement offered respectfully — the review's rarest slot.
  • Routing map n. 路由图 Your who-to-ask map — now becoming an onboarding artifact.
  • SLA abbr. 服务等级协议 The real turnaround times new joiners need before planning weeks.
  • CC list n. 抄送名单 Being removed from it is the day-60 promotion — measurable by counting.
  • Day-60 line n. 第 60 天标准 The next scoreboard line, drafted in your manager's own words.
  • New eyes n. 新眼睛 What a new joiner sees that residents stopped noticing — valuable for 90 days only.

5. Grammar Notes 语法点

① Evidence syntax: counts with definitions

"Nine asks; seven self-served; median response under four hours." — three noun phrases with numbers and defined terms. No "I asked a lot of questions" — evidence English is [count] + [category] + [defined measure]. The semicolons do the stacking; the definitions do the credibility.

"Nine asks; seven self-served; median response under four hours."——三个带数字与定义的名词短语。不是"I asked a lot of questions"——证据英语是[数量] + [类别] + [定义过的度量]。分号负责堆叠，定义负责可信度。

② Held-loosely suggestions

"One upward suggestion, held loosely: the onboarding doc has no who-to-ask section — a starter map would cost one afternoon." — the grammar prices the suggestion in two moves: the held-loosely label (my view, not a verdict) and the costed proposal (one afternoon, not "someone should fix this"). Priced suggestions get accepted; unpriced ones get nodded at.

"One upward suggestion, held loosely: the onboarding doc has no who-to-ask section — a starter map would cost one afternoon."——语法用两步为建议定价：held-loosely 标签（我的看法，非裁决）+ 有成本的提案（一个下午，而非"该有人来修"）。有定价的建议被采纳；没定价的被点头。

6. Cultural Tips 文化 / 职场 Tips

  • Run the review against the agreed scoreboard. Evidence per line — the format you and Sarah set in week one is the whole meeting.
  • Evidence is counted, not remembered. Nine asks, median four hours, signed off Thursday — numbers with definitions, assembled the week before.
  • Critique the scoreboard itself. "Nothing about relationships" is the mature gap — the measurement was incomplete, and you are the one who ran it.
  • Use your new-eyes window. The routing-map suggestion is only visible to someone in week four; after month three, it becomes invisible furniture. Spend the window.
  • Draft the next line in their words. "Stop being copied" was Sarah\'s phrase — the day-60 target belongs to her vocabulary, measured by your evidence.

7. Practice 练习题

Exercise 1 — Assemble your evidence pack

Build your own 30-day evidence: for each scoreboard line, three numbers with definitions. If a line has no number, design one this week.

The design step is the professional habit: "asks unblocked within a day" becomes countable the moment you define it — nine asks, self-served or routed, median response. Unmeasured goals expire at day 30; measured ones compound to day 365.

Exercise 2 — The upward suggestion

Write one upward suggestion for your team, priced: what it costs, who owns it, what it saves. Held-loosely label attached.

Model: "Held loosely: the standup could timebox updates to 60 seconds — cost is one timer, savings are five minutes daily across nine people. Happy to be the timer-holder for a trial week." Priced, volunteered, reversible.

Exercise 3 — Draft the day-60 line

Write your day-60 scoreboard line using your manager's vocabulary, plus the two evidence numbers you will bring.

Model: "You stop being copied — CC list halved, one stakeholder briefing delivered." The phrase is hers; the numbers are yours. That combination is what makes a review feel like a shared instrument instead of an exam.
