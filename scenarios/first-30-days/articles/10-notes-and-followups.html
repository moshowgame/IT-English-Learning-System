Meeting Notes That Actually Follow Up

Article 10 · First 30 Days English · 能落地跟进的会议纪要

⏱ 7 min read 🟢 Beginner-friendly 🎯 Decisions, owners, dates — typed before you leave the call, sent within the hour

1. Scenario 场景背景

Week two. You are invited to your first cross-team design review — eight people, four teams, one hour. Elena\'s parting instruction: "Take the notes. Not the transcript — the decisions." Nobody has ever taught you how to take notes that anyone reads.

第二周。你受邀参加第一次跨团队设计评审——8 个人、4 个团队、1 小时。Elena 的临别嘱咐："做会议记录。不是逐字稿——是决定。"从没有人教过你怎样做有人会看的记录。

The professional note is three lists, not a story: decisions (what was agreed), owners (who does what), dates (by when) — typed before you leave the call and sent within the hour, while memory is fresh and corrections are cheap.

职业化的会议记录是三张清单，不是一篇故事：决定（达成了什么）、责任人（谁做什么）、日期（何时前）——离会前敲完、一小时内发出，趁记忆新鲜、纠错成本低。

2. Dialogue 英文对话

Elena (Tech Lead): One job today: the notes. Not the transcript — the decisions. If you capture what was agreed, who owns it, and by when, the meeting was worth it. If you capture opinions, it was just a podcast.今天只有一个任务：纪要。不是逐字稿——是决定。记下达成了什么、谁负责、何时前完成，这场会就值。如果只记观点，那它只是一期播客。
You (New Joiner): Structure locked: decisions, owners, dates — typed live. One question though: when two people half-agree, is that a decision?结构锁定：决定、责任人、日期——边听边敲。一个问题：当两个人各同意一半，算决定吗？
Elena (Tech Lead): Sharp catch. A half-agreement is a to-confirm item — it goes in a fourth list: "open: X to confirm Y with Z by Friday." Never record a half-agreement as a decision; that is how phantom requirements are born.问得尖锐。半同意是"待确认项"——放进第四张清单："待确认：X 周五前与 Z 确认 Y。"永远不要把半同意记成决定；幽灵需求就是这么诞生的。
(During the meeting — the review proceeds; you type.): Decision captured: "Retry policy: three attempts, exponential backoff, dead-letter after." Owner: Daniel K. Date: design doc updated by Thursday. — Wait, Elena just said "we should probably look at the monitoring side too"… is that a decision? No — half-agreement. Open list: "Elena to scope monitoring impact — owner TBD."决定已捕获："重试策略：三次尝试、指数退避、之后进死信队列。"责任人：Daniel K。日期：设计文档周四前更新。——等等，Elena 刚说"监控侧大概也该看看"……这是决定吗？不是——半同意。待确认清单："Elena 评估监控影响——责任人待定。"
You (New Joiner): (after the call) The hour rule. Sending now, while the disagreements are still warm enough to fix. Email: decisions first — five bullets; owners and dates in the same line as each decision; open items last, prefixed "OPEN —". One line at the bottom: "Corrections welcome — I am new to this domain, so I would rather fix a wrong note than archive one."（会后）一小时法则。现在就发，趁分歧还热着能改。邮件：决定在前——五条；责任人和日期与决定同行；待确认项放最后，加"OPEN —"前缀。结尾一行："欢迎纠错——我对这个领域还新，宁可修正一条错的记录，也不愿归档一条错的。"
Daniel K (Senior Dev): Got the notes — one correction: backoff is exponential base 4, not 2. Otherwise these are the cleanest notes this meeting has produced in a year. And yes — the OPEN on monitoring is right; I owed that scope a decision.收到纪要——一处修正：退避是 4 为底的指数，不是 2。除此之外，这是这场会一年以来产出的最干净纪要。还有——监控那条标 OPEN 是对的，那个范围确实欠一个决定。
Elena (Tech Lead): The corrections are the system working. A note nobody corrects is a note nobody read. Next month, you take notes in the SteerCo — same format, higher stakes.纠错正是系统在运转。没人纠正的纪要，是没人读的纪要。下个月的 SteerCo 也由你记——同样格式，更高赌注。

3. Key Phrases 关键句型

  • Mandate Not the transcript — the decisions. 不是逐字稿——是决定。 The whole note-taking philosophy in five words: you are recording agreements, not audio.
  • Half A half-agreement is a to-confirm item. 半同意是"待确认项"。 The distinction that protects the record: enthusiasm is not consensus. OPEN-prefix it.
  • Phantom That is how phantom requirements are born. 幽灵需求就是这么诞生的。 The warning image — requirements that exist only because someone half-nodded once.
  • Format Owners and dates in the same line as each decision. 责任人和日期与决定同行。 One line = one decision = one owner = one date. Separate lists make readers cross-reference; same-line makes them act.
  • Invite Corrections welcome — I would rather fix a wrong note than archive one. 欢迎纠错——宁可修正也不归档错误的记录。 The correction invitation, framed as humility and quality control. It doubles as your learning device.
  • Signal A note nobody corrects is a note nobody read. 没人纠正的纪要是没人读的纪要。 Elena\'s aphorism: silence is not agreement, it is absence. Corrections are engagement.

4. Vocabulary 词汇表

  • Minutes n. 会议纪要 The official record: decisions, owners, dates — not a transcript.
  • Decision n. 决定 What was actually agreed — present tense, testable, owned.
  • Owner n. 责任人 The named person accountable for each decision's execution.
  • Open item n. 待确认项 A half-agreement awaiting confirmation — always prefixed OPEN.
  • Dead-letter n. 死信队列 Where failed messages go after retries exhaust — decision captured.
  • Exponential backoff n. 指数退避 Retry spacing that grows each attempt — base 4, per Daniel K.
  • Phantom requirement n. 幽灵需求 A requirement born from a misread half-agreement.
  • Corrections n. 纠错 Reader replies fixing the record — the sign the notes were read.

5. Grammar Notes 语法点

① One line per decision: the quadruple

"Retry policy: three attempts, exponential backoff, dead-letter after — Daniel K, by Thursday." — [decision]: [content] — [owner], by [date]. Four slots, one line, zero ambiguity. The discipline is grammatical: if a decision cannot fit the four slots, it is not yet a decision — it is a discussion.

"Retry policy: three attempts, exponential backoff, dead-letter after — Daniel K, by Thursday."——[决定]：[内容]——[责任人]，by [日期]。四个槽位、一行、零歧义。纪律是语法性的：如果某个决定装不进四个槽位，它还不是决定——只是一场讨论。

② Prefixed openness

"OPEN — Elena to scope monitoring impact, owner TBD." — the OPEN prefix in caps does what italics cannot in email: it makes unfinishedness searchable. Six months later, someone greps the wiki for OPEN items and finds the monitoring scope that never got decided. Prefix grammar is archive grammar.

"OPEN — Elena to scope monitoring impact, owner TBD."——大写的 OPEN 前缀完成了邮件里斜体做不到的事：让"未完成"可被检索。六个月后有人检索 wiki 的 OPEN 项，找到那个始终没有决定的监控范围。前缀语法就是归档语法。

6. Cultural Tips 文化 / 职场 Tips

  • Type decisions live, not from memory. The three-list format (decisions / owners / dates) fits in the corner of any screen.
  • Half-agreements go to OPEN. The discipline that separates useful minutes from fiction — record enthusiasm as an open item, never as a decision.
  • Send within the hour. Corrections are cheap while memories are warm; next-day notes get archived uncorrected.
  • Invite corrections explicitly. "I would rather fix a wrong note than archive one" — new joiners get a pass, and every correction is free tuition.
  • The owner-date rule is non-negotiable. A decision without an owner is a wish; a decision without a date is a wish with ambition.

7. Practice 练习题

Exercise 1 — Take real notes

At your next meeting, keep three lists live: DECISIONS / OPEN / (owners and dates inline). Send within the hour with the corrections-invite line.

Count what happens: if you get corrections, the notes worked. If you get silence, follow up with the two named owners directly — silence on decisions usually means nobody saw themselves in the owner column.

Exercise 2 — Half-agreement drill

Classify as decision or OPEN: (a) "Everyone nodded at retry with exponential backoff." (b) "We agreed on three attempts; backoff curve was contested, Daniel to model both." (c) "Elena thinks monitoring needs scope."

(a) OPEN — nodding is not agreeing; ask Daniel to confirm the curve in writing. (b) Decision — the contested part got an owner and a method. (c) OPEN — a thought is not a scope, and "thinks" is the tell.

Exercise 3 — The SteerCo upgrade

Elena says the same format goes to SteerCo. What three things change when the audience is executives?

Lead with the asks (they read the first five lines); decisions get named sponsors, not just owners; and the OPEN list becomes politically sensitive — confirm items privately before publishing, because in SteerCo notes, an unconfirmed OPEN reads like someone dodging.
