Your First Written Status Update

Article 13 · First 30 Days English · 第一次书面进度汇报

⏱ 7 min read 🟡 Intermediate 🎯 The five-line email: shipped, next, blocked, risk, ask — with your recommendation on top

1. Scenario 场景背景

End of week two, and Sarah\'s standing instruction from the coffee chat: "async with context, recommendation on top." Time for your first written status update to the team — the one document a new joiner writes that everyone actually reads. Make it the two-paragraph version, not the novel.

第二周末，Sarah 在咖啡时定下的规矩："异步 + 带上下文，推荐项放最上面。"是时候写你的第一份书面进度汇报了——新员工写的文档里唯一所有人都会真读的那份。写两段版，不写长篇。

The format: recommendation or headline first, then shipped / next / blocked as bullets, one risk honestly named, and one sized ask — five lines that take ninety seconds to read and save everyone a standup worth of curiosity.

格式：推荐或结论先行，然后 shipped / next / blocked 三条项目符号、一条诚实命名的风险、一个有尺寸的请求——五行、90 秒读完、替所有人省掉一场站会的好奇心。

2. Dialogue 英文对话

You (New Joiner): Drafting principles first. One: headline first — Sarah reads recommendations, not chronology. Two: bullets do the numbering — shipped, next, blocked. Three: the risk gets named honestly — my credibility is the only thing I own this early.先立起草原则。一：结论先行——Sarah 读的是推荐，不是时间线。二：项目符号负责编号——shipped、next、blocked。三：风险诚实命名——这个阶段我唯一拥有的资产就是可信度。
You (New Joiner): The email. Subject: "Churn dashboard — on track for Thursday; one risk needs a yes/no." The subject is the update. Headline: "Recommendation: ship the dashboard Thursday with the timestamp-based join; the 0.3% gap is understood and documented."邮件。主题："Churn dashboard——周四按期；一个风险需要是/否决定。主题即汇报。结论："建议：周四带时间戳连接的版本上线；0.3% 的偏差已查明并记录。"
You (New Joiner): Shipped: spike merged; doc fix live; reconciliation view running on event timestamp. Next: sign-off walkthrough with Elena Wednesday; production-data access day four. Blocked: nothing — the word "blocked" should be rare in my updates.Shipped：spike 已合并；文档修复已上线；对账视图跑在事件时间戳上。Next：周三与 Elena 走查签批；生产数据权限第 4 天。Blocked：无——"blocked" 一词在我的汇报里应该稀有。
You (New Joiner): Risk, named honestly: the reactivation flag in source data is unreliable (race condition REF-2214) — the dashboard is correct today; if the source job is patched, the timestamp join must be re-verified. Ask: Daniel B — ten minutes this week to confirm the re-verification step belongs in the pipeline ticket?风险，诚实命名：源数据的再激活标志不可靠（竞态 REF-2214）——仪表盘今天是对的；如果源作业将来被修复，时间戳连接需要复验。请求：Daniel B——本周 10 分钟，确认复验步骤是否应写进管道工单？
Elena (Tech Lead): Reading it as a manager would: ninety seconds, and I know the state of your week better than from any standup. The headline works — "recommendation" is the word that makes it skimmable; I read your conclusion, then decide if I need your reasons.以经理视角读了一遍：90 秒，比任何站会都清楚你这一周的状态。结论行成立——"recommendation" 一词让它变得可速读；我先读你的结论，再决定要不要看理由。
Sarah (Manager): And the risk section is the part that gets you trusted — you named the failure mode that has not happened yet, with the re-verification plan attached. That is senior behaviour in week two.风险段是赢得信任的部分——你命名了一个尚未发生的故障模式，并附上了复验计划。这是第二周就很资深的标志。
Daniel B (Senior Dev): The ask reached me as a ten-minute decision, not a mystery — yes, the re-verification step belongs in the pipeline ticket; I will add it. Note for your next update: put the ask with a deadline — "by Wednesday" — and I would have answered it same-day.这个请求到我这里是一个 10 分钟的决定，不是谜题——是的，复验步骤该进管道工单，我来加。下次更新提示：给请求加期限——"周三前"——我当天就会答。
You (New Joiner): Logged — every ask gets a deadline from now on. One habit I am taking from this: the subject line is written last, and it is written as the update itself. If the subject cannot carry the state, the email is too complicated.记下——从现在起每个请求都带期限。一个习惯：主题行最后写，而且写成汇报本身。如果主题行装不下状态，说明邮件太复杂了。

3. Key Phrases 关键句型

  • Subject Churn dashboard — on track for Thursday; one risk needs a yes/no. 流失仪表盘——周四按期；一个风险需要是/否决定。 Subject-as-update: state, date, and the decision needed. Inbox readers are done after the subject line.
  • Headline Recommendation: ship Thursday with the timestamp join; gap understood and documented. 建议：周四带时间戳连接上线；偏差已查明并记录。 "Recommendation" licenses skimming — the reader takes your conclusion or asks for your reasons, never both by default.
  • Shipped Spike merged; doc fix live; view running on timestamp. spike 已合并；文档修复已上线；视图跑在时间戳上。 Past-participle inventory — merged, live, running: finished things, each with its evidence.
  • Risk The dashboard is correct today; if the source is patched, the join must be re-verified. 仪表盘今天是对的；若源头被修复，连接需复验。 The honest risk: correct now + the trigger that would break it + the plan. Three clauses, zero fear-mongering.
  • Ask Ten minutes this week to confirm X — by Wednesday? 本周 10 分钟确认 X——周三前？ Sized ask with deadline. Daniel B told you: the deadline is what converts an ask into a same-day answer.

4. Vocabulary 词汇表

  • Status update n. 进度汇报 The written pulse: shipped, next, blocked, risk, ask.
  • Recommendation n. 建议 Your conclusion, stated first — the word that makes updates skimmable.
  • Sign-off n. 签批 Formal approval to proceed; walkthroughs produce them.
  • Risk n. 风险 A named future failure mode with a plan attached — never a vague worry.
  • Re-verification n. 复验 Re-checking an assumption after its input changes — your dashboard's dependency.
  • Race condition n. 竞态条件 REF-2214's disease; the reason your join cannot trust the flag.
  • Deadline n. 期限 What turns an ask into a scheduled answer.
  • Skimmable adj. 可速读的 Readable in ninety seconds by a manager between meetings.

5. Grammar Notes 语法点

① Past participles as shipped inventory

"Spike merged; doc fix live; view running." — past participles and adjectives as a completed-state inventory: merged, live, running. No "I have done X" needed — the participle IS the done. Status English is participle English; sentences with "I" in the shipped section read as activity, not outcome.

"Spike merged; doc fix live; view running."——过去分词与形容词构成"已完成状态"清单：merged、live、running。不需要"I have done X"——分词本身就是完成。进度英语就是分词英语；shipped 段里出现"I"读起来是活动，不是成果。

② The today/then risk frame

"The dashboard is correct today; if the source is patched, the join must be re-verified." — present simple for the current state, first conditional for the trigger, modal for the consequence. Three clauses that price a risk honestly: true now, breaks when X, plan is Y. Vague risks say "there might be issues"; priced risks say this.

"The dashboard is correct today; if the source is patched, the join must be re-verified."——现在时讲当前状态，第一类条件句讲触发器，情态动词讲后果。三个从句诚实定价一个风险：现在为真、X 时会坏、计划是 Y。模糊的风险说"可能有Issues"；定价的风险这么说。

6. Cultural Tips 文化 / 职场 Tips

  • Write the subject last, as the update itself. If the subject carries state, date and decision-needed, many readers need nothing else.
  • "Recommendation:" is the magic word. It converts your email from a story into a decision document.
  • Keep "blocked" rare. An update whose blocked line is always full reads as a diary of helplessness. Flag dependencies as dependencies.
  • Price the risk in three clauses. Correct today; breaks when X; plan is Y. That is the whole grammar of credible risk reporting.
  • Every ask gets a deadline. Daniel B answered same-day because the ask had a date. The deadline is a gift to the busy.

7. Practice 练习题

Exercise 1 — Write yours

Draft your own five-line update this week: subject-as-update, recommendation headline, shipped/next/blocked, one priced risk, one sized ask with deadline.

Then test: read only the subject line and the recommendation — does a manager know your state and your ask? If yes, the remaining lines are evidence; if no, the headline is hiding the point.

Exercise 2 — Risk pricing

Price these vague risks in the three-clause frame: (a) "the data might have issues", (b) "the migration could be risky", (c) "performance may degrade".

(a) "Counts are correct today; if the source backfills, the totals shift — re-run due Friday." (b) "Migration is reversible today; after cutover the rollback window is 24h — decision point Thursday." (c) "P95 is 400ms now; beyond 5k concurrent users it degrades — load test booked Wednesday." Today/trigger/plan, every time.

Exercise 3 — Subject line gym

Write subject lines for three states: on track with a decision needed; at risk due to external dependency; shipped and closed.

Models: "Churn dashboard — on track Thursday; need yes/no on join approach." "Access ticket day 5 — pairing workaround active, but Thursday ship at risk if no reply." "Reconciliation view — shipped and verified; doc updated." State + date + decision, under twelve words.
