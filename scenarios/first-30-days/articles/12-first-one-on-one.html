Your First Real 1:1

Article 12 · First 30 Days English · 第一次真正的 1:1

⏱ 7 min read 🟡 Intermediate 🎯 Own the agenda: wins, blockers, asks, and one question about your career path

1. Scenario 场景背景

Week two, and the first recurring 1:1 with Sarah. The week-one coffee was calibration; this is the machine that runs every week for years. The meeting is yours — Sarah\'s calendar says so, and so does every management book ever written. The question is what to do with ownership.

第二周，与 Sarah 的第一次例行 1:1。第一周的咖啡是校准；这是未来几年每周运转的机器。这个会属于你——Sarah 的日历这么写，所有管理学著作也这么写。问题是：如何行使这份所有权。

The owned 1:1 has four slots, in your order: wins (what moved), blockers (what needs her), asks (specific and sized), and one career question — the slot that turns a status meeting into a relationship.

被你拥有的 1:1 有四个槽位，按你的顺序：战果（什么推进了）、阻塞（什么需要她）、请求（具体且有尺寸）、一个职业问题——正是这个槽位把进度会变成关系。

2. Dialogue 英文对话

Sarah (Manager): 1:1 — it is your meeting, so your agenda. What have we got?1:1——这是你的会，所以议程归你。今天有什么？
You (New Joiner): Four slots, ten minutes. Wins first — two: the churn dashboard spike shipped Wednesday, and my doc fix is merged — Elena says future joiners save twenty minutes.四个槽位，十分钟。先说战果——两条：流失仪表盘 spike 周三交付，我的文档修复已合并——Elena 说未来的新人能省 20 分钟。
Sarah (Manager): Good — small, real, and merged. That is exactly what week two should contain. Next slot?好——小、真实、已合并。第二周就该是这个样子。下一个槽位？
You (New Joiner): Blockers — one, with a workaround running: the production-data ticket is day four of compliance review. I am pairing on colleagues\' screens meanwhile, but the spike I really want next week needs my own access.阻塞——一条，已有替代方案在跑：生产数据工单在合规审查第 4 天。我目前在同事屏幕上结对，但下周我真正想做的 spike 需要自己的权限。
Sarah (Manager): I know the form it is stuck on — I will mention it to the compliance lead today. That is the kind of blocker that needs me, not you. Asks?我知道它卡在哪张表——今天我会跟合规负责人提一句。这类阻塞需要的是我，不是你。请求呢？
You (New Joiner): Two asks, both sized. One: could I shadow the SteerCo prep next Tuesday? Twenty minutes of watching how you present would teach me more than a month of wiki. Two: is there a budget code for a SQL course — the window-functions one, forty dollars, would close my 0.3% gap permanently.两个请求，都有尺寸。一：下周二我能旁听 SteerCo 的准备会吗？看你怎么汇报的 20 分钟，比一个月的 wiki 更有用。二：有 SQL 课程的预算代码吗——窗口函数那门，40 美元，能永久补上我那 0.3% 的缺口。
Sarah (Manager): Both approved — the course one is the cheapest training request I have ever received, and the SteerCo invite is yours. Last slot?都批——课程那条是我收到过的最便宜的培训申请，SteerCo 的邀请也归你了。最后一个槽位？
You (New Joiner): The career one. You said at the coffee that day 90 sounds like "someone outside the team asks for me by name." What is the day-365 version of that sentence? I am not asking for a title — I am asking what to build towards so my choices compound instead of scatter.职业那条。你在咖啡时说第 90 天是"团队外有人点名找我。"那句话的第 365 天版本是什么？我不是在问职级——我是在问该朝着什么构建自己，让选择复利而不是发散。
Sarah (Manager): That is the best 1:1 question I have been asked this year. Day 365: you are the person the data platform team calls when they need something analysed and explained — internally famous for one thing, done visibly. Pick your thing this quarter; I will help you aim.这是今年我被问到的最好的 1:1 问题。第 365 天：数据平台团队需要分析并讲清某件事时，会来找的那个人——因一件事而在内部有名气，且做得可见。这个季度选出你的那件事；我帮你瞄准。
You (New Joiner): Then the quarterly goal writes itself: the thing I pick is the reconciliation storytelling — I will own one analysis area end-to-end and present it once outside the team. Same slots next week?那季度目标自己就写出来了：我选的是对账叙事——端到端拥有一个分析领域，并在团队外做一次展示。下周同样槽位？

3. Key Phrases 关键句型

  • Frame Four slots, ten minutes. 四个槽位，十分钟。 Naming the structure at the start claims the ownership the calendar gave you.
  • Wins Small, real, and merged. 小、真实、已合并。 Week-two wins should be exactly this shape — finished artifacts, not activity reports.
  • Blocker With a workaround running. 已有替代方案在跑。 Bringing the workaround shows you tried to make the blocker boring before making it visible.
  • Ask Could I shadow the SteerCo prep? Twenty minutes. 能旁听 SteerCo 准备会吗？20 分钟。 The sized shadowing ask — specific event, specific duration, specific learning. Impossible to refuse on cost.
  • Career What is the day-365 version of that sentence? 那句话的第 365 天版本是什么？ Extends the manager\'s own scoreboard into the future — the question that turns a status meeting into a mentorship.
  • Aim I am asking what to build towards so my choices compound. 问该朝什么构建自己，让选择复利。 Framing ambition as compounding, not climbing — managers invest in compounding people.

4. Vocabulary 词汇表

  • 1:1 n. 一对一 The recurring manager meeting — yours to own, slot by slot.
  • Win n. 战果 A finished, visible outcome — the currency of early employment.
  • Shadow v. 旁听/影子学习 Watching someone work to absorb their craft; best with a duration attached.
  • Ask n. 请求 A sized, specific request — the ask slot is where careers get resources.
  • SteerCo abbr. 指导委员会 The senior governance meeting; its prep sessions teach executives' language.
  • Compound v. 复利式增长 Gains building on gains — the alternative to scattered effort.
  • Budget code n. 预算科目 The accounting label that makes a request payable — forty dollars needs one.
  • Internally famous phr. 内部有名 Known beyond your team for one specific strength — the day-365 goal.

5. Grammar Notes 语法点

① Emphatic cleft for the career slot

"What is the day-365 version of that sentence?" — the what is the X of that Y cleft takes something the manager already said and asks the future tense of it. It works because it builds on THEIR words, not your ambitions — you are extending their scoreboard, not presenting yours.

"What is the day-365 version of that sentence?"——what is the X of that Y 分裂句拿经理已经说过的话问它的将来时。它有效是因为它建立在对方的话语上，而非你的野心——你在延展他们的记分板，而不是展示你的。

② Purpose clauses in the ask

"So my choices compound instead of scatter." — so that [gain] instead of [loss] frames every request with its payoff economics. The same clause upgrades any ask: "so the fix ships instead of slipping." Managers approve asks whose purpose clause they could repeat to their own boss.

"So my choices compound instead of scatter."——so that [收益] instead of [损失]为每个请求配上收益经济学。同样的从句升级一切请求："so the fix ships instead of slipping."经理批的是那些他们能向上级复述其目的从句的请求。

6. Cultural Tips 文化 / 职场 Tips

  • Own the agenda in writing. Send four bullet points the morning of the 1:1 — the meeting becomes yours before it starts.
  • Wins before blockers, always. The order is trust-building: you are someone who ships, so your blocker deserves help.
  • Bring the workaround. "Blocked, pairing meanwhile" reads as resourceful; "blocked, waiting" reads as a queue item.
  • Size every ask. Twenty minutes, forty dollars — priced asks get approved in-meeting; vague asks get "let me think about it".
  • The career slot is weekly, not annual. One compounding question per 1:1 — by month three, Sarah will be steering your aim unprompted.

7. Practice 练习题

Exercise 1 — Build your four slots

Write next week's 1:1 agenda: two wins (with links), one blocker (with workaround), two asks (sized), one career question (built on something your manager said).

The career question test: does it quote their vocabulary back? "You said X — what is the Y version?" beats "where do you see me?" because it extends their framework instead of demanding a new one.

Exercise 2 — Price an ask

You want three days with the platform team to understand the ingestion pipeline. Write the ask with its purpose clause and its cost.

Model: "Could I spend three half-days with the platform team this sprint — so the analyses I own stop guessing at ingestion behaviour? Cost: my sprint capacity drops to 60% for one week; the churn work absorbs it." Priced, purposed, approvable.

Exercise 3 — The compounding question

Sarah says day 365 is "internally famous for one thing". Write your three-candidate list of "things" and the sentence you would use to discuss them at the next 1:1.

Model: "Three candidates for my one thing: reconciliation storytelling, the data-quality alphabet, or onboarding craft. Could I trial all three this quarter and we review which one compounds?" Managers cannot argue with a trial design.
