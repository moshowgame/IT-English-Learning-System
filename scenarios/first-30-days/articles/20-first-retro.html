Your First Retrospective

Article 20 · First 30 Days English · 第一次回顾会

⏱ 7 min read 🟢 Beginner-friendly 🎯 Contribute in retro one: observations with evidence, one improvement proposal, one thank-you

1. Scenario 场景背景

End of week four — your first sprint retrospective. The room is candid by design: what went well, what did not, what we change. New joiners usually survive it in silence. You have four weeks of new eyes and one month of notes. Spend them.

第四周末——你的第一次Sprint 回顾会。这个房间以坦率为设计：什么顺利、什么不顺利、我们改什么。新员工通常在沉默中熬过它。你有四周的新眼睛和一个月的笔记。花掉它们。

The first-retro contribution is three moves: one observation with evidence (something you saw, fresh), one improvement proposal (priced and volunteered), and one thank-you (specific, named) — the new joiner\'s complete opening hand.

第一次回顾会的贡献是三步：一条带证据的观察（你亲眼所见、足够新鲜）、一个改进提案（有定价、自愿承担）、一句感谢（具体、点名）——新人的完整起手牌。

2. Dialogue 英文对话

Priya (Delivery): Retro time. Wei — first one, and yes, you are expected to contribute. New eyes are the most expensive resource in this room; they expire in a month.回顾会时间。Wei——第一次，没错，你要发言。新眼睛是这个房间里最昂贵的资源；一个月就过期。
You (New Joiner): Then I will spend them. Observation with evidence: in my first week, I watched five conversations die because someone said "I did not want to bother you." — including mine, twice. The team says "ask anything" and the reflex says "do not bother" — the two are at war.那就花掉它们。带证据的观察：第一周里，我目睹了 五次对话死于"我不想打扰你。"——包括我自己的两次。团队嘴上说"随便问"，本能却说"别打扰"——两者在交战。
Elena (Tech Lead): That is a real finding. We say the words and never notice the counter-signal. Where do you think the "do not bother" comes from?这是个真实的发现。我们说了那句话，却从没注意到反向信号。你觉得"别打扰"从哪来的？
You (New Joiner): Honest guess, held loosely: visible busyness. Everyone here is genuinely flat out — the new joiner cannot tell "deep in a problem" from "do not interrupt." A small signal would fix it: the status cube — green when interruptible, red when not. Cost: a bot command, one afternoon. I will build it if the room wants it.诚实的猜测，保留态度地给出：可见的忙碌感。这里的每个人都真的满负荷——新人分不清"深陷问题"和"别打断"。一个小信号就能修：状态立方——绿色=可打断，红色=勿扰。成本：一个机器人命令、一个下午。房间要的话我就来建。
Daniel B (Senior Dev): Volunteered and priced. I second it — I have wanted that cube for two years and never proposed it because it felt too small to table. New-joiner privilege: your first month is the only time "too small" does not disqualify an idea.自愿承担且已定价。我附议——这个立方我想要两年了，从没提案因为它"太小不上桌"。新人的特权：只有第一个月，"太小"不能否决一个想法。
You (New Joiner): Noted — and I intend to spend that privilege before it expires. Finally, the thank-you, named: Marco — the VPN fix on day one saved me a ticket round-trip and made the whole setup feel survivable. And Daniel K — the naive-approach question habit is yours; I stole it and it works.记下——我打算在特权过期前花掉它。最后，致谢，点名：Marco——第一天的 VPN 修复帮我省了一次工单往返，让整个环境搭建显得可以存活。还有 Daniel K——"先试朴素方案"的提问习惯是你教的；我偷学了，而且有效。
Marco (Buddy): (laughing) That is the first time a thank-you in a retro has named a VPN fix. Noted and appreciated.（笑）这是回顾会历史上第一次有人点名感谢一个 VPN 修复。记下并且领情。
Priya (Delivery): Action items: status cube — Wei, this sprint. And the "do not bother" finding goes in the team handbook — Elena? Wei, that is how you do a first retro. See you all in two weeks — same honesty, more scars.行动项：状态立方——Wei，本 Sprint。"别打扰"的发现写进团队手册——Elena？Wei，第一次回顾会就该这么做。两周后见——同样的诚实，更多的伤疤。

3. Key Phrases 关键句型

  • Spend New eyes expire in a month — I will spend them. 新眼睛一个月就过期——我要花掉它们。 The first-retro mindset: your newcomer perspective is a wasting asset. Retro one is where it peaks.
  • Evidence Five conversations died on "I did not want to bother you" — including mine, twice. 五次对话死于"我不想打扰你"——包括我自己的两次。 Observation with evidence: counted, specific, and self-inclusive. The "including mine" is what makes it land as honesty, not accusation.
  • Price Cost: a bot command, one afternoon. I will build it if the room wants it. 成本：一个机器人命令、一个下午。房间要的话我就来建。 The priced proposal: cost named, builder volunteered. Compare "someone should fix this" — which builds nothing.
  • Privilege Your first month is the only time "too small" does not disqualify an idea. 只有第一个月，"太小"不能否决一个想法。 Daniel naming your window — two years of senior restraint versus your one month of licence. Spend it.
  • Thank-you The VPN fix saved me a ticket round-trip — and the naive-approach habit is yours. VPN 修复帮我省了一次工单往返——"朴素方案"习惯也是你的。 The specific, named thank-you: artifact-level gratitude. "Thanks everyone" thanks no one.
  • Stolen I stole it and it works. 我偷学了，而且有效。 Attributing a stolen habit is double credit: the teacher gets named, and you get the tool. Steal openly.

4. Vocabulary 词汇表

  • Retro n. 回顾会 The sprint retrospective: what went well, what did not, what changes.
  • New eyes n. 新眼睛 The newcomer's perception — a wasting asset, peaked in month one.
  • Status cube n. 状态立方 A presence signal (green/red) — the fix for visible-busyness misreads.
  • Priced proposal n. 有定价的提案 An improvement with named cost and volunteered owner.
  • Table an idea phr. 提案 To raise something for discussion; "too small to table" is senior self-censorship.
  • First-retro hand n. 起手牌 Observation + proposal + thank-you — the new joiner's complete contribution.
  • Handbook n. 团队手册 Where findings like "do not bother" get institutionalised.

5. Grammar Notes 语法点

① Self-inclusive evidence

"Five conversations died on \'I did not want to bother you\' — including mine, twice." — the including mine clause converts an accusation into a shared finding. Evidence that includes the speaker survives the room\'s defences; evidence that points outward recruits them. One clause, whole different retro.

"Five conversations died on \'I did not want to bother you\' — including mine, twice."——including mine 从句把指控变成共同发现。包含发言者本人的证据能穿过房间的防御；指向外部的证据会招来防御。一个从句，两种回顾会。

② The volunteered conditional

"I will build it if the room wants it." — first conditional + volunteered labour: I will X if the room wants Y. The grammar keeps the proposal humble (the room decides) while the volunteering is unconditional (you are already committed to the build if wanted). Priced + volunteered is the strongest proposal shape a retro can receive.

"I will build it if the room wants it."——第一类条件句 + 自愿劳动：I will X if the room wants Y. 语法保持谦逊（房间决定），自愿是无条件的（如果需要，你已承诺动手）。有定价 + 自愿承担，是回顾会能收到的最强提案形态。

6. Cultural Tips 文化 / 职场 Tips

  • Spend the new eyes. Month-one observations are the most expensive resource in the room — use retro one before they expire.
  • Evidence, self-inclusive. Count the conversations that died, and include your own. "Including mine, twice" is what makes truth land gently.
  • Price and volunteer. "One afternoon, I will build it" beats "someone should" by an entire career.
  • The specific, named thank-you. A VPN fix, a stolen habit — gratitude at artifact level is what people remember at reference time.
  • Steal habits openly. "The naive-approach question is yours; I stole it and it works" — attribution is the compliment teachers actually want.

7. Practice 练习题

Exercise 1 — Collect your observations

From your first weeks, list three "died on the vine" moments — things that failed politely. Pick the one with a self-inclusive angle.

The self-inclusive angle matters: "I twice chose not to bother" makes the finding yours before it is the team\'s. Retro rooms reward the courage of counting yourself in the data.

Exercise 2 — Price your fix

Take one observation and write the priced proposal: the fix, the cost, the volunteer clause. Under 40 words.

Model: "A status cube — green when interruptible, red when not. Cost: a bot command, one afternoon. I will build it if the room wants it." Forty words, one decision for the room to make.

Exercise 3 — The thank-you draft

Write two artifact-level thank-yous for people who helped in month one — each names the specific help and what it made possible.

Test: could the thanked person screenshot it and keep it? "Thanks for your help" cannot be framed; "the VPN fix on day one saved my first ticket round-trip" can. Write for the screenshot.
