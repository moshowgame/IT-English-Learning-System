Your First Small Contribution

Article 16 · First 30 Days English · 第一次小贡献

⏱ 7 min read 🟡 Intermediate 🎯 Ship something visible in week three: the PR, the review reply, the credit culture

1. Scenario 场景背景

Week three, and you have found one: the onboarding doc\'s GLM section — the fix Elena approved in standup. One definition line, one corrected diagram label. Small enough to finish, visible enough to matter. Today you learn how a tiny contribution is actually shipped in a bank — and how credit works.

第三周，你找到了目标：入职文档的 GLM 一节——Elena 在站会批准的修复。一行定义、一个图示标签更正。小到能完成，可见到有意义。今天你将学到一家银行里小贡献真正的上线方式——以及署名文化如何运作。

2. Dialogue 英文对话

Elena (Tech Lead): Doc PR time. Three things make small contributions land: the PR description sells it, the reviewers get their due, and the change is findable afterwards. Show me the description.文档 PR 时间。三件事让小贡献落地：PR 描述会卖、评审者得到应得的致谢、事后能被找到。给我看描述。
You (New Joiner): Draft: "Adds one-line definition of ledger mapping to the GLM section — saves future joiners ~20 minutes (my day-two cost, itemised in the linked note)." Then a second line: "Found while onboarding; flagged in standup Tuesday; approved there by Elena." — the paper trail proves it was wanted, not vanity.草稿："在 GLM 一节加入总账映射的一行定义——为未来新人省约 20 分钟（我第二天的成本，明细见附注）。"第二行："入职期间发现；周二站会提出；Elena 当场批准。"——这份痕迹证明它是被需要的，不是自我表现。
Daniel K (Senior Dev): Reviewing now. One suggestion, one nit: the definition line is good — add the cross-link to the GLM runbook and I approve. The nit: "~20 minutes" — nice that you quantified; put the estimate\'s basis in the linked note, or someone will quote it as gospel.评审中。一条建议、一个 nit：定义行很好——加上 GLM runbook 的交叉链接我就批。nit："约 20 分钟"——量化这点很好；把估算依据放进附注，否则有人会把它当圣经引用。
You (New Joiner): Both done. And the review reply — this is the part nobody taught me: "Good catch on the estimate basis — linked note now shows the derivation (two joiners × observed time). Cross-link added." Suggestions get receipts too.都改了。还有评审回复——没人教过我的部分："估算依据的好点——附注现在展示了推导（两名新人 × 观测时间）。交叉链接已加。"建议也要有回执。
Elena (Tech Lead): Merged. Now the credit part — watch this. (types) The changelog entry: "Doc: GLM definition + diagram label — Wei, week 3." Your name in the artifact, not in my memory of a standup. In six months, when someone reads that doc, the credit is in the page.合并了。然后是署名部分——看好了。（敲字）变更日志："文档：GLM 定义 + 图示标签——Wei，第 3 周。"你的名字在产物里，而不是在我对某场站会的记忆里。六个月后有人读那份文档时，署名就在页面上。
You (New Joiner): That is the part I would have missed — I assumed the standup mention was the credit. Standups evaporate; changelogs persist.这正是我会漏掉的——我以为站会提一嘴就是署名。站会会蒸发；变更日志会留存。
Daniel K (Senior Dev): And the same rule applies to other people\'s help — when my review suggestion lands in your PR, the description credits the suggestion. "Cross-link per DK\'s review." Small habit; the seniors who help you notice it forever.而且同样的规则适用于别人的帮助——当我的评审建议进了你的 PR，描述里要署建议的来源。"交叉链接 per DK 的评审。"小习惯；帮过你的资深同事会永远记住。
You (New Joiner): Logged: ship small, cite the trail, credit the hands. Next target already queued — the glossary Daniel K inherited from me at lunch; twenty entries need a home in the wiki.记下：小步交付、引用痕迹、为每一双手署名。下一个目标已排队——午餐时 Daniel K 接手的术语表；二十个条目需要在 wiki 里安家。

3. Key Phrases 关键句型

  • Sell Saves future joiners ~20 minutes — my day-two cost, itemised. 为未来新人省约 20 分钟——我第二天的成本，已明细。 The PR description that sells: quantified benefit + the evidence link. Small changes need sales too.
  • Trail Found while onboarding; flagged Tuesday; approved in standup. 入职期间发现；周二提出；站会批准。 The paper trail proves the change was wanted — vanity asks for credit, trail-proven asks for nothing.
  • Receipt Good catch — the linked note now shows the derivation. 好点——附注现在展示了推导。 Review replies with receipts close the loop: the suggestion landed, here is where"
  • Credit Your name in the artifact, not in my memory. 你的名字在产物里，而不是我的记忆里。 Elena\'s credit culture: standups evaporate, changelogs persist. Get your name into the page.
  • Hands Cross-link per DK\'s review. 交叉链接 per DK 的评审。 Crediting the hands that helped — the small habit seniors notice forever.

4. Vocabulary 词汇表

  • PR (pull request) n. 合并请求 The unit of contribution; description and review trail included.
  • Nit n. 小瑕疵 A minor review comment — fix it, do not debate it.
  • Runbook n. 操作手册 The operational doc for a system; cross-links make docs a web.
  • Derivation n. 推导 How an estimate was produced — the antidote to numbers-as-gospel.
  • Changelog n. 变更日志 The persistent record of what changed and who did it.
  • Paper trail n. 纸面痕迹 The visible history proving a decision — wanted, approved, shipped.
  • Credit culture n. 署名文化 How the team names contributions — learn it in week three, use it for years.
  • Gospel n. 圣经/绝对真理 What unexplained numbers become when quoted without their basis.

5. Grammar Notes 语法点

① Participles close review loops

"Cross-link added.", "Derivation now shows the basis." — past participle + location: the receipt grammar. Review replies are logistics, not essays — name the fix, name where it lives, stop. Three words beat three paragraphs because the reviewer only needs one thing: confirmation it landed.

"Cross-link added."、"Derivation now shows the basis."——过去分词 + 位置：回执语法。评审回复是物流不是散文——说出修复、说出位置、停止。三个词胜过三段话，因为评审者只需要一件事：确认已落地。

② The em-dash credit line

"Doc: GLM definition + diagram label — Wei, week 3." — the changelog grammar: [artifact]: [change] — [who], [when]. Ten words that outlive every standup. When you make your first contributions, insist on this line — it is the difference between having done something and having it attributed.

"Doc: GLM definition + diagram label — Wei, week 3."——变更日志语法：[产物]：[变更] — [谁]，[何时]。十个词的寿命超过所有站会。做出第一个贡献时，坚持要这行——这是"做过"与"被署名"的区别。

6. Cultural Tips 文化 / 职场 Tips

  • Small and visible beats big and invisible. The one-line definition saved twenty minutes per joiner and put your name in the artifact.
  • The PR description sells the change. Quantified benefit + linked evidence + approval trail — even a one-line doc edit deserves three lines of sales.
  • Quantify with derivations. "~20 minutes" invited a nit until the basis was linked; numbers without basis become gospel.
  • Standups evaporate; changelogs persist. Ask for the credit line in the artifact — it is not vanity, it is the team\'s own system working.
  • Credit the hands that helped you. "Per DK\'s review" costs four words and buys a year of generous reviews.

7. Practice 练习题

Exercise 1 — Ship one this week

Find your own GLM-section: a doc gap, a broken link, a stale diagram. Ship the fix with the three-part PR description and request the changelog credit line.

The findability test: could a stranger find your fix six months later via search? If the changelog line does not exist, the answer is no — ask Elena for it explicitly; the request itself demonstrates you understand credit culture.

Exercise 2 — Review reply gym

Convert these into receipt replies: (a) "add a cross-link", (b) "this number needs a basis", (c) "the label is wrong on the diagram".

(a) "Cross-link added — section 4.2." (b) "Basis linked in the note: two joiners × observed time." (c) "Label corrected in the latest commit — good catch." Fix + location, every time.

Exercise 3 — The credit audit

Think of help you received this week: a review comment, an answered question, a shared note. Write the one-line credit you could give each in a visible artifact.

Credit needs an artifact to live in — a PR description, a doc footnote, a changelog. "Thanks in the standup" evaporates by Thursday; "per Marco\'s VPN fix" in the onboarding doc persists forever.
