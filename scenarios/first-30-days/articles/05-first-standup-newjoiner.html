Your First Standup

Article 05 · First 30 Days English · 第一次站会

⏱ 7 min read 🟢 Beginner-friendly 🎯 The three-line new joiner update: yesterday, today, and an honest blocker

1. Scenario 场景背景

Day three, 9:55 AM. The team shuffles into the standup call — Priya (delivery), Elena (tech lead), the two Daniels, and you, the newest face. Your heart rate is higher than it was for the job interview. What do you possibly report? You have shipped nothing; you barely have a laptop that trusts you.

第三天早上 9:55。团队陆续进入站会——Priya（交付）、Elena（技术负责人）、两位 Daniel，以及最新的面孔——你。你的心率比当年面试时还高。你能汇报什么呢？你什么都没交付；连你的笔记本都不太信任你。

Here is the secret: nobody expects deliverables in week one — they expect orientation. The new joiner standup update has the same three lines as everyone else\'s, with different content: yesterday (what I learned), today (what I unblock), blockers (what I need). Done in 45 seconds.

秘密是：没人期待第一周有交付——大家期待的是定向。新员工的站会汇报和所有人一样三行，只是内容不同：昨天（学了什么）、今天（解开什么阻塞）、阻塞（需要什么）。45 秒内完成。

2. Dialogue 英文对话

Priya (Delivery): Morning all. Round the circle — Marco, then Wei, you are last today.大家早上好。按顺序来——Marco 先，然后 Wei，你今天最后。
Marco (Buddy): Yesterday: closed the reporting bug, PR under review. Today: pairing with Daniel K on the fix deployment. Blockers: none.昨天：修完报表 bug，PR 评审中。今天：和 Daniel K 结对部署修复。阻塞：无。
You (New Joiner): Morning. Yesterday: onboarding day two — read Elena\'s systems doc, got data-platform access live, and sat with Daniel B through one deployment. Three systems now make sense to me that did not yesterday.早上好。昨天：入职第二天——读了 Elena 的系统文档、开通了数据平台权限、跟 Daniel B 看了一次部署。有三个系统昨天还不懂、今天懂了。
You (New Joiner): Today: my first analysis task — the churn-dashboard spike Elena gave me. Small by design; my goal is to finish something real this week, even if it is small.今天：我的第一个分析任务——Elena 给的流失仪表盘 spike。刻意挑的小；我的目标是本周完成一件真事，哪怕很小。
You (New Joiner): Blocker, a soft one: the dashboard runs on a dataset I can see but not yet query — the access ticket is in flight, day two of SLA. If it lands by tomorrow I am fine; if not, I will pair on someone\'s screen rather than wait. Not stuck — flagging the dependency.阻塞，软性的：仪表盘跑在一个我可见但还无法查询的数据集上——权限工单在途，SLA 第二天。明天前到位就没事；否则我就结对上别人的屏幕，而不是干等。没有被卡住——只是标记依赖。
Priya (Delivery): Good update — and "not stuck, flagging the dependency" is exactly the right register. I will nudge the ticket owner after standup.更新很好——"没被卡住，只是标记依赖"正是恰当的语域。站会后我去催一下工单负责人。
Elena (Tech Lead): One question, Wei — in the systems doc, which section was hardest to follow? That tells me what to rewrite for the next joiner.一个问题，Wei——系统文档里哪一段最难懂？这告诉我下一段该重写哪里。
You (New Joiner): Honestly? The GLM section assumes you know what a ledger mapping is. I brute-forced it with Marco and a whiteboard. A one-line definition at the top would save every future joiner twenty minutes.说实话？GLM 那一节默认你知道什么是总账映射。我和 Marco 用白板硬啃下来的。开头加一行定义，能为未来每个新人省 20 分钟。
Elena (Tech Lead): Done — you just made your first contribution and you are three days in. Okay people, next…改——你刚刚完成了第一次贡献，而这才第三天。好，下一位……

3. Key Phrases 关键句型

  • Learn Yesterday: onboarding day two — read X, got Y live, sat with Z. 昨天：入职第二天——读了 X、开通 Y、跟 Z 看了流程。 Week-one "yesterday" is about orientation, not output — say what now makes sense.
  • Delta Three systems now make sense to me that did not yesterday. 有三个系统昨天不懂、今天懂了。 The delta sentence — converts a study day into visible progress. Learn to say what changed in your head.
  • Goal My goal is to finish something real this week, even if small. 目标是本周完成一件真事，哪怕很小。 Sets expectations publicly and small — the anti-overpromise move for week one.
  • Dependency The access ticket is in flight, day two of SLA. 权限工单在途，SLA 第二天。 In-flight + day-count = you are tracking it, so the team does not have to.
  • Register Not stuck — flagging the dependency. 没有被卡住——只是标记依赖。 The single most useful standup sentence for a new joiner: distinguishes awareness from alarm.
  • Contribute A one-line definition would save every future joiner twenty minutes. 加一行定义能为每个未来新人省 20 分钟。 Answering "what was hardest" with a fix, not a complaint — contribution on day three.

4. Vocabulary 词汇表

  • Standup n. 站会 The daily 15-minute sync: yesterday, today, blockers.
  • Spike n. 探索任务 A timeboxed investigation task — perfect first assignment for a new joiner.
  • In flight phr. 在途 Started and moving, not yet done — tickets, approvals, PRs.
  • SLA abbr. 服务等级协议 The promised turnaround; day two of SLA says you know the clock.
  • Dependency n. 依赖 Something you need from someone else; flag it, do not hide it.
  • Orientation n. 定向熟悉 Week-one learning; the deliverable is understanding, not output.
  • Blocker n. 阻塞 Something actively stopping you; a "soft blocker" is not one yet.
  • Ledger mapping n. 总账映射 The GLM service's job — and the doc section that needed a definition.

5. Grammar Notes 语法点

① Past simple inventory for "yesterday"

"I read the doc, got access live, and sat with Daniel B." — three past-simple verbs, no connective tissue. The standup yesterday-line is an inventory, not a narrative: verbs in series, each one a completed item. If you need "and then", you are telling a story — trim it back to the list.

"I read the doc, got access live, and sat with Daniel B."——三个一般过去时动词，无连接组织。站会的"昨天"是清单不是叙事：动词并列，每项已完成。如果你需要"and then"，你就在讲故事——砍回到清单。

② The "not X — Y-ing" correction frame

"Not stuck — flagging the dependency.", "not a complaint — a fact." The not + adjective, -ing + noun frame re-labels your own message before anyone else does it. It is the politest way to say "read this correctly": deny the loud interpretation, name the precise one. New joiners who master this frame sound senior in week one.

"Not stuck — flagging the dependency."、"not a complaint — a fact."——not + 形容词, -ing + 名词句式在别人误读之前先给自己的信息重新贴标签。这是"请正确理解我"最礼貌的说法：否认响亮的解读，命名精确的那个。掌握这个句式的新人，第一周听起来就很资深。

6. Cultural Tips 文化 / 职场 Tips

  • Week-one standups report orientation, not output. "Three systems now make sense" is a legitimate yesterday.
  • Keep it to 45 seconds. Three lines. If your week-one update runs two minutes, you are narrating, not reporting.
  • Track your own tickets out loud. "Day two of SLA" tells the team you run your own follow-ups — trust arrives early for people who count days.
  • Soft blockers stay soft until they are hard. Flag the dependency, state the fallback ("I will pair rather than wait"), and only then does it graduate to a blocker.
  • Answer "what was hardest" with a patch. The doc-fix answer made you a contributor on day three. Look for one per week.

7. Practice 练习题

Exercise 1 — Your first three lines

Write tomorrow's standup update as a new joiner: yesterday (3 items), today (1 goal + why), blocker (status + fallback). 45 seconds when spoken.

Read it aloud with a timer. If it passes 60 seconds, cut adjectives, keep facts. The delta sentence ("X now makes sense that did not yesterday") is worth keeping even under time pressure — it is your week-one currency.

Exercise 2 — Blocker or dependency?

Classify: (a) access ticket day 2 of 2-day SLA; (b) access ticket day 5, no reply; (c) waiting on a colleague's review to start your task.

(a) dependency — flag only. (b) blocker — bump in the ticket, mention in standup. (c) dependency — with a named fallback (start with test data, pair). The classification decides the tone: dependencies get patience, blockers get motion.

Exercise 3 — The doc question

Elena asks which section was hardest. Write your answer using the three-part shape: which section, what it assumed, what one line would fix.

Model: "The GLM section — it assumed ledger-mapping knowledge. A one-line definition at the top would save every future joiner twenty minutes." Specific + generous: you are improving the doc for the next person, which is the cheapest reputation a new joiner can buy.
