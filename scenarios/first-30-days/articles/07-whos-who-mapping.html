Who's Who: Mapping the Org

Article 07 · First 30 Days English · 认清组织地图

⏱ 7 min read 🟢 Beginner-friendly 🎯 Learn who decides, who delivers, who unblocks — and route your questions accordingly

1. Scenario 场景背景

Week one, day four. You have met fifteen people and remember six names. Worse, you do not know who does what: two more Sarahs exist in the wider group, the org chart in the HR system is four levels deep, and yesterday you asked a budget question to the wrong person entirely. Time to build your own map — on one page.

第一周第四天。你见过 15 个人，记住了 6 个名字。更糟的是，你不知道谁做什么：更大的部门里还有两个 Sarah，HR 系统的组织架构图深达四层，而昨天你把预算问题问错了人。是时候自己画一张地图了——就一页。

The useful map is not the org chart — it is the question-routing map: for each kind of question you will actually have, who decides, who delivers, who unblocks, who must not be surprised. You build it by asking one routing question per person you meet.

有用的地图不是组织架构图，而是问题路由图：对你真正会有的每类问题——谁拍板、谁交付、谁解阻塞、谁不能被惊吓。构建方法是：每见到一个人，问一个路由问题。

2. Dialogue 英文对话

You (New Joiner): Ruling from yesterday\'s mistake: I asked Priya about budget. Priya owns delivery, not money. Lesson one: map by question type, not by job title.昨天错误的裁定：我把预算问题问给了 Priya。Priya 管交付，不管钱。教训一：按问题类型建图，而不是按职位头衔。
You (New Joiner): The map I am building has four columns: decides, delivers, unblocks, must-not-be-surprised. Today I fill it with one routing question per person — eleven coffees, one question each, done by Friday.我在建的地图有四栏：拍板、交付、解阻塞、不能被惊吓。今天用"每人一个路由问题"来填——11 杯咖啡、每人一个问题、周五前完成。
You (New Joiner): Testing the routing question on Marco first. "Marco — if I had a question about the data retention policy at 4 PM on a Friday, who do I ask, and how would they want to be asked?"先在 Marco 身上测试路由问题。"Marco——周五下午 4 点我有一个数据保留政策的问题，该问谁？他们希望被怎么问？"
Marco (Buddy): Ask Elena — but not at 4 PM Friday. Policy questions go to her in writing, weekdays, before noon. She answers writing better than talking, and Friday-afternoon policy debates are how bad decisions get made. Who to ask is half the answer; when and how is the other half.问 Elena——但别在周五下午 4 点。政策类问题书面提、工作日、中午前。她读文字的反应比口头好，而周五下午的政策辩论正是坏决策的生产线。问谁只是答案的一半；何时问、怎么问是另一半。
You (New Joiner): Adding a fifth column then: how they want to be asked. Elena: written, before noon. That column just saved my first impression. Next test: the two Daniels.那就加第五栏：他们希望被怎么问。Elena：书面、中午前。这一栏刚刚拯救了我的第一印象。下一个测试：两位 Daniel。
Daniel B (Senior Dev): Routing question? Fire away.路由问题？问吧。
You (New Joiner): "If production data access is stuck in compliance and my analysis is blocked — you, Elena, or Priya?""如果生产数据权限卡在合规，而我的分析被阻塞——找你、Elena 还是 Priya？"
Daniel B (Senior Dev): Me first — I have been through compliance four times and know which form actually moves. If I am dark, Priya escalates; she owns the unblocking muscle. Elena only hears about it if compliance says no — she is the architecture decision, not the process nudge.先找我——合规这条路我走过四次，知道哪张表真的管用。如果我不在，Priya 负责升级；解阻塞的肌肉归她。Elena 只在合规说不时才出场——她管架构决策，不管流程推动。
You (New Joiner): That is exactly the map — three names, three different jobs, one question. Last routing test: who must not be surprised? I hear that phrase in every onboarding doc and nobody defines it.这正是我要的地图——三个名字、三种分工、一个问题。最后一个路由测试：谁不能被惊吓？每份入职文档里都有这句话，但没人定义它。
Daniel B (Senior Dev): Whoever owns the thing before your work touches it. Your dashboard reads my pipeline? I should hear about scope changes before your demo does. It is not permission — it is no-surprise courtesy, and the phrase means: if learning it from the meeting would feel like an ambush, that is who.在你的工作触及之前就拥有那件事的人。你的仪表盘读我的管道？范围变更应该在你的演示之前让我知道。这不是审批权——这是"不惊吓"的礼节，这句话的意思是：如果从会议上得知会像被伏击，那个人就是。

3. Key Phrases 关键句型

  • Lesson Map by question type, not by job title. 按问题类型建图，而不是按职位头衔。 Titles tell you hierarchy; question types tell you routing. Only one of them answers your 4 PM Friday problem.
  • Columns Decides, delivers, unblocks, must-not-be-surprised. 拍板、交付、解阻塞、不能被惊吓。 The four-column map — one page, updated after every coffee.
  • Routing Who do I ask, and how would they want to be asked? 该问谁？他们希望被怎么问？ The double-barrel routing question — target plus protocol. The second half is where impressions are made.
  • Escalation If I am dark, Priya escalates. 如果我不在，Priya 负责升级。 Every node in your map needs a fallback name — "dark" is team slang for unreachable.
  • Define If learning it from the meeting would feel like an ambush, that is who. 如果从会议上得知会像被伏击，那个人就是。 The operational definition of "no surprises" — testable against any future decision.

4. Vocabulary 词汇表

  • Org chart n. 组织架构图 The official hierarchy — accurate, and almost useless for daily routing.
  • Routing n. 路由 Directing a question to the right person via the right channel.
  • Escalate v. 升级 Moving a blocked item to someone with more authority or reach.
  • Dark adj. 联系不上 Team slang for unreachable — on leave, heads-down, or asleep.
  • No-surprise courtesy n. 不惊吓礼节 Briefing owners before their domain is touched — not permission, courtesy.
  • Compliance step n. 合规步骤 The approval layer for sensitive access — slow, form-driven, survivable.
  • Ambush n. 伏击 Learning about a change to your domain in public — the feeling to avoid creating.
  • Stakeholder n. 利益相关方 Anyone affected by your work; some of them must not be surprised.

5. Grammar Notes 语法点

① Conditionals as routing rules

"If production data access is stuck in compliance and my analysis is blocked — you, Elena, or Priya?" — the if [situation], who? question form builds your map in real time. Routing rules are conditionals by nature: each coffee adds one. By week four you have a decision tree instead of a phone book.

"If production data access is stuck in compliance and my analysis is blocked — you, Elena, or Priya?"——if [情境], who? 问句实时构建你的地图。路由规则天然是条件句：每杯咖啡加一条。到第四周，你拥有的不是通讯录，而是一棵决策树。

② The emphatic "that is who" close

"If learning it from the meeting would feel like an ambush — that is who." — the conditional setup lands on the emphatic that is who, which converts a rule into a memorable answer. The same close works for any definition you are asked to give: "if it would embarrass the team in the retro — that is the thing to flag."

"If learning it from the meeting would feel like an ambush — that is who."——条件句铺垫后落在强调的 that is who 上，把规则变成难忘的答案。任何被要求给出的定义都可以用这个收尾："if it would embarrass the team in the retro — that is the thing to flag."

6. Cultural Tips 文化 / 职场 Tips

  • One page, four columns. Decides / delivers / unblocks / must-not-be-surprised — plus the fifth: how they want to be asked.
  • One routing question per coffee. "Who do I ask about X, and how do they want to be asked?" People love answering it — you are asking them to describe their own importance.
  • Every node needs a fallback. "If I am dark, Priya escalates" — maps without fallbacks fail on the first holiday.
  • Budget questions go to budget people. Yesterday\'s mis-route was the tuition; today\'s map is the scholarship.
  • "No surprises" is the phrase that rules them all. Before any demo or decision touching someone\'s domain, ask: would this ambush them?

7. Practice 练习题

Exercise 1 — Draft your one-page map

List the six people you will rely on in month one. For each: their column (decides/delivers/unblocks), their ask-protocol, and their fallback when dark.

Gaps in the map are tomorrow\'s coffees: anyone with a blank ask-protocol gets the double-barrel question this week. The map is never done — it is maintained, like Elena\'s acronyms doc.

Exercise 2 — The ambush test

Your dashboard demo tomorrow will show a metric sourced from Daniel B's pipeline — with a change you made to its logic. Does he need a heads-up?

Yes — and the test explains it: learning his pipeline\'s behaviour changed from a meeting would feel like an ambush. One line tonight: "Tomorrow\'s demo shows the pipeline metric with my adjusted logic — flagging before you see it live." Thirty seconds, no-surprise courtesy delivered.

Exercise 3 — Route three questions

Route these using your map: (a) a scope change to your analysis, (b) a policy question on data retention, (c) budget approval for a new tool.

(a) Elena decides, Priya informed — scope touches architecture; (b) Elena, written, before noon; (c) Sarah or her manager — never delivery. If your answers differ from these, your map disagrees with the org — which is worth one clarifying coffee.
