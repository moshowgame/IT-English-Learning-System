The Salary Expectations Question

Article 10 · Job Interview English · 薪资期望问题

⏱ 8 min read 🔴 Advanced 🎯 Anchor with data, stay warm, and trade packages — not just base numbers

1. Scenario 场景背景

Late in the second round for a Developer role. Priya, the recruiter who screened you, reappears for the compensation conversation. She opens pleasantly with the question candidates fear: "So — what are your salary expectations?"

第二轮面试接近尾声。初筛时见过的招聘伙伴 Priya 再次出现，进行薪酬沟通。她愉快地抛出候选人最怕的问题："那么——你的薪资期望是多少？"

The salary question is not a number question — it is a calibration exercise. Priya needs to know whether you fit the band; you need to avoid anchoring yourself below it or pricing yourself out of it. The professional play: market data first, a range second, total package third, and warmth throughout.

薪资问题不是数字问题，而是校准练习。Priya 要确认你在带宽内；你要避免报低锚死自己，或报高把自己挤出带宽。职业化打法：先市场数据、再给区间、再看总包、全程保持温度。

2. Dialogue 英文对话

Priya (Talent Acquisition): So — what are your salary expectations?那么——你的薪资期望是多少？
You (Candidate): Happy to talk numbers. May I ask what band the role is budgeted at? That way I can tell you honestly whether we fit, instead of shouting numbers across a gap.很乐意谈数字。我能先问一下这个岗位的预算带宽吗？这样我可以诚实判断彼此是否匹配，而不是隔着鸿沟互相喊数字。
Priya (Talent Acquisition): I can share it once we\'re at offer stage. Give me your expectation first.Offer 阶段我可以分享。你先说你的期望。
You (Candidate): Fair enough. The anchor I\'d use is the market, not my current pay: for a senior developer role at a global bank in this city, surveyed ranges put the midpoint around 55–65k, depending on stack and on-call. Given my payments domain depth, I\'d see myself in the upper half of that band.合理。我的锚是市场，不是我现在的薪资：全球银行在本市的资深开发岗位，调研区间中位在 55–65k，取决于技术栈和值班安排。考虑到我的支付领域深度，我自认在带宽上半段。
Priya (Talent Acquisition): That may be a stretch for this req. What are you on now?对这个岗位可能偏高了。你现在薪资多少？
You (Candidate): I\'m on 48k — but I\'d ask us both not to use that as the yardstick, because I took that number at a smaller company for the domain switch, and I\'ve delivered since. Current pay tells you my past; the market band tells you the job. I\'d rather calibrate on the job.我现在 48k——但我想我们都别拿它当标尺，因为那个数字是我为转领域在小公司接受的，此后我有实打实的交付。当前薪资说明我的过去；市场带宽说明这个岗位。我更愿意按岗位来校准。
Priya (Talent Acquisition): Noted. If the base lands below your ask, is that a dealbreaker?记下了。如果底薪低于你的期望，是一票否决吗？
You (Candidate): Not automatically — the package is a system, not a number. Bonus structure, on-call allowance, learning budget, hybrid days, and the title all carry value. If the base is 50 with a real bonus and a real review at six months, that may beat 55 flat. I\'m negotiating the shape, not just the top line.不自动否决——薪酬是一个系统，不是一个数字。奖金结构、值班津贴、学习预算、混合办公天数、职级都有价值。如果底薪 50 但有实打实的奖金和六个月复评，可能好过光秃秃的 55。我谈的是结构，不只是顶线。
Priya (Talent Acquisition): That\'s a refreshingly reasonable framing. I\'ll take this into the offer discussion.这个框架非常理性。我会带进 Offer 讨论。
You (Candidate): Thank you — and whatever the number, I\'d rather we both feel the package was honest than win a point today and regret it in March.谢谢——无论数字多少，我希望我们双方都觉得这个薪酬包是诚实的，而不是今天赢一分、三月后悔。

3. Key Phrases 关键句型

  • Reverse May I ask what band the role is budgeted at? 能先问岗位的预算带宽吗？ The polite attempt to flip the order. Even when refused, it signals you know bands exist.
  • Anchor The anchor I\'d use is the market, not my current pay. 我的锚是市场，不是我现在的薪资。 Saying the anchor out loud prevents silent substitution of your old salary for the band.
  • Range Surveyed ranges put the midpoint around 55–65k. 调研区间中位在 55–65k。 "Surveyed" — attributing the number to data keeps it from being personal desire.
  • Deflect I\'d ask us both not to use that as the yardstick. 我想我们都别拿它当标尺。 Declines the current-salary anchor without refusing the question — the key move of this conversation.
  • System The package is a system, not a number. 薪酬是一个系统，不是一个数字。 Opens the components conversation: bonus, allowance, learning, title, review points.
  • Shape I\'m negotiating the shape, not just the top line. 我谈的是结构，不只是顶线。 Tells the recruiter you can be made happy below your base ask — flexibility with dignity.
  • Warmth Rather we both feel the package was honest than win a point today. 宁可双方都觉得诚实，而不是今天赢一分。 Ends hard money talk as a future colleague, not an adversary.

4. Vocabulary 词汇表

  • Band n. 薪酬带宽 The approved salary range for a role; recruiters must fit within it.
  • Anchor n. 锚点 The first number placed in a negotiation — it drags every later number.
  • Yardstick n. 标尺 The measure being used to judge; reject the wrong one explicitly.
  • Req n. 招聘需求 Requisition — the approved opening, with its budget attached.
  • Dealbreaker n. 一票否决项 A condition that ends the deal on its own.
  • On-call allowance n. 值班津贴 Extra pay for carrying the pager; part of the package system.
  • Comp review n. 薪酬复评 A scheduled re-examination of pay — the six-month review is worth real money.
  • Total package n. 总薪酬包 Base + bonus + benefits + allowances, viewed as one system.
  • Calibrate v. 校准 Aligning expectations to data — what this conversation actually is.
  • Hybrid days n. 混合办公天数 Days working remotely vs office; worth real value in the package.

5. Grammar Notes 语法点

① Would rather X than Y — the negotiation conditional

"I\'d rather we both feel the package was honest than win a point today." — would rather + clause + than + clause states preference between futures, and in salary talk it does double duty: it offers flexibility while refusing desperation. Same grammar, softer use: "I\'d rather calibrate on the job than anchor on my past."

"I\'d rather we both feel the package was honest than win a point today."——would rather + 从句 + than + 从句表达对未来的偏好，在谈薪场景一语双关：既展示灵活，又拒绝廉价。同款："I\'d rather calibrate on the job than anchor on my past."

② Imperative-in-disguise: "I'd ask us both to…"

"I\'d ask us both not to use that as the yardstick." — a request dressed as a soft conditional. Compare the blunt "don\'t use my current salary" (accusatory) with "I\'d ask us both…" (joint). The grammar of "us both" makes the norm shared rather than imposed — the politeness register that keeps recruiters on your side mid-negotiation.

"I\'d ask us both not to use that as the yardstick."——伪装成柔性条件句的请求。对比生硬的"别用我现在的薪资"（指责感）与 "I\'d ask us both…"（共同视角）。"us both" 的语法让规范变成共享的而非强加的——这是谈判中让招聘伙伴保持友好的礼貌语域。

6. Cultural Tips 文化 / 职场 Tips

  • Try to get the band first. If refused, anchor to market data — never to your current salary.
  • Give a researched range, not a point. Ranges calibrate; points get matched or beaten by exactly one increment.
  • Separate past from price. "Current pay tells you my past; the market band tells you the job" — memorise the sentence, it does a lot of work.
  • Negotiate the shape. Bonus, allowance, review timing, title — a package conversation gives the recruiter room to say yes to you.
  • Stay warm. You are speaking to a future colleague. The best salary endings sound like "honest package", not "hard win".

7. Practice 练习题

Exercise 1 — Build your range

For your target role and city, find two salary surveys and write your one-line anchor: "Surveyed ranges put the midpoint around X–Y, depending on ___."

The "depending on" tail is doing real work — it names the variables (stack, on-call, domain depth) that justify the upper half of your range. Without it, the range is just hope.

Exercise 2 — Yardstick deflection

Priya pushes three times: "What are you on now?" Draft your deflection for the third push — each one warmer, none surrendering the anchor.

1) "I\'d rather calibrate on the job — the band tells you more than my history." 2) "My current number reflects a domain switch I chose; I\'d rather we price the role." 3) "I\'ll share it at offer stage if it helps paperwork — but let\'s agree the band is the yardstick." Warmth held, anchor held.

Exercise 3 — Package math

Offer A: 55k flat. Offer B: 50k base, 10% bonus, 3k learning budget, comp review at six months. Write the two sentences you would say to compare them.

"B\'s guaranteed value is 55k-equivalent if the bonus is real — and the comp review reprices it in six months, which A never does. I\'d take B if the review is contractual, and A if it\'s a vibe." — shape, not top line.
