Researching the Bank and the Role

Article 03 · Job Interview English · 研究银行与岗位

⏱ 8 min read 🟡 Intermediate 🎯 Turn homework into interview answers: the org, the platform, the pain, the strategy

1. Scenario 场景背景

Your interview is in three days for a Product Owner role in HSBC\'s digital channels. A friend who works there told you one useful thing: "They ask what you know about us — and most candidates say nothing useful." Tonight you sit down to do the research properly, and your notes become tomorrow\'s answers.

距离 Product Owner（数字渠道）岗位面试还有 3 天。一位在汇丰工作的朋友告诉你一条有用信息："他们会问你对汇丰了解多少——大多数候选人答不出有用的东西。"今晚你坐下来认真做功课，笔记将变成面试时的答案。

Good research answers four questions: what the bank is changing (strategy), who you would work with (org), what the team is struggling with (pain), and what the role actually does (JD beyond the ad). Anything you cannot connect to one of those four is trivia, not preparation.

有效的调研回答四个问题：银行正在改变什么（战略）、你将与谁共事（组织）、团队的痛点、以及岗位实际做什么（JD 之外的解读）。无法对应到这四点的信息只是冷知识，不是准备。

2. Dialogue 英文对话

You (Candidate): OK, research plan. Layer one: the bank. I need two strategy facts I can say out loud — not twenty I downloaded and forgot.好，调研计划。第一层：银行。我需要两条能脱口而出的战略事实——而不是二十条下载了就忘的。
You (Candidate): From the annual report: they are pushing "digitise the bank at scale" and shrinking the branch network. Both mean the digital channels team is a cost-and-growth centre, not a side project. That is my opening fact.从年报看：他们在推进"大规模数字化银行"并缩减网点。这意味着数字渠道团队是成本与增长中枢，不是边缘项目。这是我的开场事实。
You (Candidate): Layer two: the org. The job ad says "Digital Channels, RBWM". I need to know who that reports to and what squads exist. LinkedIn is the tool — search the title, read three profiles, note the tools and jargon they repeat.第二层：组织。JD 写的是 "Digital Channels, RBWM"。我要弄清它向谁汇报、有哪些 squad。用 LinkedIn——搜岗位名、读三份主页、记下他们反复提到的工具和黑话。
You (Candidate): Interesting — three product owners all mention "mobile app 7.0". That must be the current programme. Jot that down; asking about it shows I read the room before arriving.有意思——三位产品负责人都提到 "mobile app 7.0"。这应该就是当前的大项目。记下来；面试时问起它，说明我到场前做足了功课。
You (Candidate): Layer three: the pain. Search the app store reviews for their mobile app — the one-star reviews are the backlog. Login flow complaints, session timeouts… that is what a PO in that team probably wrestles with daily.第三层：痛点。去应用商店搜他们 App 的评论——一星评论就是待办清单。登录流程差评、会话超时……这些大概率就是那个团队的 PO 每天在搏斗的东西。
You (Candidate): Layer four: the JD, read backwards. Under each responsibility I ask: what problem makes this responsibility exist? "Own the roadmap for login journeys" ← that exists because login is where customers leak. Now I know what the first month would really be about.第四层：倒着读 JD。对每条职责问：什么问题让这条职责存在？"负责登录旅程路线图" ← 它存在是因为登录是客户流失点。现在我知道入职第一个月真正要做什么了。
You (Candidate): Last thing — my three questions for them, built from this research. One about the programme, one about the team, one about success. Questions made of research are worth ten generic ones.最后一件事——基于调研准备三个反问。一个关于项目、一个关于团队、一个关于成功标准。由调研构成的十个问题抵得上一个泛泛之问。

3. Key Phrases 关键句型

  • Frame Layer one / two / three… 第一层/第二层/第三层…… Numbering your prep keeps research finite — you stop when the layers are covered, not when the internet ends.
  • Strategy They are pushing "digitise the bank at scale". 他们在推进"大规模数字化银行"。 Quote the strategy in their own words from their own report — accuracy signals real reading.
  • Implication That means the team is a cost-and-growth centre, not a side project. 这意味着该团队是成本与增长中枢，不是边缘项目。 Facts are cheap; the implication is what proves you can think in their shoes.
  • Signal Asking about it shows I read the room before arriving. 问起它说明我到场前做足了功课。 "Read the room" — knowing the current programme is the interview equivalent of knowing the dress code.
  • Pain The one-star reviews are the backlog. 一星评论就是待办清单。 A memorable one-liner product people use — using it in an interview marks you as one of them.
  • JD What problem makes this responsibility exist? 什么问题让这条职责存在？ Reading a JD backwards finds the real job under the listed job.
  • Close Questions made of research are worth ten generic ones. 由调研构成的十个问题抵得上一个泛泛之问。 Your reverse questions are graded — researched ones double as evidence you prepare like an owner.

4. Vocabulary 词汇表

  • Annual report n. 年报 The bank's yearly public document — strategy, priorities, numbers.
  • RBWM abbr. 财富管理及个人银行业务 Retail Banking and Wealth Management — the customer-facing business line.
  • Digital channels n. 数字渠道 Mobile app, online banking and other digital customer touchpoints.
  • Product Owner (PO) n. 产品负责人 Owns the backlog and priorities for a squad's product area.
  • Roadmap n. 路线图 The sequenced plan of what gets built when, owned by product.
  • Backlog n. 待办列表 The prioritised list of everything a team might build.
  • Journey n. （用户）旅程 The end-to-end path a customer takes through a flow, e.g. login.
  • Customer leak n. 客户流失点 A step in a journey where customers abandon or churn.
  • Due diligence n. 尽职调查 Research done before a commitment — interviews included.
  • Panel n. 面试小组 Multiple interviewers in one session; researched questions shine here.

5. Grammar Notes 语法点

① Imperatives in your own head, declaratives to the room

Your notes can be imperative — "search the title, read three profiles" — but interview answers must be declarative and past-tense: "I read three PO profiles and noticed all of them mention mobile app 7.0." Practice: convert every research note into one past-simple sentence you could say aloud. Notes are for you; sentences are for them.

给自己看的笔记可以用祈使句（"search the title, read three profiles"），但面试回答必须是陈述句和过去时："I read three PO profiles and noticed…"。练习方法：把每条调研笔记改写成一句可以脱口而出的过去时句子。笔记给自己，句子给对方。

② Because-clauses prove inference

"Both mean the digital channels team is a cost-and-growth centre." The grammar that makes research impressive is fact + because-clause + implication: "They are shrinking branches, which means…", "The ad asks for roadmap ownership, because login is where customers leak." Anyone can quote a fact; the which-means clause is what turns you from a reader into an analyst.

让调研显得有深度的语法是事实 + 因果从句 + 推论："They are shrinking branches, which means…"、"The ad asks for roadmap ownership, because login is where customers leak."。引用事实人人都会；which means 从句才把读者变成分析者。

6. Cultural Tips 文化 / 职场 Tips

  • Two facts said well beat twenty facts said badly. Prepare two strategy sentences and two org sentences. No more.
  • Use their words. If the annual report says "digitise at scale", you say "digitise at scale" — not your paraphrase of it.
  • App-store reviews are free user research. Three one-star reviews tell you the team\'s real backlog better than any press release.
  • Do not quote confidential internals. If a friend tells you something non-public, keep it as context, never as an answer — it signals poor judgement.
  • Your reverse questions are the exam\'s second half. Prepare exactly three: programme, team, success criteria.

7. Practice 练习题

Exercise 1 — The four-layer drill

For any company you are interviewing with, write one sentence per layer: strategy, org, pain, JD-backwards. Ban yourself from more than four.

The constraint is the exercise: if you cannot compress the research into four load-bearing sentences, you have collected trivia instead of answers. Each sentence should contain a which-means clause.

Exercise 2 — Upgrade the question

Turn this generic question into a researched one: "What is the team culture like?"

Researched version: "I saw three of your POs mention mobile app 7.0 — what does success look like for that programme in the next six months, and how does the team celebrate or review it?" Specific anchor + real question.

Exercise 3 — Spot the trivia

Which of these is preparation, and which is trivia? (a) The bank's founding year. (b) The app's login-flow complaints in recent reviews. (c) The CEO's nationality. (d) The strategy sentence from the annual report.

Preparation: (b) and (d) — pain and strategy, both usable in answers. (a) and (c) are trivia: pleasant, but they prove nothing about how you will think in the job.
