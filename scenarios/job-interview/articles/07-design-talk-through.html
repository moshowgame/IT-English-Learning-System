System Design Talk-Through

Article 07 · Job Interview English · 系统设计题讲解

⏱ 9 min read 🔴 Advanced 🎯 Think aloud, drive with questions, trade off honestly — design interviews are dialogue

1. Scenario 场景背景

Final round for a Senior Developer role: the design exercise. Mei, an architect, slides a one-line prompt across the table: "Design a notification service for a retail bank — email, push, SMS, 20 million customers." Then she says the sentence that defines the next 40 minutes: "I care less about the destination and more about the journey — think aloud."

Senior Developer 岗位的最终轮：设计题。Mei（架构师）把一行题目推到桌面："为一个零售银行设计通知服务——邮件、推送、短信，2000 万客户。"然后她说了一句定义接下来 40 分钟的话："我更在乎过程而不是终点——把思考说出来。"

A design interview is not a whiteboard exam, it is a dialogue. The candidates who fail are not the ones with wrong answers — they are the ones who go silent, or who never ask what the problem actually is. Your tools: clarifying questions, visible trade-offs, and a steady stream of thinking-aloud.

设计面试不是白板考试，而是对话。失败的候选人不是答案错的——而是陷入沉默、或从不追问问题本质的人。你的工具：澄清问题、外显的取舍、以及源源不断的思考外化。

2. Dialogue 英文对话

Mei (Architect): Design a notification service for a retail bank — email, push, SMS, 20 million customers. Think aloud; I care about the journey.为零售银行设计通知服务——邮件、推送、短信，2000 万客户。把思考说出来；我看重过程。
You (Candidate): Before drawing anything — two clarifying questions. First: are notifications marketing or operational? A fraud alert and a credit-card ad have very different reliability contracts. Second: what does "late" mean here — is a balance alert 5 minutes late a bug or fine?画图之前——两个澄清问题。第一：通知是营销还是业务运营？反欺诈提醒和信用卡广告的可靠性契约完全不同。第二：这里"迟到"意味着什么——余额提醒晚 5 分钟是故障还是可接受？
Mei (Architect): Operational only — fraud, balance, service alerts. And late is a bug over one minute for fraud, ten for the rest.只做业务运营——反欺诈、余额、服务提醒。反欺诈迟到 1 分钟算故障，其他 10 分钟。
You (Candidate): That shapes everything. Fraud alerts are the critical path, so they get priority lanes and tighter SLAs; the rest can batch. Let me sketch the shape: producers publish events, a router maps event types to channels, a per-channel sender fleet, and — because this is a bank — a full audit trail and idempotent sends.这决定了整个设计。反欺诈提醒是关键路径，享有优先通道和更严的 SLA；其余可以批量。画一下整体：生产方发布事件，路由器把事件类型映射到渠道，每个渠道一个发送集群，而且——这是银行——完整审计链路 + 幂等发送。
Mei (Architect): Why idempotent? Convince me.为什么要幂等？说服我。
You (Candidate): Because retries are not failures in this system — they are Tuesday. Every sender will time out and retry, and a fraud alert sent twice is not redundancy, it is a trust problem with the customer. So every notification carries an idempotency key; the senders treat duplicates as no-ops. Better to dedupe inside the service than to apologise outside it.因为在这个系统里重试不是故障——是家常便饭。每个发送方都会超时重试，反欺诈提醒发两次不是冗余，是客户信任问题。所以每条通知带幂等键，发送方把重复当作无操作。宁可服务内去重，不要服务外道歉。
Mei (Architect): 20 million customers, fraud alert at midnight. Where does this break first?2000 万客户，午夜反欺诈提醒。哪里先坏？
You (Candidate): Honest answer: the SMS gateway is a third party and I do not own it — my first bottleneck is probably their throughput, which is why the design needs a queue with backpressure rather than a direct call. Second break: the fan-out — 20 million rows is fine to store, but building 20 million personalised messages at midnight needs pre-computed templates, not string-building at send time.诚实回答：短信网关是第三方，不归我管——第一个瓶颈很可能是他们的吞吐量，所以设计上要排队 + 背压，而不是直接调用。第二个坏点：扇出——2000 万行存储没问题，但午夜生成 2000 万条个性化消息需要预计算模板，而不是发送时拼字符串。
Mei (Architect): Good. What did you choose NOT to build, and why?好。你选择不做什么？为什么？
You (Candidate): Three deliberate noes. No user-facing preferences UI — out of scope today, but the schema reserves the flags. No custom SMS infrastructure — a commodity third party is cheaper than being special. No real-time analytics — valuable, but not before the reliability contract is proven. Every one of those is a one-way door I would rather leave open than walk through early.三个刻意的"不"。不做用户偏好界面——今天超纲，但表结构预留字段。不自建短信基础设施——标准化第三方比自建便宜。不做实时分析——有价值，但排在可靠性契约被验证之后。每一项都是我宁可留着不走的单向门。
Mei (Architect): That is the answer I was listening for. Well done.这正是我在等的答案。表现出色。

3. Key Phrases 关键句型

  • Clarify Before drawing anything — two clarifying questions. 画图之前——先问两个澄清问题。 The strongest opening move in a design interview. Requirements are the first test.
  • Think aloud That shapes everything. 这一下决定了整个设计。 Says out loud that new information changed the design — the exact skill being scored.
  • Prioritise Fraud alerts are the critical path. 反欺诈提醒是关键路径。 Name the critical path explicitly; it licenses every later prioritisation.
  • Convince Retries are not failures in this system — they are Tuesday. 在这个系统里重试不是故障——是家常便饭。 Humor plus reframing: normalising the failure mode is the first step of designing for it.
  • Trade-off Better to dedupe inside the service than to apologise outside it. 宁可服务内去重，不要服务外道歉。 A portable trade-off aphorism — candidates who coin these get remembered.
  • Honesty Honest answer: the SMS gateway is a third party and I do not own it. 诚实回答：短信网关是第三方，不归我管。 Naming what you do not control is senior behaviour — it shows system thinking beyond your box.
  • Scope Every one of those is a one-way door I would rather leave open. 每一项都是我宁可留着不走的单向门。 The "what did you NOT build" answer — reversible decisions early, irreversible late.

4. Vocabulary 词汇表

  • Think aloud phr. 思维外化 Narrating your reasoning as you work — the core skill design interviews score.
  • Operational notification n. 业务运营通知 Fraud alerts, balance alerts, service messages — not marketing.
  • SLA abbr. 服务等级协议 The promised reliability/latency contract; fraud gets tighter ones.
  • Critical path n. 关键路径 The part of the system where failure hurts most and first.
  • Idempotency key n. 幂等键 A unique marker ensuring one notification is sent exactly once.
  • Backpressure n. 背压 Queues refusing upstream load when downstream is saturated.
  • Fan-out n. 扇出 One event multiplied to many recipients — the scaling pain of notifications.
  • Throughput n. 吞吐量 How much a component can process per unit time.
  • One-way door n. 单向门 A hard-to-reverse decision — take these late and deliberately.
  • Out of scope phr. 超出范围 Explicitly excluded from today's design — but say what you reserved for it.

5. Grammar Notes 语法点

① Conditionals frame the break points

"If 20 million fraud alerts queue at midnight, the SMS gateway saturates first." Design interviews run on first conditionals — [if scenario], [consequence]. The structure keeps your speculation disciplined: every hypothetical has a named consequence, which is what separates analysis from guessing. Keep the if-clause concrete: numbers, times, components.

设计面试靠第一类条件句运转：[if 场景]，[后果]。"If 20 million fraud alerts queue at midnight, the SMS gateway saturates first." 这个结构让推测保持纪律：每个假设都有明确的后果——这是分析与瞎猜的分界。if 从句要具体：数字、时间、组件。

② Gerunds name the patterns

"Building 20 million personalised messages at midnight needs pre-computed templates." — building as subject turns an activity into a designable thing. The same grammar carries your noes: "No custom SMS infrastructure — being special is expensive." Gerunds let you talk about kinds of work (fan-out, dedupe, string-building) as objects you can trade off.

building 作主语把活动变成可设计的对象："Building 20 million personalised messages at midnight needs pre-computed templates." 同样的语法支撑你的"不"："No custom SMS infrastructure — being special is expensive." 动名词让你把一类工作（扇出、去重、拼字符串）当作可权衡的对象来谈。

6. Cultural Tips 文化 / 职场 Tips

  • Clarify before you draw. Two questions in the first two minutes signal seniority louder than any architecture.
  • Say what changed your mind. "That shapes everything" is thinking aloud in its purest form — interviewers cannot read your head; narrate it.
  • Name what you do not own. Third-party gateways, upstream teams, customer behaviour — saying "I do not control that" is system thinking, not weakness.
  • Have a "what I did not build" list. Scope discipline is the most senior signal in design interviews — every no needs a one-line why.
  • Silence is the only real failure. A mediocre architecture discussed honestly outscores a brilliant one delivered mute.

7. Practice 练习题

Exercise 1 — Clarify first

For "design a payment refund service", write the four clarifying questions you would ask before drawing anything.

Strong set: internal-only or customer-facing refunds? What volume and peak (sales events)? What is the SLA — when does "late" become a bug? What exists already — ledger, payment service, or greenfield? Requirements first; architecture second.

Exercise 2 — Trade-off aphorisms

Turn these into one-line trade-off statements: (a) dedupe vs duplicate sends, (b) queue vs direct call to a weak dependency, (c) build vs buy for SMS.

(a) "Better to dedupe inside than apologise outside." (b) "A queue absorbs what a direct call would broadcast." (c) "Be special where you differentiate; be commodity where you don\'t." Each names both sides in one breath.

Exercise 3 — The noes drill

For the notification service, write the "what I would build differently" answer Mei asked for — two items, each with a mechanism.

Model: "I would start with the audit log, not bolt it on — replay is the feature ops loves. And I would question the easy pass earlier: easy to build is not a reason." Specific change + portable mechanism, every time.
