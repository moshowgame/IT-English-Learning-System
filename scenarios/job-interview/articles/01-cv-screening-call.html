The CV Screening Call

Article 01 · Job Interview English · 简历初筛电话

⏱ 7 min read 🟢 Beginner-friendly 🎯 The 15-minute recruiter call: pitch yourself, stay calm, get to the next round

1. Scenario 场景背景

It is 2:00 PM on a Tuesday. You applied for a Business Analyst role at HSBC through the careers portal. Three days later, a talent acquisition partner named Priya calls you for a 15-minute screening call before anything else happens.

周二下午 2 点。你通过招聘网站投递了汇丰的 Business Analyst 岗位。三天后，招聘伙伴 Priya 打来电话，进行正式流程前的 15 分钟简历初筛。

The screening call has one purpose: decide whether you go to the next round. Priya will check three things — can you explain your CV in a straight line, do your salary and notice-period expectations fit, and do you sound like someone the hiring manager should meet. Your job is to be clear, honest and easy to recommend.

初筛电话只有一个目的：决定你是否进入下一轮。Priya 会确认三件事：你能否一条线讲清自己的简历、薪资和到岗时间是否匹配、以及你是否听起来值得推荐给用人经理。你要做的就是清晰、诚实、容易被推荐。

2. Dialogue 英文对话

Priya (Talent Acquisition): Hi, this is Priya from HSBC Talent Acquisition. Is this a good time for a quick chat about the BA role you applied for?你好，我是汇丰招聘团队的 Priya。现在方便快速聊几句你投递的 BA 岗位吗？
You (Candidate): Hi Priya, yes, this is a good time. Thanks for calling.Priya 你好，现在方便，谢谢你来电。
Priya (Talent Acquisition): Great. Walk me through your CV — keep it to two minutes, and focus on what fits the BA role.好的。请用两分钟过一下你的简历，重点讲和 BA 岗位匹配的部分。
You (Candidate): Sure. The one-line version: I have been a BA for four years in a retail bank, mostly on payments and onboarding flows. Most recently I led the requirements for a KYC remediation programme — that is about 60% of this role, which is why I applied.好的。一句话版本：我在零售银行做了四年 BA，主要做支付和开户流程。最近我负责 KYC 整改项目的需求——大约覆盖这个岗位 60% 的内容，所以我投了。
Priya (Talent Acquisition): That helps. Why HSBC?明白了。为什么选择汇丰？
You (Candidate): Two reasons. First, the scale of change — you are mid-way through a big regulatory programme, and that is exactly the BA work I do best. Second, honestly, your graduates: two colleagues I respect moved here and grew fast. I want that environment.两个原因。第一，变化的规模——汇丰正处在一个大型监管项目中期，这正是我最擅长的 BA 工作。第二，说实在的，这里的人：两位我尊敬的同事跳过来后成长很快，我想要这样的环境。
Priya (Talent Acquisition): Good. What are your salary expectations?好。你的薪资期望是多少？
You (Candidate): I am currently at X, and based on the market band for this role I am looking at around Y. That said, the package overall matters more to me than the base alone — happy to discuss the full picture at offer stage.我目前是 X，参考这个岗位的市场带宽，我的期望在 Y 左右。不过相比单一底薪，我更看重整体薪酬——Offer 阶段细聊完全没问题。
Priya (Talent Acquisition): Noted. How much notice do you need to give?记下了。你需要多长的离职通知期？
You (Candidate): One month with my current employer. I can be flexible by two weeks either way if the start date matters.现公司是一个月。如果入职时间有要求，前后两周内我都可以配合。
Priya (Talent Acquisition): Perfect. Do you have any questions for me?很好。你有什么想问我的吗？
You (Candidate): One quick one: what does the interview loop look like, and who makes the final call?一个小问题：面试流程有几轮，最终由谁拍板？
Priya (Talent Acquisition): One technical round, one panel with the hiring manager, then offer. I will put you forward — expect an email by Friday.一轮技术面，一轮用人经理小组面，然后是 Offer。我会推荐你进入下一轮——预计周五前邮件通知。

3. Key Phrases 关键句型

  • Open Is this a good time for a quick chat? 现在方便快速聊几句吗？ The recruiter is asking you — echo it back positively: "yes, this is a good time."
  • Pitch The one-line version: … 一句话版本是…… Signalling a summary first shows you respect the 15-minute window. Then expand.
  • Fit That is about 60% of this role, which is why I applied. 这大约覆盖该岗位 60% 的内容，所以我投了。 Quantify the overlap between your CV and the JD — it does the matching for the recruiter.
  • Why The scale of change — that is exactly the work I do best. 变化的规模正是我最擅长的工作。 "Exactly the work I do best" ties your answer back to the role, not to the brand.
  • Money Based on the market band for this role, I am looking at around Y. 参考该岗位市场带宽，我的期望在 Y 左右。 "Market band" anchors your number to data, not to your current salary.
  • Money Happy to discuss the full picture at offer stage. Offer 阶段细聊整体薪酬没问题。 Defers deep negotiation without refusing to answer. Keep it warm.
  • Close What does the interview loop look like, and who makes the final call? 面试流程几轮？谁最终拍板？ Process questions at the end signal you are already planning to join the loop.

4. Vocabulary 词汇表

  • Talent Acquisition (TA) n. 招聘团队 The HR function that sources and screens candidates before hiring managers see them.
  • Screening call n. 初筛电话 A short first call to check basics: CV fit, salary range, notice period.
  • Notice period n. 离职通知期 How much warning you must give your current employer before leaving.
  • Interview loop n. 面试流程 The full set of rounds between screening and offer.
  • Hiring manager n. 用人经理 The manager with the open role — usually makes the final call.
  • Market band n. 市场薪酬带宽 The salary range typical for a role in the market; anchors your expectation.
  • Remediation programme n. 整改项目 A programme fixing past compliance or process issues — common in banks.
  • Put you forward phr. 推荐你进入下一轮 TA-speak for "I am advancing your application".

5. Grammar Notes 语法点

① Present simple for facts, past simple for history

When you walk a recruiter through your CV, keep the tenses clean: "I have been a BA for four years" (present perfect — still true), "Most recently I led the requirements for…" (past simple — a completed chapter). Mixing them up ("I am leading" for a finished project) makes your timeline fuzzy in exactly the call where clarity is being scored.

过简历时保持时态干净：仍在持续的用现在完成时（I have been…），已完成的一章用一般过去时（I led…）。在一场专门考察"表达是否清晰"的电话里，时态混乱会让你的经历时间线变模糊。

② Conditionals soften money talk

"I can be flexible by two weeks either way if the start date matters." The if-clause makes flexibility sound like planning, not desperation. The same pattern works for salary: "If the band allows, I am looking at around Y." Conditionals move the number from a demand to a scenario.

if 从句让"灵活"听起来是规划而不是妥协："I can be flexible… if the start date matters."。谈薪同理："If the band allows, I am looking at around Y."——条件句把数字从"要求"变成"情景"。

6. Cultural Tips 文化 / 职场 Tips

  • Screening calls are pass/fail, not graded. The recruiter is not scoring your brilliance — they are checking for red flags. Clear, honest, structured wins.
  • Prepare your one-line CV. "Four years as a BA in retail banking, payments and onboarding, most recently KYC remediation." If you cannot say it in one breath, trim it.
  • Never lie about notice periods or salary — both are verified at offer stage, and a discrepancy kills offers faster than a weak interview.
  • Always take one process question. "What does the loop look like?" costs five seconds and signals commitment.
  • Sound like a recommendation. Priya has to write two lines to the hiring manager. Make them easy: "strong KYC fit, realistic expectations, one-month notice."

7. Practice 练习题

Exercise 1 — Rebuild your one-line CV

Compress your own CV into exactly two sentences: one for experience, one for why this role. Say it aloud in under 15 seconds.

Sample frame: "I have been a [role] for [N] years in [industry], focused on [domain A] and [domain B]. Most recently I [single proudest relevant achievement], which is about [X]% of this role — that is why I applied."

Exercise 2 — Spot the weak answer

Which salary answer hurts the candidate most, and why?

  1. "I need at least Y because my rent went up."
  2. "Based on the market band for this role, I am looking at around Y — happy to discuss the full package."
  3. "Whatever you offer is fine."

① ties the number to personal expenses (weak position, invites lowball); ③ surrenders all negotiating power. ② anchors to market data and keeps warmth — it is the defensible answer.

Exercise 3 — Your turn — full run

Write your own answers to Priya's five questions: CV walkthrough, why this bank, salary expectations, notice period, one process question. Record yourself once.

Check your recording for three things: a one-line CV summary, a salary answer anchored to market (not to rent), and exactly one process question at the end. If any answer ran over 30 seconds, it is too long for a screening call.
