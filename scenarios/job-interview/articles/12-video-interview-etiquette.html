Video Interview Etiquette

Article 12 · Job Interview English · 视频面试礼仪

⏱ 7 min read 🟢 Beginner-friendly 🎯 Camera, light, audio, recovery scripts — the tech failures are part of the test

1. Scenario 场景背景

First-round interview for a PM role, on Microsoft Teams, camera on. The interview starts in five minutes. Everything that can go wrong with video calls is quietly deciding whether you look like someone who runs remote meetings for a bank — because in a cross-region bank, you will be.

PM 岗位第一轮面试，Microsoft Teams，摄像头开启。面试还有五分钟。视频会议里所有可能出错的事情，都在悄悄决定你是否看起来像能为银行主持远程会议的人——因为在跨区域银行里，你真的会。

The etiquette has two layers: setup you control before the call (camera height, light, background, audio, backup connection) and recovery scripts for when tech fails anyway — because in a bank interview, how you handle the freeze is itself a work sample.

礼仪分两层：通话前可控的准备（摄像头高度、光线、背景、音频、备用网络）和技术故障时的救场话术——因为在银行面试里，你处理卡顿的方式本身就是一份工作样本。

2. Dialogue 英文对话

You (Candidate): Five minutes out. Final checklist: laptop on books, camera at eye level — not up my nose. Window light in front of me, not behind. Phone on silent, door closed, and the backup plan is the phone app with headphones if the Wi-Fi drops.还有五分钟。最终清单：笔记本垫在书上，摄像头与视线平齐——别对着鼻孔。窗光在脸前方，不在背后。手机静音、关门，备用方案是手机 App 加耳机，以防 Wi-Fi 掉线。
You (Candidate): Background: bookshelf, one plant, nothing that talks before I do. Audio test: recording app for three seconds, playback — "clarity beats fidelity"; a cheap headset in the right place beats studio mic in the wrong one.背景：书架、一盆绿植、没有任何东西会抢在我发言前"说话"。音频测试：录音三秒回放——"清晰胜过保真"；摆对位置的头戴耳机胜过摆错位置的录音棚麦克风。
Sarah (Interviewer): Hi, can you see and hear me all right?你好，图像和声音都正常吗？
You (Candidate): Loud and clear on my side — how do I sound and look on yours?我这边非常清楚——你那边看我听我都正常吗？
Sarah (Interviewer): Perfect. Let\'s begin with — 很好。我们先从——
You (Candidate): — sorry, you froze for about three seconds there. Could you repeat the last part?——抱歉，你刚才卡了大约三秒。能重复一下后半句吗？
Sarah (Interviewer): Of course — I asked about your experience with regulatory projects.当然——我问的是你做监管项目的经验。
You (Candidate): Thank you. Also a housekeeping note while we\'re here: if my video freezes on your side, the fastest fix is I turn my camera off for a moment — my audio held fine last time my bandwidth dipped. Shout if it happens.谢谢。借这个机会说个约定：如果我这边画面卡住，最快的处理是我暂时关掉摄像头——上次带宽波动时音频一直正常。卡了就喊我。
Sarah (Interviewer): Good to know. And your question — how do you keep stakeholders engaged over video across three time zones?了解了。你的问题——跨三个时区你怎么用视频会议让利益相关方保持投入？
You (Candidate): Three habits from doing it badly, then better. One: rotate the painful slot so the same region is not always at 8 AM. Two: camera on for decisions, off for broadcasts — decisions need faces; broadcasts need recordings. Three: every meeting ends with decisions and owners typed in chat, because the note is the meeting, the call is just its delivery mechanism.从做砸到做好的三条习惯。一：轮流承担最难受的时段，别让同一区域永远早上 8 点开会。二：做决策开摄像头，做广播关摄像头——决策需要人脸；广播需要录像。三：每次会议以聊天区打出的"决定 + 责任人"收尾，因为纪要才是会议本身，通话只是它的投递方式。
Sarah (Interviewer): You practised what you preached even before I asked. Nice. Let\'s talk about a scenario…还没等我问你就已经在实践自己的方法论了。不错。我们来聊一个场景……

3. Key Phrases 关键句型

  • Setup Camera at eye level — not up my nose. 摄像头与视线平齐——别对着鼻孔。 Prep rules are facts, not fussiness: banks notice who can run a room they are not in.
  • Audio Clarity beats fidelity. 清晰胜过保真。 The one-line audio doctrine: the right cheap headset in the right place beats the wrong expensive mic.
  • Check How do I sound and look on yours? 你那边看我听我都正常吗？ Asking about THEIR experience of you — the inverse of the usual "can you hear me?" and twice as professional.
  • Recovery You froze for about three seconds — could you repeat the last part? 你卡了大约三秒——能重复一下后半句吗？ Specific diagnosis + specific request. No drama, no apology spiral.
  • Protocol If my video freezes, the fastest fix is I turn my camera off. 如果我画面卡住，最快的处理是我关掉摄像头。 Agreeing a fallback protocol in advance is remote-leadership behaviour — say it once, calmly.
  • Doctrine The note is the meeting; the call is just its delivery mechanism. 纪要才是会议，通话只是投递方式。 A portable remote-work aphorism — interviewers quote these to each other.

4. Vocabulary 词汇表

  • Eye level n. 视线平齐 Camera at the height of your eyes — the single biggest video upgrade.
  • Fidelity n. 保真度 How faithfully audio reproduces sound; clarity matters more.
  • Bandwidth dip n. 带宽波动 A temporary drop in connection quality — the freeze culprit.
  • Housekeeping n. 事务性约定 Small process agreements made upfront so meetings run smoothly.
  • Painful slot n. 难受时段 The meeting time that is someone's early morning or late night.
  • Broadcast n. 广播型会议 One-to-many information sharing — camera optional, recording essential.
  • Delivery mechanism n. 投递机制 The channel a thing arrives through; the artifact matters more.
  • Work sample n. 工作样本 Evidence of how you actually work — your freeze recovery is one.

5. Grammar Notes 语法点

① Imperatives as doctrine

"Rotate the painful slot.", "Camera on for decisions, off for broadcasts." — bare imperatives sound bossy in conversation but authoritative as doctrine, especially numbered: One… Two… Three… The number-plus-imperative frame says "this is a system I use", not "this is my mood today". Keep each rule short enough to be quoted.

"Rotate the painful slot."、"Camera on for decisions, off for broadcasts."——裸祈使句在闲聊中显得颐指气使，作为"准则"却显得权威，尤其是编号后：One… Two… Three…"编号 + 祈使句"表达的是"这是我在用的系统"，而非"我今天的心情"。每条要短到可以被引用。

② Em-dash definitions

"Decisions need faces; broadcasts need recordings." — the semicolon joins two parallel clauses into one doctrine. Same device: "The note is the meeting; the call is just its delivery mechanism." Parallel structure + semicolon is the grammar of maxims: balanced halves, quotable whole. Build two per interview.

"Decisions need faces; broadcasts need recordings."——分号把两个平行分句连成一条准则。同款："The note is the meeting; the call is just its delivery mechanism."平行结构 + 分号是格言的语法：两半对仗，整体可引。每次面试准备两条。

6. Cultural Tips 文化 / 职场 Tips

  • Camera at eye level changes everything. Books under the laptop: free, instant, and it makes you look like a colleague rather than a security camera.
  • Light in front, never behind. Face the window. A backlit face reads as "hiding something", even when you are not.
  • Do a 3-second audio test. Record and play back. You will hear what they hear — usually that you are too far from the mic.
  • Have the phone backup rehearsed. Know how to join from the app with headphones before you need to. Recovery speed is the skill.
  • Recover without drama. "You froze for three seconds — could you repeat the last part?" Two clauses. The calm is what is being graded.

7. Practice 练习题

Exercise 1 — Your five-minute checklist

Write your own pre-interview checklist: camera, light, background, audio test, backup connection. Six items, testable, no philosophy.

Compare with the article\'s list — the item most people miss is the backup connection rehearsal. A plan you have never joined from is not a plan.

Exercise 2 — Recovery script bank

Write recovery lines for: (a) your audio breaks, (b) their screen-share fails, (c) an echo makes you talk over each other twice.

(a) "My audio just dropped — I\'m switching to my phone, thirty seconds." (b) "Share seems stuck on your side — I can see slides two onwards, want me to recap one?" (c) "Let\'s do a hand rule — whoever isn\'t speaking mutes, two seconds each." Each names the problem and proposes the protocol.

Exercise 3 — Doctrine factory

Turn your own remote-work habits into two semicolon maxims, e.g. "Camera on for decisions; off for broadcasts."

Good maxims are learned from failure: the painful-slot rotation exists because one region burned out. Your best maxim is probably the habit you built after your worst month.
