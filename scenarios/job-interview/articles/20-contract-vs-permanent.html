Contract vs Permanent

Article 20 · Job Interview English · 合同工 vs 长期雇员

⏱ 7 min read 🟡 Intermediate 🎯 Answer the vendor question honestly: rate, renewal, boundary, and what you optimise for

1. Scenario 场景背景

You are a contractor interviewing for a role — and the interviewer has noticed: "You\'ve been contracting for three years. Why move to permanent now — and would you take a contract extension if we offered one instead?" Meanwhile, the reverse question comes up just as often: permanent employees being asked whether they would consider contracting. Both conversations are about what you optimise for.

你是合同工，正在面试一个岗位——面试官注意到了这一点："你做了三年合同工，为什么现在想转长期？如果我们给你合同延期呢？"反过来同样常见：长期雇员被问是否考虑过做合同工。两个对话的本质都是：你的最优解是什么。

The professional answer has three anchors: the honest optimisation function (what you maximise: rate, learning, stability, or scope), the boundary statement (what you will not trade), and the evidence (what your track record says about how you behave inside either model).

职业化回答有三个锚点：诚实的最优函数（你最大化什么：费率、学习、稳定还是空间）、边界声明（什么你不会拿来交换）、证据（你的履历说明你在两种模式里分别怎么表现）。

2. Dialogue 英文对话

Sarah (Hiring Manager): Your CV says three years contracting. Why permanent now — and if we offered a contract extension instead, would you take it?简历上写着三年合同工。为什么现在想转长期——如果我们给合同延期，你会接受吗？
You (Candidate): Two honest answers, and they connect. Why permanent: my optimisation function changed. Years one and two, I optimised for rate and variety — three industries in eighteen months taught me more than any single employer could. What I optimise for now is scope and consequence — owning a domain long enough to see my decisions age. Contracts rarely give you that.两个诚实的回答，而且相互关联。为什么转长期：我的最优函数变了。头两年我最大化的是费率和多样性——18 个月里三个行业，比任何单一雇主教得都多。现在我最大化的是空间和后果——长期拥有一个领域，看着自己的决定随时间发酵。合同很少给你这个。
You (Candidate): Would I take the extension? If it were offered first and permanent became possible later — yes, without drama; I have done two renewals and left both cleanly. But I would not want the extension to become the plan — my answer today is that I am structuring my next three years around ownership, and I would rather that be explicit on both sides.会接受延期吗？如果先有延期、长期后置——会，不带戏剧性；我续过两次约，两次都干净退场。但我不想让延期变成默认计划——我今天的回答是：我的下一个三年是围绕"拥有"来构建的，这一点我希望双方都明确。
Sarah (Hiring Manager): That is clear. Contractors sometimes get frozen out of the culture — team meetings, strategy days. How do you handle being on the outside of the permanent circle?很清楚。合同工有时会被排除在文化之外——团队会议、战略日。你怎么面对"长期圈子之外"的位置？
You (Candidate): By being precise about the boundary instead of resenting it. I attend what affects my delivery — standups, planning, incident reviews. I do not expect comp committees or org design offsites, and I do not ask. The boundary is real; the professionalism is choosing not to poll it. In my last engagement I ran the team\'s incident process for eleven months — the permanent engineers briefed me into everything that mattered. Inclusion follows delivery, not employment status.我的方式是把边界说清楚，而不是心怀怨气。影响我交付的我参加——站会、计划会、事故复盘。我不期待薪酬委员会或组织设计外出会，也不去问。边界是真实的；职业化在于选择不去试探它。上一个项目里我主持了团队 11 个月的事故流程——正式员工把所有重要的事都同步给了我。融入跟随交付，而不是雇佣身份。
Sarah (Hiring Manager): Last angle: if we hire you permanent and a hot market doubles contractor rates next year — will you be back on the market?最后一个角度：如果我们给你长期合同，明年市场火热、合同工费率翻倍——你会回到市场吗？
You (Candidate): You should ask that question — and the honest answer has the same shape as the first one: I priced the market for three years; I know exactly what it pays. I am choosing permanent at a known discount because scope is what I am buying. If I ever re-enter the market, you will hear it from me first, in a handover plan — not from a competitor\'s invoice.这个问题应该问——诚实的答案和第一个答案同构：我为市场定过三年价，我很清楚它付多少。我在已知折价的情况下选择长期，因为我买的是空间。如果有一天我重回市场，你会第一个从我这里听到——带着交接计划，而不是从竞争对手的账单上。
Sarah (Hiring Manager): That is the most honest answer to that question I have heard this year. Welcome aboard — pending the usual process.这是今年我对这个问题听到的最诚实的回答。欢迎加入——走完常规流程即可。

3. Key Phrases 关键句型

  • Function My optimisation function changed. 我的最优函数变了。 Borrowed from engineering, perfect for this question: name what you maximise, and when it changed.
  • Then/Now Years one and two, I optimised for rate and variety. Now, scope and consequence. 头两年我要费率和多样性；现在要空间和后果。 The then/now frame makes change sound like strategy, not drift.
  • Renewal I have done two renewals and left both cleanly. 我续过两次约，两次都干净退场。 Contractor evidence that answers the loyalty question better than any promise.
  • Boundary The boundary is real; the professionalism is choosing not to poll it. 边界是真实的；职业化在于选择不去试探它。 How contractors talk about culture exclusion without resentment — and how permanent staff should hear it.
  • Inclusion Inclusion follows delivery, not employment status. 融入跟随交付，而不是雇佣身份。 The maxim that reframes the whole contractor-culture question.
  • Guarantee You will hear it from me first, in a handover plan. 你会第一个从我这里听到——带着交接计划。 The only credible answer to "will you leave": not a promise of forever, a protocol of exit.

4. Vocabulary 词汇表

  • Contractor n. 合同工 Engaged for a fixed term via an agency or own company; paid for scope, not tenure.
  • Permanent n. 长期雇员 Direct employment with benefits, notice periods and tenure.
  • Rate n. 费率 The contractor's day rate — typically higher, with no paid leave.
  • Renewal / extension n. 续约/延期 Extending a contract term; the contractor's version of a performance review.
  • Optimisation function n. 最优函数 What you are maximising — rate, learning, stability, or scope.
  • Comp committee n. 薪酬委员会 Where permanent salaries are decided — firmly outside contractor scope.
  • Handover plan n. 交接计划 The documented exit — the professional's answer to leaving.
  • Notice period n. 通知期 Permanent employees give one; contractors give whatever the contract says.
  • Day rate n. 日费率 Contractor pricing unit — compare with permanent pay carefully, not naively.

5. Grammar Notes 语法点

① Past simple for the old function, present for the new

"Years one and two, I optimised for rate and variety." — past simple, closed chapter. "What I optimise for now is scope and consequence." — present simple with a what-cleft, putting the new function on stage. The tense system is the argument: change is real, the present is chosen. Career-direction answers live in this past-to-present grammar.

"Years one and two, I optimised for rate and variety."——过去时，已完结的章节。"What I optimise for now is scope and consequence."——现在时 + what 分裂句，把新的最优函数请上台。时态系统就是论点：改变是真的，现在是选择的。职业方向类回答都活在这种"过去到现在"的语法里。

② Protocols instead of promises

"You will hear it from me first, in a handover plan." — notice the grammar of the answer: not "I will never leave" (a promise about feelings) but a protocol sentence — who gets told, in what form, first. Protocols are checkable; promises are vibes. When an interviewer asks about loyalty, upgrade the answer from promise to protocol.

"You will hear it from me first, in a handover plan."——注意答案的语法：不是"我永远不会走"（关于情绪的承诺），而是一句协议式陈述——谁先被告知、以什么形式。协议可核验；承诺只是氛围。当面试官问忠诚度时，把答案从承诺升级为协议。

6. Cultural Tips 文化 / 职场 Tips

  • Name your optimisation function. Rate, variety, stability, scope — pick honestly and date the change. The then/now frame turns three years of contracting into a strategy.
  • Renewals are your loyalty evidence. Two clean renewals and two clean exits answer the "will you stay" question better than any vow.
  • Do not poll the boundary. Attend what affects delivery; skip what does not. Resentment at excluded meetings is a career tax you choose.
  • "Inclusion follows delivery" is the line to live by. It is also the line panels quote to each other.
  • Answer the leaving question with a protocol. A handover plan promised in advance is worth more than forever promised in the moment.

7. Practice 练习题

Exercise 1 — Your function, dated

Write your optimisation function — what you maximise now, what you maximised before, and the year it changed. Two sentences.

If the two functions are identical, that is an answer too — but say why it suits you: "I optimise for stability and depth; contracting was never my game." The error is drifting into an interview without knowing what you are maximising.

Exercise 2 — Boundary rehearsal

You are offered a contractor seat that excludes strategy days but includes incident command. Write your two-sentence boundary statement.

Model: "I attend everything that touches my delivery — including incident command, where I expect the same authority as anyone holding the pager. Strategy days are outside my scope and outside my interest." Authority attached to responsibility, boundaries stated without apology.

Exercise 3 — Protocol, not promise

Rewrite "I will definitely stay for three years" as a protocol sentence a hiring manager can actually rely on.

Model: "If either side misprices this in six months, you will hear it from me first — with a handover plan and a notice period, not a resignation email on a Friday." Honesty with structure beats enthusiasm with no exit plan.
