Welcome and Farewell Gatherings

Article 12 · Small Talk & Social English · 迎新与欢送会

⏱ 7 min read 🟢 Beginner-friendly 🎯 The two ritual speeches: welcome with a question, farewell with a debt named

1. Scenario 场景背景

Two rituals in one week: a farewell for Daniel B (moving to the London office) and a welcome for a new analyst on Monday. Both involve cake, both involve a short speech, and both speeches have a grammar that most people never learn — they improvise, and it shows.

一周之内有两个仪式：为 Daniel B 送行（调任伦敦办公室）和周一迎新分析师。都有蛋糕、都要讲几句，而且这两种发言都有各自多数人没学过的语法——大家即兴发挥，然后漏洞百出。

The welcome speech is a question handed to the newcomer — you are not summarising them; you are giving them a door into their first conversation. The farewell speech is a debt named — one specific thing they did for you, said in front of the room. Both take ninety seconds. Both get remembered for years.

迎新词是递给新人一个问题——你不是在总结他们，而是在给他们打开进入第一场对话的门。欢送词是点名一笔人情债——一件对方为你做过的具体的事，当着全屋人的面说出来。都只要 90 秒。都会被记住很多年。

2. Dialogue 英文对话

Priya (Delivery): Daniel B\'s do — a few words from the team? Wei, you worked closest with him recently — go on.Daniel B 的欢送会——团队里谁说两句？Wei，最近你和他合作最多——你来。
You (New Joiner): Ninety seconds, one debt. My second week, my dashboard query ran nine minutes and I was about to ship it anyway. Daniel walked over, looked at it for twenty seconds, and said "try the window function — and check the reactivation flag." Both halves of that sentence saved the project.90 秒，一笔债。我第二周时，仪表盘查询要跑 9 分钟，我差点就这么上线了。Daniel 走过来、看了 20 秒、说了句"试试窗口函数——再查一下再激活标志。"那句话的两半都救了这个项目。
Daniel B (Leaving): (laughing) I do not remember the fix — I remember the query being slow enough to make tea during.（笑）修复我不记得了——我只记得那个查询慢到够泡杯茶。
You (New Joiner): That is exactly the debt — you answered it before I finished typing the question. London is getting the person who answers questions mid-typing. We will manage the typing alone. Good luck — and leave the mug.这正是那笔债——我问题还没打完你就答了。伦敦要得到的是一个"边打字边答题"的人。我们会独自面对打字。祝好运——杯子留下。
Daniel B (Leaving): The mug stays. It has seen three CISOs — it outranks all of us.杯子留下。它见过三任 CISO——级别比我们都高。
(Monday — the welcome): You: The welcome speech, drafted: "Everyone — Mei joins the analysis team from our Shanghai office, where she rebuilt the reconciliation reporting single-handedly. Mei — question for you, and everyone will want the answer: what is the one thing Shanghai does that we should steal?"（周一——迎新）你：迎新词，起草："各位——Mei 从上海办公室加入分析团队，她在那边独立重建了对账报表。Mei——问你一个问题，大家都想知道答案：上海有什么最值得我们偷师？"
Mei (New Joiner): (to the room) Oh — good question. The Friday sign-off summary: one page, every number pre-agreed with finance before the meeting. It saved us roughly two hours of arguing per week — I will draft one for here by Friday.（对全场）哦——好问题。周五签批摘要：一页纸，每个数字会前都与财务预先对齐。它每周为我们省下大约两小时的争吵——周五前我为这里起草一份。
Elena (Tech Lead): Watch what just happened: the welcome speech made Mei a contributor before her first standup. The question did it — "what should we steal" assumes she arrives with value. Most welcome speeches assume she arrives with needs.看看刚才发生了什么：迎新词让 Mei 在第一次站会前就成了贡献者。是那个问题做到的——"该偷师什么"预设她带着价值而来。大多数迎新词预设她带着需求而来。

3. Key Phrases 关键句型

  • Debt Both halves of that sentence saved the project. 那句话的两半都救了这个项目。 The farewell debt: one specific moment, its exact words, its consequence. Vague warmth thanks no one.
  • Deflect I remember the query being slow enough to make tea during. 我只记得那个查询慢到够泡茶。 The humble deflection — meet it by restating the debt with the deadline detail only they would remember.
  • Steal What is the one thing Shanghai does that we should steal? 上海有什么最值得我们偷师？ The welcome question — assumes incoming value, hands the newcomer expertise as their opening move.
  • Contributor The welcome speech made Mei a contributor before her first standup. 迎新词让 Mei 在第一次站会前就成了贡献者。 Elena\'s yardstick for welcomes: the speech should create a contribution, not just an introduction.
  • Mug Leave the mug. 杯子留下。 The running joke as farewell close — shared lore in one final beat beats a paragraph of sentiment.

4. Vocabulary 词汇表

  • Farewell do n. 欢送会 The leaving party; speeches are short, debts are specific, cake is mandatory.
  • Debt n. 人情债 The specific help someone gave you — named publicly, it becomes their legacy.
  • Welcome speech n. 迎新词 Ninety seconds ending in a question the newcomer will enjoy answering.
  • Steal v. 偷师 To adopt another team's good practice — the friendliest word in cross-office transfer.
  • Lore n. 传说 Shared objects and jokes (the mug) — closing on one beats closing on sentiment.
  • Sign-off summary n. 签批摘要 Mei's steal-able practice: pre-agreed numbers on one page.

5. Grammar Notes 语法点

① The debt narrative arc

"My second week, my query ran nine minutes… Daniel walked over, looked for twenty seconds, and said —" — past continuous for the struggling state, past simple for the intervention, present for the consequence: "both halves of that sentence save projects to this day." The farewell debt is a three-tense story: stuck, helped, still paying.

"My second week, my query ran nine minutes… Daniel walked over, looked for twenty seconds, and said——"过去进行时铺陈困境，一般过去时给出手，现在时讲后果："那句话的两半至今还在拯救项目。"欢送词的债是三时态故事：受困、获助、持续受益。

② The question that assumes value

"What is the one thing Shanghai does that we should steal?" — the grammar is should-steal framing: the question presupposes the newcomer\'s origin is a source of value. Compare "how are you finding the adjustment?" — needs-framing. Welcome speeches hand over doors; value-framed questions hand over the good doors.

"What is the one thing Shanghai does that we should steal?"——语法在should-steal 框架：问题预设新人的出身是价值源头。对比"适应得怎么样？"——需求框架。迎新词递上门；价值框架的问题递上好门。

6. Cultural Tips 文化 / 职场 Tips

  • Farewell: one debt, ninety seconds. The exact words they said, the exact consequence it had. Daniel will remember your speech longer than the cake.
  • Welcome: end in a question. "What should we steal?" makes the newcomer a contributor before their first standup.
  • Meet deflections by re-sharpening the debt. "I only remember the slow query" — answer with the detail only they could have given.
  • Close on lore. "Leave the mug" — a shared joke in one beat is warmer than a paragraph of sentiment.
  • Watch Elena\'s yardstick. The welcome succeeded because it produced a contribution — the Friday sign-off summary was in the wiki by Friday.

7. Practice 练习题

Exercise 1 — Write your debt speech

Pick a colleague who helped you. Write the 90-second farewell: the stuck state, their exact intervention, the present-tense consequence.

The exact-words rule is what separates debts from greetings: quote the sentence they actually said. "Try the window function" is a memory; "he helped me a lot" is a placeholder.

Exercise 2 — The steal question

A new joiner arrives from another team next Monday. Write your welcome speech: one sentence of specific credit, one should-steal question.

The credit must come from real reading — their last project, their known strength. "She rebuilt the reconciliation reporting single-handedly" took one Slack search; it makes the steal question land as respect, not as quiz.

Exercise 3 — The lore close

Identify one piece of shared lore in your team (an object, a running joke). Write the one-line farewell close that uses it.

Lore closes work because they compress: "leave the mug" carries three years of history in three words. Your version should be equally load-bearing — if it needs explanation, it is not lore yet.
