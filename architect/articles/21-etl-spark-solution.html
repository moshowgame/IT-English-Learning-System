ETL Spark Solution Architecture

Article 21 · Module 6: Solution Proposals & Stakeholder Alignment · Architect English

⏱ 11 min read 🟡 Intermediate 🎯 Proposing a Spark ETL platform: cluster model, batch vs micro-batch, incremental loads, and a PoC with exit criteria

1. Scenario 场景背景

It is Tuesday, 2:00 PM HKT. You are a Solution Architect in the RBWM Data tribe. The nightly ETL that feeds the wealth-platform reporting mart runs on 40+ SQL stored procedures wrapped in shell scripts, orchestrated by Control-M, all on a single vertically-scaled database box. On an average night the batch takes four hours against a six-hour window. At month-end it takes nine — and last quarter it overran twice, which means the dashboards opened to yesterday's numbers.

周二下午 2 点香港时间，你是 RBWM 数据 tribe 的 Solution 架构师。给财富平台 报表集市供数的夜间 ETL，跑的是 40 多个 SQL 存储过程 + shell 脚本， 由 Control-M 编排，全部压在一台纵向扩容的数据库服务器上。平常一晚 要跑 4 小时，窗口是 6 小时；到了月末要跑 9 小时—— 上个季度已经超窗两次，意味着第二天早上打开报表，看到的还是前一天的数。

You have booked a solution-outline session. The audience is exactly right for a proposal of this size: Mei, the Tech Lead who owns the ETL code and will ask the hard technical questions, and Marcus, the Senior Manager who owns delivery and will ask the hard money questions. Your goal is not to get approval to build — your goal is to get approval to prove: a two-week proof of concept on one pipeline, with exit criteria agreed in the room.

你约了一场 方案概要会。听众配置正好符合这种体量的提案： Mei——负责 ETL 代码的 Tech Lead，会问最硬的技术问题； Marcus——管交付的 Senior Manager，会问最硬的钱的问题。 你今天的目标不是拿到"批准建设"，而是拿到"批准验证"： 在一条管道上做两周的 PoC，并且当场把退出标准谈定。

On the screen behind you: one page — current state on the left, proposed state on the right, seven rows, numbers in every row. No architecture diagram yet; diagrams come after the direction is agreed. And one discipline for the hour: every claim gets a number, every option gets a trade-off, and the ask fits in one sentence — a two-week PoC, one pipeline, three exit criteria.

你身后的屏幕上只有一页——左边现状、右边方案，7 行对比，每行都有数字。 先不放架构图；方向谈定之后再画图。这一小时的纪律是：每个论断带数字、 每个选项带取舍，而请求压缩成一句话——两周 PoC、一条管道、三条退出标准。

Remember the rule for solution proposals: never present one option. Present two or three, with a clear recommendation and the trade-off that drove it. An architect who arrives with a single option is a salesperson; an architect who arrives with options and a recommendation is an advisor.

记住方案提案的规则：永远不要只带一个选项。给出两三个选项，配上明确的 推荐和驱动这个推荐的取舍。只带一个选项来的架构师是销售；带着选项和推荐来的架构师才是顾问。

3. Dialogue 英文对话

Marcus (Senior Manager): Thanks for the slot, Wei. Before we get to the slides — give me the problem in plain English. What breaks, and when? Wei，谢你约时间。先别看幻灯片——用大白话把问题讲一遍。什么东西会坏，什么时候坏？
You (Architect): Plain English: the current pipeline is buckling under month-end volumes. Today it's 40-plus SQL procedures wrapped in shell scripts, all running sequentially on one vertically-scaled box. Average night, four hours against a six-hour window. Month-end, nine hours. Growth is 20 percent year on year, so the math says we miss the window every month within 18 months. 大白话是：现有管道在月末数据量下已经扛不住了。现在是 40 多个 SQL 存储过程套 shell 脚本，在一台纵向扩容的服务器上串行跑。平常一晚 4 小时，窗口 6 小时；月末 9 小时。数据量年增 20%，按这个数学推算，18 个月内我们每个月都会超窗。
Mei (Tech Lead): Before we buy a new platform — why not just tune the SQL? Some of those procedures were written in 2016. I can name three obvious full-table scans. 先别急着买新平台——为什么不直接优化 SQL？那批存储过程有些是 2016 年写的，我能报出三个明显的全表扫描。
You (Architect): We did. Last year's index-and-partition work bought us eighteen months, not a new ceiling — the bottleneck is the execution model, not the code. So the proposal: move the transformation layer to Spark. The model is driver-plus-executors — the driver coordinates the job, the executors do the work in parallel across a cluster, in memory where possible. Scale out, not up: month-end peaks are absorbed by adding executors, not by procuring hardware for eleven months. 优化过了。去年的索引和分区改造给我们买到的是 18 个月，不是新的天花板——瓶颈在执行模型，不在代码。所以提案是：把转换层迁到 Spark。它的模型是 driver + executor——driver 负责调度作业，executor 在集群上并行干活，能走内存就走内存。横向扩容而不是纵向扩容：月末峰值靠加 executor 吸收，而不是为了一年只用一个月的峰值去采购硬件。
Mei (Tech Lead): OK, the model makes sense. The real question is the processing pattern — do we go batch, micro-batch, or full streaming? Because I'm not retraining the team on streaming for a nightly mart. 模型我认。真正的问题是处理模式——走批量、微批，还是全流式？为了一个夜间集市让团队转流式，我不干。
You (Architect): Three rungs, and I recommend we start on the lowest one. Rung one — daily batch on Spark: same windows, same consumers, same schedule, just faster and horizontally scalable. Rung two — micro-batch via Structured Streaming, only if the business later wants hourly freshness. Rung three — true streaming, only where there's a genuine real-time use case. Don't buy streaming for its own sake. And the increment logic comes with it: partitioned by business date, loaded incrementally from the last watermark, each output partition written idempotently — overwrite by partition, never append. That means we rerun one bad date without touching history, and a failed run leaves no half-written data behind. 三档，我建议从最低一档起步。第一档——Spark 日批：窗口、消费者、调度都不变，只是更快、可横向扩展。第二档——微批，用 Structured Streaming，只有业务以后要小时级新鲜度才上。第三档——真流式，只用于真有实时需求的场景。不要为流式而流式。增量逻辑也一并定下：按业务日期分区，基于上一次成功的水位线做增量加载，输出分区按幂等方式写——按分区覆盖，绝不追加。这意味着重跑某一天不用碰历史数据，失败的作业也不会留下写了一半的数据。
Marcus (Senior Manager): Two questions from the money side. One — platform: I keep hearing Databricks and EMR in the same sentence; are they the same thing? Two — scheduling: does this mean ripping out Control-M? 钱这边两个问题。一——平台：Databricks 和 EMR 总被放在一起说，它们是一回事吗？二——调度：这是要把 Control-M 拆掉吗？
You (Architect): Not the same thing. Databricks is the managed platform — notebooks, cluster lifecycle, job orchestration, a higher platform premium. EMR is raw managed compute — cheaper per hour, more platform engineering on us. For the PoC I'm recommending Databricks for time-to-evidence; we revisit the trade-off at scale. And Control-M stays the scheduler of record — it submits the Spark jobs, it owns the alerting, and nobody re-learns scheduling in a migration. Airflow is a possible later step, not part of this proposal. 不是一回事。Databricks 是托管平台——notebook、集群生命周期、作业编排，平台溢价更高。EMR 是裸的托管算力——每小时更便宜，平台工程要我们自己扛。PoC 阶段我推荐 Databricks，优先拿到证据；规模化时再重新权衡这个取舍。至于调度，Control-M 仍然是唯一的调度权威——由它提交 Spark 作业、负责告警，迁移期间没人需要重新学一套调度。Airflow 是以后可能的演进，不在本提案里。
Mei (Tech Lead): One more from me — data quality. Today the reconciliation check is a SQL script someone runs manually at 7 AM. If Spark writes the mart, who certifies the numbers? 我再补一个——数据质量。现在对账校验是有人早上 7 点手工跑的 SQL。如果集市改由 Spark 写，谁来给数字背书？
You (Architect): The platform does, as a gate, not a hope. Every run ends with data quality checks before publish: row counts reconciled to source, null and duplicate checks on keys, and balance reconciliation against the previous day. Fail any check, the partition doesn't publish, Control-M raises the alert. Cost per run is the anchor metric for Marcus — elastic clusters bill only while jobs run, so I can put a pound figure on every pipeline. Which brings me to the ask: a two-week PoC on the single worst pipeline, with three exit criteria — same row counts to source, run time under sixty minutes, cost per run within budget. Hit all three, we scale the model; miss any one, we come back with evidence, not excuses. 由平台来做，而且是"闸门"，不是"祈祷"。每次作业结束、发布之前跑数据质量校验：行数与源端对账、主键空值与重复检查、余额与前一日对账。任何一项不过，该分区不发布，Control-M 告警。给 Marcus 的锚定指标是单次运行成本——弹性集群只在作业运行时计费，我可以给每条管道标出英镑数字。这就引出我的请求：在最痛的一条管道上做两周 PoC，三条退出标准——行数与源端一致、单次运行 60 分钟以内、单次成本在预算内。三条全中，我们就按这个模型铺开；任何一条不中，我们带证据回来，不带借口。
Marcus (Senior Manager): Good pitch. Bring me the PoC exit criteria in writing — one page, numbers not adjectives — and I'll sponsor it at Thursday's governance forum. Mei, from your side, what does two weeks cost the squad? 讲得不错。把 PoC 的退出标准写成文字给我——一页纸，用数字别用形容词——周四的治理评审会我去立项。Mei，从你那边看，两周要占用 squad 多少成本？
Mei (Tech Lead): One engineer half-time plus Wei's review hours. Acceptable — as long as the exit criteria are the ones we just agreed, not ones that appear later. 一名工程师半人力，加上 Wei 的评审时间。可以接受——前提是退出标准就用刚才谈定的这三条，不能事后又冒出新的。

4. Key Phrases 关键句型

  • CORE The current pipeline is buckling under month-end volumes. 现有管道在月末数据量下已经扛不住了 "Buckle under" = bend under weight. Opens a proposal with a picture, not a platform name. 注意 buckling 是"被压弯"的画面感，比 "has performance issues" 有力得多。
  • CORE Tuning bought us eighteen months, not a new ceiling. 优化买到的是 18 个月，不是新的天花板 Pre-empts the "why not optimise first?" question by crediting the past work while naming the structural limit. 先肯定过去的优化，再点明结构性上限，防止"为什么不继续优化"的质疑。
  • CORE Driver coordinates, executors do the work — scale out, not up. driver 负责调度、executor 并行干活——横向扩展而非纵向扩展 The whole Spark pitch in one breath. "Scale out" = add machines; "scale up" = buy a bigger machine. 一口气讲完 Spark 卖点：scale out 加机器，scale up 换大机器。
  • CORE Partitioned by business date, loaded incrementally from the last watermark. 按业务日期分区，基于上次水位线做增量加载 The standard incremental-ETL sentence. Watermark = the last successfully processed point. 增量 ETL 的标准句式：watermark 指上次成功处理到的位点。
  • CASUAL Don't buy streaming for its own sake. 不要为了流式而流式 "For its own sake" = without a real reason. The anti-hype sentence that makes engineers trust you. "For its own sake" 指没有真实理由地追时髦，这句话能让工程师信任你。
  • FORMAL Control-M stays the scheduler of record. Control-M 仍然是唯一的调度权威 "System of record / scheduler of record" = the single authoritative system. Removes the "do we migrate scheduling too?" scope creep in one sentence. "Scheduler of record" 表示唯一权威系统，一句话挡掉"调度也要迁移吗"的范围蔓延。
  • CORE Cost per run is the anchor metric. 单次运行成本是锚定指标 "Anchor metric" = the one number the decision hangs on. Managers decide on money; give them one number, not architecture diagrams. "Anchor metric" 指决策所依赖的核心数字。管理者看钱说话：给一个数字，别给架构图。
  • FORMAL Data quality checks act as a gate before publish — not a hope. 数据质量校验是发布前的闸门，而不是祈祷 "Gate, not a hope" contrasts enforced controls with wishful thinking. Strong vocabulary for any control discussion. "Gate, not a hope" 对比"强制控制"与"一厢情愿"，任何控制项讨论里都好用。
  • FORMAL A two-week PoC on the single worst pipeline, with three exit criteria. 在最痛的一条管道上做两周 PoC，配三条退出标准 "Exit criteria" = pre-agreed conditions to declare the PoC a success or failure. Ask small, measure hard, scale on evidence. "Exit criteria" 指事先约定的成败判定条件。请求要小、度量要严、用证据决定推广。
  • FORMAL Hit all three, we scale the model; miss any one, we come back with evidence, not excuses. 三条全中就铺开，任何一条不中就带证据回来、不带借口 The symmetrical close. It shows confidence and pre-commits you to honesty — sponsors remember this line. 对仗式收尾，既显自信，又预先承诺了诚实——赞助人会记住这句话。

5. Vocabulary 词汇表

  • driver n. 驱动节点 The Spark process that coordinates a job — builds the execution plan, schedules tasks, collects results. Never runs the heavy data work itself.
  • executor n. 执行节点 The worker process in a Spark cluster that runs tasks on its slice of the data and caches blocks in memory. Adding executors is how a Spark job scales out.
  • partition n. 分区 The unit of data and of parallelism — one logical slice of a dataset (commonly one business date) that a job reads, transforms, or overwrites independently.
  • micro-batch n. 微批 A processing pattern that treats a stream as a sequence of small batches (e.g., every 10 minutes). Spark Structured Streaming's default model — between classic batch and true streaming.
  • incremental load n. 增量加载 Loading only the data that arrived or changed since the last successful run, instead of reprocessing full history every night.
  • watermark n. 水位线 The last successfully processed point (e.g., a business date or offset) that marks where the next incremental run resumes.
  • idempotent adj. 幂等的 Running the same job twice produces the same result — e.g., overwriting a partition rather than appending to it. The property that makes reruns safe.
  • Databricks n. Databricks 数据平台 A managed Spark platform (notebooks, cluster lifecycle, job orchestration). Higher platform premium, lower operational burden.
  • EMR (Elastic MapReduce) n. AWS EMR 托管集群 AWS's managed Hadoop/Spark compute service. Cheaper raw compute, more platform engineering owned by you.
  • scheduler of record n. 唯一调度权威 The single system that owns what runs, when, and the alerting when it fails — here, Control-M. One migration should move one layer at a time.
  • proof of concept (PoC) n. 概念验证 A small, time-boxed trial on real data with pre-agreed exit criteria, run to produce evidence before committing to a full build.
  • data quality check n. 数据质量校验 Automated validations that gate publication: row counts vs source, null/duplicate key checks, balance reconciliation vs the previous day.

6. Grammar Notes 语法点

① "X bought us eighteen months" — credit the past, name the limit

When proposing a new platform, you will be asked "why not optimise what we have?". The architect's answer pattern is credit + limit: tuning bought us eighteen months, not a new ceiling. "Bought us + duration" acknowledges the past investment as real value; "not a + noun" states what it could never deliver. One sentence, no blame, no defensiveness. The same pattern works for any past fix: caching bought us a quarter, not a growth plan.

提案必然会被问"为什么不继续优化"。架构师的句式是先肯定 + 再点限：优化买到了 18 个月，但没有买到新天花板。"bought us + 时长"承认历史投入的价值，"not a + 名词"点明它永远给不了什么。一句话，不指责、不防御。这个句式适用于任何历史改造：缓存买到了一个季度，但买不到增长方案。

② "Rung one / two / three" — staged options

Present processing-pattern choices as rungs of a ladder: rung one — daily batch; rung two — micro-batch; rung three — true streaming. A ladder implies a migration path: you climb only when the business need appears. It also justifies starting small — nobody climbs to rung three first. Compare with layer one / two / three from Article 20: layers stack spatially, rungs progress temporally.

用"梯子的横档"呈现处理模式选项：日批、微批、流式。梯子暗示演进路径：业务需要出现时才往上爬，也没人会先爬第三档。对比第 20 篇的 layer（空间上叠加），rung 是时间上递进。

③ Exit criteria with "under / within / same"

PoC exit criteria are stated as three short measurable clauses: same row counts to source, run time under sixty minutes, cost per run within budget. Notice the pattern: same + noun (equality), under + number (ceiling), within + budget (envelope). Three clauses, each verifiable by a script — never "significantly faster" or "much cheaper". Add the symmetrical close and the criteria become a contract: hit all three, we scale; miss any one, we stop.

PoC 退出标准写成三个短小可测的条款：行数与源端一致（same）、运行时长低于 60 分钟（under）、成本在预算内（within）。每条都能用脚本验证——绝不说"明显更快""便宜很多"。再加上对仗式收尾，标准就成了契约：三条全中就铺开，任何一条不中就停。

7. Cultural Tips 文化 / 职场 Tips

🎯 Presenting Options at HSBC — the 2+1 Rule
  • Never present one option. Bring two or three (batch / micro-batch / streaming; Databricks / EMR), each with a one-line trade-off, plus your recommendation. A single option reads as a sales pitch; options plus a recommendation read as engineering judgement.
  • Separate the two audiences. Mei (Tech Lead) audits the model — driver/executors, partitioning, idempotency. Marcus (Senior Manager) audits the money — cost per run, platform premium, what Control-M keeps doing. Answer each in their language, in that order.
  • Anchor on one number. "Cost per run" beats ten slides. Elastic compute lets you price a single pipeline — put a pound figure on it and the conversation becomes concrete.
  • Show the current state before the future state. The seven-row comparison table does the persuading for you: every row pairs a pain with a mechanism. People approve migrations they can see row by row.
⚠️ The Oversell Trap — "Spark Fixes Everything"
  • ❌ "Spark will make everything faster and cheaper." — an unfalsifiable promise that owns you at the first overrun.
  • ✅ "A two-week PoC on the worst pipeline, three exit criteria, evidence decides." — falsifiable, cheap, and it protects your credibility.
  • Never propose a platform migration without a PoC gate. If the PoC fails on the best-understood pipeline, the migration is dead — better to learn that in two weeks than in two quarters.
  • Watch the skew trap. If asked "will Spark fix our month-end?", the honest answer is "it moves the ceiling; skew and the upstream extract still need separate work". Architects who claim a platform solves upstream problems lose the room at the first load test.
✅ Get the Exit Criteria Agreed in the Room
  • Exit criteria agreed verbally are exit criteria negotiable later. Mei said it best: "as long as the exit criteria are the ones we just agreed, not ones that appear later." Write them down before the meeting ends — one page, numbers not adjectives.
  • The symmetrical close wins sponsorship. "Hit all three, we scale; miss any one, we come back with evidence, not excuses." It pre-commits you to honesty and makes it easy for a Senior Manager to say yes.
  • Keep the scheduler of record unchanged in phase one. Migrations fail on blast radius, not on technology. "Control-M stays" tells Marcus the change is contained to one layer.
  • Let the Tech Lead price the team cost. Marcus asked Mei, not you, what two weeks costs the squad. The architect proposes; the Tech Lead owns the delivery cost. Respecting that boundary makes both yeses easier.

Mei（技术负责人）：模型我认。真正的问题是处理模式——走批量、微批，还是全流式？为了一个夜间集市让团队转流式，我不干。

你（架构师）：三档，我建议从最低一档起步。第一档——Spark 日批：窗口、消费者、调度都不变，只是更快、可横向扩展。第二档——微批，用 Structured Streaming，只有业务以后要小时级新鲜度才上。第三档——真流式，只用于真有实时需求的场景。不要为流式而流式。增量逻辑也一并定下：按业务日期分区，基于上一次成功的水位线做增量加载，输出分区按幂等方式写——按分区覆盖，绝不追加。这意味着重跑某一天不用碰历史数据，失败的作业也不会留下写了一半的数据。

Marcus（高级经理）：钱这边两个问题。一——平台：Databricks 和 EMR 总被放在一起说，它们是一回事吗？二——调度：这是要把 Control-M 拆掉吗？

你（架构师）：不是一回事。Databricks 是托管平台——notebook、集群生命周期、作业编排，平台溢价更高。EMR 是裸的托管算力——每小时更便宜，平台工程要我们自己扛。PoC 阶段我推荐 Databricks，优先拿到证据；规模化时再重新权衡这个取舍。至于调度，Control-M 仍然是唯一的调度权威——由它提交 Spark 作业、负责告警，迁移期间没人需要重新学一套调度。Airflow 是以后可能的演进，不在本提案里。

Mei（技术负责人）：我再补一个——数据质量。现在对账校验是有人早上 7 点手工跑的 SQL。如果集市改由 Spark 写，谁来给数字背书？

你（架构师）：由平台来做，而且是"闸门"，不是"祈祷"。每次作业结束、发布之前跑数据质量校验：行数与源端对账、主键空值与重复检查、余额与前一日对账。任何一项不过，该分区不发布，Control-M 告警。给 Marcus 的锚定指标是单次运行成本——弹性集群只在作业运行时计费，我可以给每条管道标出英镑数字。这就引出我的请求：在最痛的一条管道上做两周 PoC，三条退出标准——行数与源端一致、单次运行 60 分钟以内、单次成本在预算内。三条全中，我们就按这个模型铺开；任何一条不中，我们带证据回来，不带借口。

Marcus（高级经理）：讲得不错。把 PoC 的退出标准写成文字给我——一页纸，用数字别用形容词——周四的治理评审会我去立项。Mei，从你那边看，两周要占用 squad 多少成本？

Mei（技术负责人）：一名工程师半人力，加上 Wei 的评审时间。可以接受——前提是退出标准就用刚才谈定的这三条，不能事后又冒出新的。

"现有管道在月末数据量下已经扛不住了——窗口 6 小时，现在要跑 9 小时，数据量还在年增 20%。 我推荐用 Spark 做转换层：driver 负责调度，executor 并行干活，横向扩展而非纵向扩展。 按业务日期分区，基于上次水位线做增量加载，每个分区按幂等方式覆盖写入。 Control-M 继续作为唯一调度权威，数据质量校验作为发布前的闸门。 我的请求是：在最痛的一条管道上做两周 PoC——行数与源端一致、单次运行 60 分钟以内、单次成本在预算内。"
Q2. Spot the architect mistake — which line in this PoC plan is wrong?
  1. "The PoC pipeline will be the month-end wealth positions pipeline — our worst performer."
  2. "Each output partition will be written idempotently — overwrite by business date, never append."
  3. "For maximum future-proofing, the PoC will use Spark Structured Streaming with a 10-minute trigger."
  4. "Control-M will remain the scheduler of record and submit the Spark jobs."
③ is the mistake. A nightly reporting mart has no real-time use case — introducing Structured Streaming in a two-week PoC adds retraining cost, new failure modes, and no business value. This is "buying streaming for its own sake". The correct pattern is rung one: daily batch Spark with the same windows and consumers. ① is right — test on the worst pipeline so the evidence is strongest. ② is right — idempotent partition overwrite makes reruns safe. ④ is right — keep the scheduler of record unchanged to contain the blast radius.

Write your 60-second closing pitch in English, using at least 3 patterns from the Key Phrases section.
