Cross-System Performance Assessment

Article 23 · Module 6: Solution Proposals & Stakeholder Alignment · Architect English

⏱ 12 min read 🔴 Advanced 🎯 Five hops, throughput per hop, month-end capacity maths, finding the bottleneck, and an SLA with headroom

1. Scenario 场景背景

It is Wednesday, 4:00 PM HKT. You are a Solution Architect in the RBWM Data tribe. Tomorrow you present the wealth reporting pipeline to the sponsor, and the sponsor has asked for one number: "when is the mart ready?". Before you answer a single-number question, you need an end-to-end performance assessment across every system the data touches — because "mart ready" is the sum of five hops, not one.

周三下午 4 点香港时间，你是 RBWM 数据 tribe 的 Solution 架构师。明天你要向赞助人 汇报财富报表管道，对方只想要一个数字："集市几点就绪？"。在回答这个"一个数字" 的问题之前，你需要先做一份覆盖数据所经每个系统的端到端性能评估——因为 "集市就绪"是 5 段链路之和，不是一段。

The flow, top to bottom: upstream — the nightly extract from HUB (core banking) plus the GLCM payments feed; middle — your ETL into the reporting mart; downstream — three consumers: the QlikSense dashboards (Article 22's estate), a regulatory report, and a downstream finance system refresh. The session today is a working walkthrough with Tom, the Tech Lead who owns the pipeline code, to pin down where the time actually goes and what we can honestly commit to.

数据流从上到下：上游——HUB（核心银行）的夜间抽取， 加上 GLCM 支付数据源；中游——你们的 ETL 写入报表集市； 下游——三个消费者：QlikSense 报表（第 22 篇的那套体系）、监管报送报表， 以及下游财务系统的刷新。今天这场会是和负责管道代码的 Tom（Tech Lead） 做一次工作级走查：搞清时间到底花在哪、我们能诚实承诺什么。

Two of the five hops belong to other teams, which is exactly why this assessment is called cross-system: your run time is hostage to the upstream extract rate and to the downstream index rebuild, and neither is yours to fix alone. The assessment has to be written so that those teams can see themselves in it — same table, same timestamps, their name against their hop — or the fixes will never be scheduled.

5 段链路里有两段属于别的团队——这正是它叫跨系统评估的原因：你的运行时长被上游 抽取速率和下游索引重建"扣为人质"，而这两样都不归你单独修。所以评估报告必须写成 那些团队能在其中"看见自己"的样子——同一张表、同一批时间戳、他们的名字对着他们那一段—— 否则修复永远不会被排上日程。

The discipline for the next hour: every claim gets a number, every number gets a measurement, and every bottleneck gets an owner. No "the extract is slow" — instead "the extract is 40 minutes on average days, 2 hours 10 at month-end, and the constraint is archive-log contention in the source database, which is the HUB team's to fix".

接下来一小时的纪律：每个论断带数字，每个数字带测量方式，每个瓶颈带 owner。 不说"抽取很慢"——要说"抽取平常 40 分钟，月末 2 小时 10 分，约束是源库的归档日志竞争， 归 HUB 团队修"。

2. The Five-Hop Table 五段链路时间表

The single table tomorrow's meeting is built on: current measurements per hop, average day versus month-end, and the target each hop will be held to.

明天会议所依据的那张表：逐段链路的实测时长，平常日对比月末，以及每段要达成的目标。

Hop 链路段 Owner 负责方 Average day 平常日 Month-end 月末 Target 目标
1 · Extract (HUB + GLCM) HUB team 40 min 2 h 10 ≤ 60 min via partitioned extract
2 · Transfer & landing Data platform 10 min 20 min ≤ 15 min; APM-traced
3 · Transform (ETL) Data platform 95 min 4 h+ Scales out; not the constraint
4 · Load to mart Data platform 25 min 35 min ≤ 30 min
5 · Downstream refresh BI / Finance / Regulatory 60 min 90 min Rebuild after finance feed (−25 min)
End to end 端到端 — 3 h 50 (window 4 h 30) ~8 h (window unchanged) 04:30 / 06:30 with 20% headroom

3. Dialogue 英文对话

Tom (Tech Lead): Before we open the dashboards — what exactly are we assessing? The sponsor asked one question: "when is the mart ready?" Are we answering that, or the whole chain? 先别打开报表——我们到底在评估什么？赞助人只问了一句："集市几点就绪？"我们是回答那一句，还是评估整条链？
You (Architect): The whole chain, because "mart ready" is the sum of five hops, not one. Hop one, the HUB extract; hop two, file landing and transfer to our platform; hop three, our transform; hop four, the load into the mart; hop five, the downstream refresh — QlikSense, the regulatory report, and the finance feed. Each hop gets its own timestamp and its own target. If we only measure the end, we can never say why a run was late — and "why" is the only question worth answering. 整条链。因为"集市就绪"是 5 段链路之和，不是一段。第一段，HUB 抽取；第二段，文件落地与传输到我们平台；第三段，我们的转换；第四段，加载进集市；第五段，下游刷新——QlikSense、监管报送、财务供数。每段链路都要有自己的时间戳和自己的目标。只测终点，就永远说不清"为什么晚了"——而"为什么"是唯一值得回答的问题。
Tom (Tech Lead): Fine. So where does the time actually go? Give me the current numbers, average day first. 行。时间实际花在哪？先给平常日的数字。
You (Architect): Average day: extract 40 minutes, transfer 10, transform 95, load 25, downstream refresh 60 — just under four hours against a four-and-a-half-hour window. Tight but legal. Month-end is the problem: extract blows out to 2 hours 10, transform to over 4 hours, downstream to 90 minutes — pushing eight hours, and the window doesn't move. The window isn't the problem; the month-end multiple is. 平常日：抽取 40 分钟、传输 10、转换 95、加载 25、下游刷新 60——不到 4 小时，窗口是 4 小时半。紧但合法。月末才是问题：抽取涨到 2 小时 10 分，转换超 4 小时，下游 90 分钟——逼近 8 小时，而窗口纹丝不动。窗口不是问题；月末的倍数才是。
Tom (Tech Lead): So which hop is the bottleneck? My instinct says our transform — it's the one we own. 哪一段是瓶颈？我直觉是我们的转换——那是我们自己负责的一段。
You (Architect): The evidence says two bottlenecks, and neither is our transform. Upstream: the HUB extract tops out around 800 thousand rows an hour — the constraint is archive-log contention in the source database, so the extract is the HUB team's code on the HUB team's infrastructure. Downstream: the mart's index rebuild takes 40 minutes regardless of volume — it's serial, single-threaded, and it delays every downstream consumer that waits on it. The middle hops — our transform and load — scale fine. The bottleneck is upstream and downstream; here's the evidence. 证据指向两个瓶颈，都不在我们的转换。上游：HUB 抽取顶到每小时约 80 万行——约束是源库的归档日志竞争，这段代码和基础设施都归 HUB 团队。下游：集市的索引重建无论数据量多大都要 40 分钟——串行、单线程，还挡住所有等它的下游消费者。中间两段——我们的转换和加载——扩得动。瓶颈在上游和下游，证据在这里。
Tom (Tech Lead): OK, but the sponsor will want the capacity maths — "we fixed it last year" is not an answer this year. What are the numbers? 但赞助人要看容量数学——"去年修过"在今年不是答案。数字是多少？
You (Architect): Throughput is rows divided by seconds, per hop. Volume: 45 million records on an average day, 220 million at month-end — a five-times peak. Growth is 20 percent year on year, so in 18 months the month-end peak is around 320 million, and at the current extract rate the extract alone misses the window. One table, five hops, average versus peak, now versus eighteen months. That's the slide — and it converts "it feels slow" into a forecast with a date on it. 吞吐量 = 每段链路的行数除以秒数。数据量：平常日 4500 万条，月末 2.2 亿——5 倍峰值。年增 20%，18 个月后月末峰值约 3.2 亿，按当前抽取速率，光是抽取就会超窗。一张表、5 段链路、均值对比峰值、现在对比 18 个月后。这就是那一页幻灯片——它把"感觉变慢了"变成一个带日期的预测。
Tom (Tech Lead): And how do we prove the numbers before we put them in front of Marcus? Because the HUB team will dispute the contention claim. 那在拿给 Marcus 之前，我们怎么证明这些数字？HUB 团队肯定会质疑"归档日志竞争"这个说法。
You (Architect): Three instruments, in order of authority. Timestamp instrumentation at every hop boundary — we're 70 percent there; finish the other two hops this week. APM on the transfer service for the hop-two traces. And a load test replaying last month-end at one-times and one-and-a-half-times volume — that's the one that settles arguments. No SLA is signed without a load test behind it. 三件测量工具，按权威度排序。每段链路边界打时间戳插桩——已经完成 70%，本周补齐剩下两段。传输服务上上 APM，拿第二段的调用链。再用负载测试回放上个月末的数据，按 1 倍和 1.5 倍量——这才是终结争论的那个。没有负载测试背书的 SLA 一律不签。
Tom (Tech Lead): Then the commitment question. Marcus originally asked for "mart ready by 03:00" — what do we actually commit to, and can I defend it in the room? 那就是承诺问题。Marcus 最初要的是"集市 3 点就绪"——我们到底承诺什么，而且这个数我能在会议室里守住吗？
You (Architect): Not 03:00 — that number has zero headroom against the window, and headroom is what absorbs the upstream extract running long. We commit to 04:30 on average days and 06:30 at month-end, with 20 percent headroom — and we say why in the same breath: the number they asked for was never measured, the number we offer is. Then we bring an owner ask per bottleneck: the HUB team to parallelise the extract partitions — worth about 40 percent off the extract — and us to move the index rebuild to after the finance feed — worth 25 minutes. We commit to the number only after the load test passes, and if the fixes land, the number improves next quarter. That's an SLA we can hold for a year, not a press release. 不承诺 3 点——那个数字对窗口零余量，而余量才是吸收上游抽取超时的东西。我们承诺平常日 4:30、月末 6:30，留 20% 余量——并且同一句话里说清为什么：他们要的数字从没被测量过，我们给的数字是被测过的。然后每个瓶颈带一个 owner 请求：请 HUB 团队把抽取分区并行化——抽取大约省 40%；我们把索引重建挪到财务供数之后——省 25 分钟。承诺数字只在负载测试通过之后给出，修复落地后下季度数字还会变好。这是一个能守一年的 SLA，不是一页新闻稿。

4. Key Phrases 关键句型

  • CORE "Mart ready" is the sum of five hops, not one. "集市就绪"是 5 段链路之和，不是一段 The sentence that reframes a one-number question into a chain assessment. Say it before showing any data. 把"一个数字的问题"重构为"链路评估"的那句话——放在展示任何数据之前说。
  • FORMAL Each hop gets its own timestamp and its own target. 每段链路有自己的时间戳和自己的目标 "Per-hop targets" is what turns monitoring into diagnosis — you can name the hop that moved, not just the night that failed. "逐段目标"让监控变成诊断：能指出哪一段变了，而不只是哪一晚失败了。
  • CORE The window isn't the problem — the month-end multiple is. 窗口不是问题——月末的倍数才是 "Multiple" = the peak-to-average ratio. Naming the multiple (5x) turns a vague "it's slow sometimes" into a capacity planning input. "Multiple" 指峰值与均值之比。点名倍数（5 倍）把"有时会慢"变成容量规划的输入。
  • CORE Throughput is rows divided by seconds, per hop. 吞吐量 = 每段链路的行数除以秒数 The performance-maths definition. Keep it this blunt in stakeholder decks — everyone can repeat it, so everyone can check it. 性能数学的定义句。在干系人材料里保持如此直白——人人能复述，所以人人能检验。
  • FORMAL The bottleneck is upstream and downstream — and here's the evidence. 瓶颈在上游和下游——证据在这里 Always pair an inconvenient finding with "here's the evidence" in the same sentence. It converts defensiveness into shared problem-solving. 不受欢迎的结论要和"证据在这里"出现在同一句里——把防御性对话变成共同解题。
  • FORMAL No SLA is signed without a load test behind it. 没有负载测试背书的 SLA 一律不签 "Behind it" = as backing, as proof. The gate sentence for any commitment conversation — it applies to latency promises as much as to throughput ones. "Behind it" 指作为背书、证据。任何承诺谈判的闸门句——对延迟承诺和吞吐承诺同样适用。
  • CORE Commit to the number with 20 percent headroom, not the number they asked for. 承诺带 20% 余量的数字，而不是对方要的数字 "Headroom" = the margin that absorbs variance. A committed number without headroom is a scheduled breach, not an SLA. "Headroom" 指吸收波动的余量。没有余量的承诺数字是排期好的违约，不是 SLA。
  • FORMAL One table, five hops, average versus peak, now versus eighteen months. 一张表、5 段链路、均值对比峰值、现在对比 18 个月后 The four-contrast slide in one sentence. Stakeholders remember the rhythm even when they forget the rows. 一句话说完四组对比的幻灯片。干系人即使忘了行数据，也会记住这个节奏。
  • CORE Every bottleneck gets an owner ask. 每个瓶颈配一个明确的 owner 请求 "Owner ask" = the specific request to the team who can fix it ("the HUB team to parallelise the extract partitions"). Turns an assessment into an action plan. "Owner ask" 指向能修它的团队提出的具体请求（如"请 HUB 团队并行化抽取分区"）。把评估变成行动计划。
  • CASUAL That's an SLA we can hold for a year, not a press release. 这是一个能守一年的 SLA，不是一页新闻稿 "Press release" = an announcement written for applause instead of for operations. Use it to close a commitment conversation with self-aware humour. "Press release" 指为掌声而写、不为运维而写的公告。用这句话带着自知之明的幽默收尾承诺谈判。

5. Vocabulary 词汇表

  • throughput n. 吞吐量 Work completed per unit of time — rows per second per hop. The first number in any batch performance conversation.
  • latency n. 延迟 The time a single item (or a hop) takes end to end. In batch pipelines, latency per hop; in APIs, response time percentiles.
  • bottleneck n. 瓶颈 The hop that constrains the whole chain — throughput of the pipeline can never exceed throughput of the bottleneck. Often the upstream extract, not your code.
  • SLA (service level agreement) n. 服务等级协议 The measured commitment — a completion time, an availability, a freshness — with a measurement method and an owner. Unmeasured SLAs are wishes.
  • headroom n. 余量 The margin between the committed number and the observed worst case — what absorbs the upstream extract running long. 20 percent is a defensible default.
  • capacity planning n. 容量规划 Forecasting when volume growth breaks the current design — average vs peak, now vs 18 months — so the fix is scheduled before the breach, not after it.
  • month-end peak n. 月末峰值 The recurring maximum load (here 5x average). Batch designs are sized for the peak, not the average — the average is what runs on the quiet nights.
  • instrumentation n. 插桩 / 埋点 Timestamps written at every hop boundary so a run can be reconstructed after the fact. The cheapest, most decisive performance tool.
  • APM (application performance monitoring) n. 应用性能监控 Tooling that traces requests across services (e.g., Dynatrace, AppDynamics) — the evidence source for hop-two style claims.
  • load test n. 负载测试 Replaying realistic peak volumes (here, last month-end at 1x and 1.5x) against the pipeline to prove the numbers before anyone signs them.
  • critical path n. 关键路径 The sequence of hops that determines total run time. Speeding up a hop that isn't on it changes nothing — the classic wasted-optimisation trap.
  • index rebuild n. 索引重建 The serial maintenance step after loading a mart. Frequently the hidden downstream bottleneck — volume-independent and single-threaded.

6. Grammar Notes 语法点

① "X versus Y" — the four-contrast rhythm

The stakeholder slide is one sentence with four contrasts: one table, five hops, average versus peak, now versus eighteen months. "Versus" (often abbreviated vs or v) is stronger than "and" because it frames a comparison the audience must resolve. Build your performance decks on pairs: average vs peak, current vs forecast, measured vs assumed.

干系人幻灯片浓缩为一句话四个对比："均值对比峰值""现在对比 18 个月后"。"Versus"（简写 vs/v）比 "and" 有力，因为它框定了一个听众必须回答的比较。性能材料全部用成对对比搭建：均值 vs 峰值、现状 vs 预测、测过 vs 拍脑袋。

② "Regardless of volume" — the volume-independent claim

The index rebuild takes 40 minutes regardless of volume uses "regardless of + noun" to state that a variable you would normally expect to matter does not. The pattern is diagnostic gold: it separates fixed costs from variable costs in one phrase. Compare irrespective of volume (more formal) and no matter how big the volume is (more spoken).

"索引重建无论数据量多大都要 40 分钟"用"regardless of + 名词"表明一个通常应当起作用的变量并不起作用。这是诊断利器：一个短语就区分了固定成本和可变成本。更正式可用 irrespective of volume，更口语可用 no matter how big the volume is。

③ "The number they asked for was never measured, the number we offer is."

This is a parallel clause pair with ellipsis — the second clause drops the repeated verb phrase ("was measured"). The rhythm gives the sentence its punch, and the ellipsis gives it its speed. Use the pattern when contrasting an assumed number with a measured one: the estimate came from a vendor demo; the estimate we bring comes from a load test.

这是"平行分句 + 省略"结构——后半句省略了重复的动词短语（was measured）。节奏赋予句子冲击力，省略赋予它速度。对比"拍脑袋数字"与"测量数字"时可用此句式：对方报的数来自厂商演示，我们报的数来自负载测试。

7. Cultural Tips 文化 / 职场 Tips

🔢 Numbers Anchor Expectations — the HSBC Way
  • Never say "it should be fine by 3 AM." Say the table: five hops, measured times, average vs peak. British banking culture trusts a measured number with a method over a confident adjective — "measured beats assured".
  • Convert volume into dates. "Growth is 20 percent year on year, so month-end misses the window in about 18 months" is a forecast with a deadline — it gets funded; "the system feels slower lately" does not.
  • Keep the maths on one slide. Throughput, volume, multiple, growth, forecast. If the capacity argument needs three slides, it isn't understood well enough to defend.
  • Write the table so other teams see themselves in it. Owner column included: the HUB team finds their hop, their number, their fix. People fund fixes for rows that carry their name.
⚠️ Two Ways to Lose the SLA Conversation
  • Committing the asked-for number without a load test. 03:00 with zero headroom is a scheduled breach: the first month-end blowout burns the team's credibility, and the sponsor stops believing the next number too.
  • Blaming the upstream team without evidence. "The extract is slow" invites a turf war; "the extract tops out at 800k rows an hour, and here's the APM trace" invites the HUB team into the fix. Evidence first, finger never.
  • Optimising off the critical path. If the bottleneck is the upstream extract and the downstream index rebuild, tuning your transform further is visible effort with zero effect — and sponsors notice when the number doesn't move.
✅ From Status Report to Action Plan
  • Every bottleneck gets an owner ask. "The HUB team to parallelise the extract partitions (worth 40 percent)"; "us to move the index rebuild after the finance feed (worth 25 minutes)". An assessment with owner asks is an action plan; without them, it's a complaint.
  • Commit with headroom, explain in the same breath. "04:30 average, 06:30 month-end, 20 percent headroom — because the number you asked for was never measured, and this one is." The explanation and the number travel together, or neither lands.
  • Let the number improve on evidence. "If the fixes land, the commitment improves next quarter" — an SLA that can get better is easier to sign than one that can only get worse.
  • Measurement order of authority: instrumentation, then APM, then load test. Start with the cheap tools, end with the one that settles arguments — and never let a disputed claim reach a steering forum without the strongest instrument behind it.

Tom（技术负责人）：那在拿给 Marcus 之前，我们怎么证明这些数字？HUB 团队肯定会质疑"归档日志竞争"这个说法。

你（架构师）：三件测量工具，按权威度排序。每段链路边界打时间戳插桩——已经完成 70%，本周补齐剩下两段。传输服务上上 APM，拿第二段的调用链。再用负载测试回放上个月末的数据，按 1 倍和 1.5 倍量——这才是终结争论的那个。没有负载测试背书的 SLA 一律不签。

Tom（技术负责人）：那就是承诺问题。Marcus 最初要的是"集市 3 点就绪"——我们到底承诺什么，而且这个数我能在会议室里守住吗？

你（架构师）：不承诺 3 点——那个数字对窗口零余量，而余量才是吸收上游抽取超时的东西。我们承诺平常日 4:30、月末 6:30，留 20% 余量——并且同一句话里说清为什么：他们要的数字从没被测量过，我们给的数字是被测过的。然后每个瓶颈带一个 owner 请求：请 HUB 团队把抽取分区并行化——抽取大约省 40%；我们把索引重建挪到财务供数之后——省 25 分钟。承诺数字只在负载测试通过之后给出，修复落地后下季度数字还会变好。这是一个能守一年的 SLA，不是一页新闻稿。

Q2. Spot the performance mistake — which line in this optimisation plan is wrong?
  1. "Instrument all five hop boundaries with timestamps so any late run can be reconstructed."
  2. "The evidence shows the upstream extract and the index rebuild are the bottlenecks, so we will spend the next sprint tuning our transform code by another 15 minutes."
  3. "We will replay last month-end's data at 1x and 1.5x volume before committing any SLA number."
  4. "We will commit to 04:30 average with 20 percent headroom instead of the 03:00 originally requested."
② is the mistake — the transform is not on the critical path, so tuning it another 15 minutes changes the total run time by zero. That is the classic wasted-optimisation trap: effort went where the team owned code, not where the evidence pointed. ① is right — per-hop instrumentation is what turns a late run into a diagnosis. ③ is right — no SLA without a load test behind it. ④ is right — commit with headroom, because the asked-for number has zero margin against the window.
