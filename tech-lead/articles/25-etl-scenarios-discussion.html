ETL Scenarios Discussion

Article 25 · Module 6: Reliability & Delivery · Tech Lead English

⏱ 10 min read 🟡 Intermediate 🎯 Full vs incremental load, late-arriving data, dedup, SFTP feeds, checksums, reconciliation, backfill

1. Scenario 场景背景

It is Tuesday, 10:30 AM HKT, the weekly design huddle. You are Wei, Tech Lead of the Data Platform squad. With you are Raj, senior developer, and Lin, backend developer. The squad owns the nightly ETL that loads card transactions from the global acquirer feed into the risk analytics mart used by the Financial Crime team.

周二上午 10:30，每周设计讨论会。你是 Wei，数据平台squad 的 Tech Lead，同行的有高级开发 Raj 和后端开发 Lin。squad 负责夜间 ETL，把卡交易从全球收单方数据源加载进金融犯罪团队使用的风险分析集市。

Two live issues from last night: a failed reconciliation (row counts don't match between source and target) and a recurring late-arriving data problem. Beyond the fix, the BA has asked you to explain the behaviour in business language to the risk stakeholders — so the design discussion must end with a plain-English summary.

昨晚留下两个问题：一次对账失败（源和目标行数对不上），以及反复出现的晚到数据问题。除了修复，BA 还要求你用业务语言向风险干系人解释——所以设计讨论最后要落到一段大白话总结上。

2. Dialogue 英文对话

Lin (Dev): Last night's reconciliation failed — source 1,204,558 rows, target 1,204,531. Twenty-seven rows short. The risk team flagged it at 6 AM.昨晚对账失败了——源 1,204,558 行，目标 1,204,531 行，差 27 行。风险团队早上 6 点就找过来了。
You (TL): Before we panic — missing or collapsed? Our dedup key is card number, transaction timestamp, and amount. If duplicates were collapsed by the dedup merge, a count break is expected and benign.先别慌——是真丢了还是被去重合并了？我们的 dedup key 是卡号、交易时间戳、金额。如果是去重合并把重复行折叠了，行数差是预期内的、无害的。
Raj (Senior Dev): I traced it. Nine of the twenty-seven were duplicates collapsed by design. The other eighteen are genuinely absent — their timestamps show they arrived after our cutoff.我追过了。27 行里有 9 行是按设计去重折叠的。另外 18 行是真的没有——时间戳显示它们在我们的截数点之后才到。
Lin (Dev): That's the late-arriving pattern again. Some acquirer feeds promise T+1 but land at T+2. Our incremental job uses a high-water mark — once it advances past those rows, the next run skips them forever.又是晚到数据的套路。有些收单 feed 承诺 T+1，实际 T+2 才到。我们的增量作业用高水位——水位一推进去过这些行，后面的作业就永远跳过它们。
You (TL): Right. Full load versus incremental load: a full reload is simplest but ninety minutes and it hammers the source. We keep incremental — but add a three-day lookback window: each run re-scans the last three days instead of just since-watermark.对。全量 vs 增量：全量重载最简单，但要 90 分钟还压垮源端。增量保留——但加一个 3 天回看窗口：每次作业重扫最近 3 天，而不是只扫水位之后。
Raj (Senior Dev): And the out-of-order rows? If day 3 arrives with a transaction dated day 1, a naive insert would duplicate or conflict.那乱序数据呢？如果第 3 天的数据里带了一条第 1 天的交易，直接 insert 会重复或者冲突。
You (TL): Merge, don't insert. Stage the rows, then upsert into the mart keyed on the dedup key — late or out-of-order rows simply overwrite with the same content. And make every re-run idempotent: same input, same result, no double-loading.用合并，不要插入。先落 staging，再按 dedup key upsert 进集市——晚到或乱序行用相同内容覆盖即可。每次重跑都要幂等：同样输入、同样结果，绝不重复加载。
Lin (Dev): For rows that fail transformation — bad currency codes, malformed amounts — do we drop them silently?那些转换失败的行——坏币种、格式错的金额——是悄悄丢掉吗？
You (TL): Never silently. They go to a dead-letter queue — an error table with the original payload and the failure reason — we alert, fix, and replay. Silent drops are how banks lose transactions and auditors find out last.绝不悄悄丢。进 死信队列——带原始报文和失败原因的错误表——告警、修复、重放。悄悄丢数据就是银行丢交易、审计最后才发现的方式。
Raj (Senior Dev): File side, we also had a partial file last night — the SFTP pickup grabbed a feed truncated mid-transfer.文件侧昨晚还遇到一个半截文件——SFTP 抓取抓到了传输中途被截断的 feed。
You (TL): Guard rail: validate the checksum from the feed spec — MD5 sidecar plus the trailer record count — before processing. Reject partial files, alert the upstream, re-poll for thirty minutes, then escalate. Never load a partial file — a reconciliation break is cheaper than corrupt data in the mart.护栏：处理之前按 feed 规范校验校验和——MD5 伴随文件加尾部记录数。绝不加载半截文件——对账报错比把脏数据灌进集市便宜得多。
Lin (Dev): And if we ever need to reload a whole week — a schema fix, say?如果要重灌整整一周呢——比如改了 schema？
You (TL): That's backfill — a documented runbook: truncate-and-reload per partition in staging, upsert to the mart, re-run reconciliation, and freeze risk reports until counts match. One owner, one channel, one checklist.那就是 backfill——要有成文的 runbook：staging 里按分区清空重载，upsert 进集市，重跑对账，行数对上之前冻结风险报表。一个 owner、一个频道、一份清单。
Raj (Senior Dev): The BA's real question though — how do we explain last night to the risk team without jargon?不过 BA 真正的问题是——怎么不用行话跟风险团队解释昨晚的事？
You (TL): In business terms: "Of 1.2 million transactions, twenty-seven landed late from the acquirer and will appear in tomorrow's report; nine were duplicates removed by design; zero data lost." Then agree the reconciliation tolerance and a single owner with the business, so a count break is a process, not a panic.用业务语言："120 万笔交易里，27 笔晚到，明天报表会补上；9 笔是按设计剔除的重复；零数据丢失。"然后跟业务方一起敲定对账容差和唯一 owner，让行数差走流程，而不是靠慌乱。

4. Key Phrases 关键句型

  • CORE Full load versus incremental load: full is simplest, incremental is cheapest. 全量 vs 增量加载：全量最简单，增量最省资源 The first trade-off to name in any feed design. Say which side you chose and why. 这是 feed 设计第一个要摆出来的取舍；说出你选了哪边、为什么。
  • CORE The high-water mark advanced past those rows, so the next run skipped them forever. 高水位推进过了那些行，下次作业就永远跳过 High-water mark is the standard term for the "last processed position" in incremental loads. High-water mark 是增量加载"上次处理位置"的标准术语。
  • CORE Add a three-day lookback window so late-arriving rows are still picked up. 加一个 3 天回看窗口，让晚到的行也能被扫到 The standard fix for T+2 feeds. Quantify the window against the worst upstream delay. 这是 T+2 feed 的标准修法；窗口大小按上游最大延迟来定。
  • CORE Our dedup key is card number, timestamp, and amount — duplicates collapse on merge. 去重键是卡号、时间戳、金额——合并时重复行被折叠 Always name the dedup key explicitly — "duplicates" means nothing without it. 去重键要明确说出来——没有键，"重复"一词毫无意义。
  • CORE Validate the checksum before processing — never load a partial file. 处理前先校验校验和——绝不加载半截文件 Never… statements are guard rails. Pair each with the cheaper failure: a count break beats corrupt data. "绝不…"句式是护栏；每条都配上更便宜的失败方式：对账报错好过脏数据。
  • CORE Failed rows go to a dead-letter queue with the payload, and we replay after the fix. 失败行带原始报文进死信队列，修复后重放 Replay (not "re-run everything") shows you can recover surgically. 用 replay（重放）而不是"全部重跑"，说明你能外科手术式地恢复。
  • CORE Re-runs must be idempotent — same input, same result, no double-loading. 重跑必须幂等——同样输入、同样结果、不重复加载 Idempotent is the word reviewers listen for in any re-run design. 任何重跑设计评审中，评审者都在等 idempotent 这个词。
  • FORMAL Let's agree the reconciliation tolerance and a single owner with the business. 和业务方一起敲定对账容差和唯一 owner Turns recurring count breaks from escalations into an agreed process. 把反复出现的行数差从"升级"变成"约定的流程"。
  • CASUAL In business terms: nothing is lost — it just landed late and will show up tomorrow. 用业务语言说：数据没丢——只是晚到了，明天会补上 In business terms is the magic prefix that switches your register for stakeholders. In business terms 是切换到干系人频道的关键前缀。
  • CORE Backfill is a runbook, not an improv: per partition, re-run reconciliation, freeze reports until counts match. Backfill 靠 runbook，不靠临场发挥：按分区、重跑对账、行数对上前冻结报表 Not an improv signals discipline to juniors and confidence to auditors. Not an improv 向新人传递纪律，向审计传递可控。

5. Vocabulary 词汇表

  • ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) n. 抽取-转换-加载 The pipeline pattern that moves data from source systems into a warehouse or mart. ELT loads first and transforms inside the target. 把数据从源系统搬进数仓/集市的管道模式；ELT 是先加载再在目标端转换。
  • full load n. 全量加载 Reload everything, every run. Simple and self-healing, but slow and heavy on the source. 每次全部重载。简单、能自愈，但慢且压源端。
  • incremental load n. 增量加载 Load only rows newer than the last processed position (the high-water mark). Fast, but misses late-arriving data unless widened. 只加载比上次处理位置（高水位）新的行。快，但不加宽窗口就会漏晚到数据。
  • high-water mark n. 高水位（增量断点） The timestamp or sequence number of the last row processed. Stored by the job and compared on each run. 上次处理到的最新时间戳或序号；作业保存并在每次运行时比对。
  • late-arriving data n. 晚到数据 Rows that reach the pipeline after the cutoff that should have contained them — the classic cause of reconciliation breaks. 在本应包含它们的截数点之后才到达的数据——对账失败的典型原因。
  • dedup / deduplication n. 去重 Collapsing duplicate rows using a dedup key (natural key). Requires the key to be defined and agreed, not guessed. 用去重键（自然键）折叠重复行；键必须明确定义并达成一致，不能靠猜。
  • upsert v. 有则更新、无则插入 Update if the key exists, insert if it doesn't. The merge primitive that makes re-runs and late data safe. 键存在则更新，不存在则插入；是让重跑和晚到数据变安全的合并原语。
  • staging table n. 暂存表 An intermediate landing area between raw files and the target mart — validate, dedup, and transform there before publishing. 原始文件和目标集市之间的中转区——发布前先在这里校验、去重、转换。
  • checksum n. 校验和 A digest (MD5/SHA-256) delivered with a file to prove it arrived intact; in banking feeds often paired with a trailer record count. 随文件一起交付的摘要（MD5/SHA-256），用来证明文件完整送达；银行 feed 常配尾部记录数。
  • reconciliation n. 对账 Comparing counts and control totals (e.g., sum of amounts) between source and target to prove nothing was lost or duplicated. 比对源和目标的行数与控制总额（如金额合计），证明没有丢失或重复。
  • dead-letter queue (DLQ) n. 死信队列（错误表） Where rows that fail processing are parked with their payload and failure reason, awaiting fix and replay. 处理失败的行连同原始报文和失败原因一起停放的地方，等修复后重放。
  • backfill n./v. 回填 / 重灌历史数据 Re-processing a historical range after a fix. Must be idempotent, partitioned, and followed by reconciliation. 修复后重处理一段历史区间；必须幂等、按分区，并重新对账。

6. Grammar Notes 语法点

① "X versus Y: X is simplest, Y is cheapest" — the design trade-off frame

Design discussions run on X versus Y frames: full load versus incremental load, insert versus upsert, drop versus dead-letter. Give both sides a one-adjective verdict (simplest / cheapest / safest), then state your choice. Never present only your preference — the comparison is what earns trust.

设计讨论靠 X versus Y 框架推进：全量 vs 增量、insert vs upsert、丢弃 vs 死信。给两边各一个形容词结论（simplest / cheapest / safest），再说出你的选择。只给偏好不给对比，赢不了信任。

② Zero conditional for pipeline rules — "If X fails, we Y"

Pipeline behaviour is stated with the zero conditional (if + present, present): "If the checksum fails, we reject and re-poll." "If duplicates collapse, the count breaks by design." This tense signals a permanent rule, not a one-off decision — exactly what a runbook needs.

管道规则用零条件句（if + 现在时, 现在时）表达："If the checksum fails, we reject and re-poll." 这个时态表示永久规则而非临时决定——正是 runbook 需要的语气。

③ "In business terms, ..." — the register-switch prefix

When a stakeholder's eyes glaze over, reset with "In business terms: ..." followed by three facts — a number, a cause, a resolution time. "Twenty-seven transactions landed late; they will appear in tomorrow's report; zero data lost." Three beats, no jargon, done.

当干系人开始眼神涣散，用 "In business terms: ..." 重置话题，后面跟三个事实——一个数字、一个原因、一个解决时间。"27 笔晚到；明天报表补上；零数据丢失。"三拍收尾，零行话。

7. Cultural Tips 文化 / 职场 Tips

💡 Explaining Data Gaps to Non-Technical Stakeholders
  • Lead with the outcome, not the pipeline. "Zero data lost, tomorrow's report is complete" lands before any talk of watermarks. 先讲结果，不讲管道。"零数据丢失、明天报表完整"永远放在高水位之前说。
  • Pre-agree the tolerance. A count break within an agreed tolerance is a log entry; without one, it's a 6 AM escalation. 容差要提前约定。约定内的行数差只是一条日志；没约定就是早上 6 点的升级电话。
  • Numbers, then cause, then ETA. "27 rows, late from the acquirer, in tomorrow's report." Stakeholders repeat that sentence to their bosses — write it for them. 数字→原因→时间点。"27 行、收单方晚到、明天补上。"干系人会把这句话转述给老板——替他们把话写好。
⚠️ Don't Blame the Upstream in Public Channels
  • Say "the feed arrived after our cutoff" — a fact. Avoid "the acquirer team messed up again" — a relationship tax you pay at the next SLA negotiation. 说"feed 在截数点后才到"——是事实；别说"收单团队又搞砸了"——这是在下次 SLA 谈判前预付的关系税。
  • Raise feed SLA gaps through the agreed channel with data attached (timestamps, counts), not in a group chat pile-on. feed SLA 问题要走约定渠道并附数据（时间戳、行数），不要在群聊里围攻。
✅ Credit-Sharing in Data Work
  • Name the tracer. "Raj traced eighteen rows to the cutoff; Lin ruled out dedup first." Precise credit makes juniors volunteer for the next investigation. 点名追查者："Raj 把 18 行追到了截数点；Lin 先排除了去重。"精确的功劳归属会让新人主动认领下一次排查。
  • Share the fix, not just the finding. A TL who publishes the lookback-window design — with Raj as author — turns a one-off fix into squad knowledge. 公布修复方案并署名 Raj，把一次性修复变成 squad 知识。

8. Practice 练习题

✍️ Practice Drills
Q1. Translate to English (stakeholder summary style):

"120 万笔交易中，27 笔由收单方晚到，会出现在明天的报表里；9 笔是按设计剔除的重复；零数据丢失。我们已加了一个 3 天回看窗口，并和业务方约定了对账容差。"

"Of 1.2 million transactions, twenty-seven landed late from the acquirer and will appear in tomorrow's report; nine were duplicates removed by design; zero data lost. We have added a three-day lookback window and agreed a reconciliation tolerance with the business."
Q2. Pick the correct handling — your SFTP pickup grabs a file whose checksum does not match. What do you do?
  1. Load it anyway — most rows look fine, and reconciliation will catch issues.
  2. Reject the file, alert the upstream, re-poll for thirty minutes, escalate if it never arrives, and never load a partial file.
  3. Wait silently and load tomorrow's file instead — no alert needed.
  4. Manually edit the file to fix the truncated rows, then load it.
② is correct. ① corrupts the mart — a reconciliation break after loading is far more expensive than before. ③ hides the incident and breaks the SLA trail. ④ is a data-integrity and audit violation — never hand-edit feeds. ② 正确。① 会污染集市——加载后再对账的代价远高于加载前。③ 隐瞒事故、破坏 SLA 留痕。④ 违反数据完整性和审计要求——绝不手工改 feed 文件。
Q3. Your turn — design the fix, then explain it:

Your nightly incremental ETL skips late-arriving rows because the high-water mark has advanced. Write: (1) two English sentences describing the root cause technically, and (2) two English sentences explaining the impact and fix in business terms. Use high-water mark, lookback window, and upsert.

(1) "Our incremental job advances the high-water mark each run, so rows that arrive after the cutoff are skipped permanently. Because the acquirer feeds land at T+2 against a T+1 promise, late transactions never enter the mart." (2) "In business terms: some transactions miss the daily risk report and appear a day late — nothing is lost. We are adding a three-day lookback window and merging late rows with an upsert, so tomorrow's report is complete without a full reload."
