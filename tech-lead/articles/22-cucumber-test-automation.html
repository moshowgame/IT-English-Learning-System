Cucumber Test Automation

Article 22 · Module 6: Engineering Automation · Tech Lead English

⏱ 9 min read 🟢 Beginner-friendly 🎯 Gherkin, three amigos, step definitions, living documentation, CI reporting

1. Scenario 场景背景

It is Tuesday, 2:00 PM HKT, in a sprint where the Payment Gateway squad is building the daily payment limit feature (story PAY-330). You have booked a 45-minute three-amigo session — the BA, the QA, and you as Tech Lead. The goal: turn the story's acceptance criteria into Cucumber feature files that everyone — business included — can read.

周二下午 2 点香港时间。Payment Gateway squad 正在开发日累计支付限额功能（故事 PAY-330）。你订了一个 45 分钟的 three-amigo 会议——BA、QA 加上你这位 Tech Lead。目标：把故事的验收标准变成人人都读得懂——包括业务方——的 Cucumber feature 文件。

Joining you are Priya, the BA (Mumbai), and Mei, the QA (Hong Kong). The ground rule you set: the Gherkin stays business-readable; the implementation detail lives elsewhere. By the end, the feature file will be living documentation — it describes what the system does, and it is executed by CI on every merge.

参会的是 BA Priya（孟买）和 QA Mei（香港）。你定的基本规则：Gherkin 保持业务可读；实现细节住在别处。会议结束时，feature 文件将成为活文档——它描述系统行为，并且 CI 在每次合并时都执行它。

2. Dialogue 英文对话

You (TL): Thanks for making time. This is a three-amigo session — BA, QA, dev perspective. Story PAY-330, the daily payment limit. Priya, walk us through the acceptance criteria, and we'll turn them into Gherkin as we go. 谢谢两位抽时间。这是 three-amigo 会议——BA、QA、开发三方视角。故事 PAY-330，日累计支付限额。Priya，先过一遍验收标准，我们边听边转成 Gherkin。
Priya (BA): First rule from the business: Given a customer with a daily limit of 50,000 HKD, When they submit a payment of 60,000 HKD, Then the payment is rejected — with a clear "limit exceeded" message, and no money leaves the account. 业务第一条规则：Given 一个日限额 5 万港币的客户，When 提交一笔 6 万港币的支付，Then 支付被拒绝——提示"超出限额"，且资金不离开账户。
You (TL): That's a textbook Given / When / Then — precondition, action, observable outcome. And notice the last clause: "no money leaves the account". That's the business risk stated as a checkable outcome. Keep that precision and the feature file becomes living documentation — it describes the system and proves it, on every CI run. 这是教科书级的 Given / When / Then——前置条件、动作、可观察的结果。注意最后一句："资金不离开账户"。这是把业务风险写成了可验证的结果。保持这种精确度，feature 文件就成了活文档——既描述系统，又在每次 CI 运行时证明它。
Mei (QA): I'll draft the feature file. One scenario per business rule, plus the edge cases the ticket is missing: the boundary — exactly 50,000 should go through; and cumulative limits — two payments of 30,000 and 25,000 mean the second one is rejected even though each is under the limit. 我来起草 feature 文件。每条业务规则一个 scenario，再加上工单里漏掉的边界情况：边界值——正好 5 万应该放行；累计限额——3 万加 2.5 万两笔，第二笔要被拒，虽然单笔都没超限。
Priya (BA): The cumulative rule is the one the business actually cares about — good catch. And use a Background section for the shared precondition: "Given a verified customer with a daily limit of 50,000 HKD" — so we don't repeat it in every scenario. 累计规则才是业务真正在意的——抓得好。另外把公共前置条件放到 Background 段："Given 一个日限额 5 万港币的已验证客户"——免得每个 scenario 都重复一遍。
Mei (QA): Agreed. And I'll keep the steps business-readable: "When the customer submits a payment of 60,000 HKD" — no button names, no CSS selectors, no URLs. If the UI redesigns next month, the feature file shouldn't change. 同意。我会让步骤保持业务可读："When 客户提交一笔 6 万港币的支付"——不写按钮名、不写 CSS 选择器、不写 URL。就算下个月 UI 改版，feature 文件也不该跟着改。
You (TL): Exactly — Gherkin is the contract, implementation is the plumbing. Mei, the step definitions glue the two together: a C# method annotated [When(@"the customer submits a payment of {int} HKD")] — the {int} captures the amount from the sentence and passes it to the code. 完全正确——Gherkin 是契约，实现是管道。Mei，step definition 把两者粘起来：一个标注了 [When(@"the customer submits a payment of {int} HKD")] 的 C# 方法——{int} 从句子里捕获金额传给代码。
You (TL): And for the BA side of the house: Jenkins will publish the Cucumber report on every run — scenario names in plain English, green or red, with step-level detail. We'll tag the scenarios — @payments @smoke @regression — so the pipeline runs @smoke on every merge and the full tagged suite nightly. Priya, you can filter the report by tag and read it like a status page. 再说说 BA 关心的部分：Jenkins 每次运行都会发布 Cucumber 报告——scenario 名字是纯英文，绿或红，带步骤级细节。我们还会给 scenario 打 tag——@payments @smoke @regression——流水线每次合并跑 @smoke，每夜跑全量打标套件。Priya，你可以按 tag 过滤报告，像看状态页一样读它。
Mei (QA): One risk I want on the table early: flaky step implementations. The cumulative-limit check waits on the async ledger update — sometimes the step races the backend and goes red randomly. I don't want to paper over it with infinite retries. 我想早点把一个风险摆到桌面上：flaky 的 step 实现。累计限额检查要等异步账务更新——有时步骤和后端抢跑，随机变红。我不想用无限重试把它糊弄过去。
You (TL): Agreed — fix the wait, don't mask it. The step should wait on the ledger event or poll with a timeout, not sleep and hope. A retry policy is a monitoring tool, not a repair: if a scenario is red, red must mean broken. The day "green means nothing" is the day the business stops reading the report. 同意——修等待逻辑，别掩盖它。步骤应该等账务事件或带超时轮询，而不是睡一觉赌运气。重试策略是监控手段，不是修复手段：scenario 变红，红就必须等于真的坏了。哪天"绿了也不代表什么"，业务方就不会再看这份报告了。
Priya (BA): Then let's make it official: the feature file, the step definitions, and the code land in the same pull request — and my sign-off is reading the scenarios, not clicking through test cases. The scenarios are the acceptance criteria, executable. 那咱们把它定成规矩：feature 文件、step definition 和代码进同一个 pull request——我的签批就是读 scenario，而不是逐个点测试用例。scenario 就是可执行的验收标准。
You (TL): Deal. Actions: Mei drafts the feature file today and I review it for readability before any code; step definitions go in the same PR as the implementation; the pipeline runs the tagged suite and publishes the report. If this sticks, our acceptance criteria stop rotting in Jira and start running in Jenkins. 成交。行动项：Mei 今天起草 feature 文件，写代码前我先做可读性评审；step definition 和实现进同一个 PR；流水线跑打标套件并发布报告。如果坚持下来，我们的验收标准就不再在 Jira 里烂掉，而是在 Jenkins 里跑起来。

3. Key Phrases 关键句型

  • CORE This is a three-amigo session — BA, QA, dev perspective. 这是 three-amigo 会议——BA、QA、开发三方视角。 Name the format and the roles upfront; it sets expectations that everyone contributes, not just listens. 开场先说明会议形式与角色，确立"人人贡献"的预期。
  • CORE Given a customer with a daily limit of 50,000 HKD, When they submit a payment of 60,000 HKD, Then the payment is rejected. Given 一个日限额 5 万的客户，When 提交 6 万的支付，Then 支付被拒绝。 The Given / When / Then skeleton: precondition / action / observable outcome. If "Then" isn't observable, it isn't a test yet. Given/When/Then 三段式：前置/动作/可观察结果。Then 不可观察就还不是测试。
  • CORE The feature file becomes living documentation. feature 文件成为活文档。 "Living" = it can't go stale, because CI executes it. Contrast with Word documents that describe last year's system. "Living" 指因 CI 持续执行而永不过时，对比会过时的 Word 文档。
  • CORE Keep the steps business-readable — no button names, no CSS selectors. 步骤保持业务可读——不写按钮名、不写 CSS 选择器。 The one rule that keeps BA engagement. Implementation detail in Gherkin couples your documentation to your UI. 这条规则保住 BA 的参与度；Gherkin 里混实现细节会让文档绑死 UI。
  • CORE Gherkin is the contract, implementation is the plumbing. Gherkin 是契约，实现是管道。 A quotable separation-of-concerns line. The contract changes when business rules change — never when code refactors. 契约只在业务规则变化时改，重构时永远不改。
  • CORE The step definitions glue the two together — {int} captures the amount from the sentence. step definition 把两者粘起来——{int} 从句子里捕获金额。 Step definitions = 从 Gherkin 步骤到代码的粘合层。Parameter capture ({int}, {string}) is what keeps one step reusable for many scenarios. 参数捕获让一个步骤服务多个 scenario。
  • CORE We tag the scenarios — @smoke on every merge, the full tagged suite nightly. 给 scenario 打 tag——每次合并跑 @smoke，每夜跑全量打标套件。 Tags are how CI selects subsets. Fast feedback first (@smoke), depth later (@regression) — the same shape as your pipeline gates. Tag 是 CI 的选择器；先快反馈后全深度，与流水线门禁同构。
  • CORE Flaky step implementations — I don't want to paper over it with infinite retries. flaky 的 step 实现——我不想用无限重试糊弄过去。 "Paper over" = 掩盖而非修复. QA engineers who raise flakiness early are protecting the report's credibility — back them. QA 主动暴露 flaky 是在保护报告公信力，TL 要力挺。
  • FORMAL If a scenario is red, red must mean broken. scenario 变红，红就必须等于真的坏了。 The TL's trust principle in one sentence. Retries may be a monitoring tool, never a repair strategy. 一句话讲透测试公信力；重试可以是监控手段，但不能是修复策略。
  • CASUAL Deal — the scenarios are the acceptance criteria, executable. 成交——scenario 就是可执行的验收标准。 "Deal" closes a negotiation in one word; pairing it with the shared artifact makes the agreement concrete. 一个词敲定共识，配上共同工件让约定落地。

4. Vocabulary 词汇表

  • BDD (Behaviour-Driven Development) n. 行为驱动开发 A practice where business behaviour is specified in shared, readable examples that are then automated as tests.
  • Cucumber n. Cucumber 测试框架 The BDD tool that executes Gherkin feature files against step definitions in Java, C#, JavaScript and more.
  • Gherkin n. Gherkin 语法 The structured, business-readable language of feature files — keywords: Feature, Scenario, Given, When, Then, And, Background.
  • Feature file n. 特性文件 A text file (e.g., daily-payment-limit.feature) holding scenarios for one business capability — the unit of living documentation.
  • Scenario n. 场景 One concrete example of behaviour: a Given/When/Then sequence that CI can execute and report green or red.
  • Step definition n. 步骤定义 The code behind a Gherkin step — an annotated method (Java annotation / C# attribute) that parses parameters like {int} and drives the system.
  • Given / When / Then phr. 前置/动作/结果 The Gherkin trio: Given sets preconditions, When performs the action, Then asserts the observable outcome.
  • Background n. 公共前置 A feature-file section holding steps shared by every scenario, so preconditions aren't repeated.
  • Tag n. 标签 A label (@smoke, @regression, @payments) used to select which scenarios a CI job runs and to filter reports.
  • Living documentation n. 活文档 Documentation that cannot go stale because it is executed by CI on every change — the feature files and their reports.
  • Three amigos n. 三剑客会议 BA + QA + dev reviewing a story together before build — turning acceptance criteria into shared examples.
  • Flaky test n. 不稳定测试 A test that passes and fails randomly without a product change — usually a race, a wait problem, or environment noise.

5. Grammar Notes 语法点

① "Turn X into Y" — the workshop verb

Three-amigo sessions are transformations: turn the acceptance criteria into Gherkin; turn user stories into scenarios; turn the feature file into living documentation. This verb frames the meeting as producing an artifact, not just talk.

three-amigo 会议是"转化"：把验收标准转成 Gherkin；把故事转成 scenario；把 feature 文件变成活文档。这个动词把会议定义为产出工件，而不是闲聊。

  • ✅ "We'll turn these three acceptance criteria into two scenarios and one Background."

② "X must mean Y" — defining semantic contracts

Use this pattern when defining what test results mean. Stronger than "should" — it's a definition, not a preference.

定义测试结果的语义时用这个句式。比 should 强——这是定义，不是偏好。

  • ✅ "Red must mean broken; green must mean releasable."
  • ✅ "A scenario must mean a business rule, not a button."

③ "Paper over" / "race the backend" — test-failure idioms

Two idioms QA engineers use constantly. Use them and QA will know you've been in their shoes.

QA 高频习语。用上它们，QA 立刻知道你懂行。

  • Paper over = 掩盖 — hide the symptom instead of fixing it: "Don't paper over the race with retries."
  • Race the backend = 和后端抢跑 — a timing race causing flakiness: "The step races the ledger update."

6. Cultural Tips 文化 / 职场 Tips

💡 Making the BA a first-class citizen of your test suite
  1. Invite the BA to read the Cucumber report, not the code. "Priya, filter by @payments — every green line is a business rule we just proved." Shared ownership of quality starts with shared artifacts.
  2. Review feature files for readability before code exists. If the BA can't predict what a scenario does, the scenario is wrong — fix it before step definitions fossilize it.
  3. Sign-off by reading, not clicking. "My sign-off is reading the scenarios" — this reframes UAT from manual clicking to reviewing executable evidence.
  4. Keep acronyms out of scenario titles. "Cumulative payments exceeding daily limit are rejected" beats "CLX-04 negative path v2". The title is documentation too.
⚠️ The two ways BDD efforts die
  • ❌ Gherkin that leaks implementation. "When I click the #submitPayBtn div" — one UI refactor and your living documentation dies as dead documentation.
  • ❌ Chasing green with retries. A step that retries 5 times "until it passes" hides real races. Flakiness compounds; trust in the report evaporates.
  • ❌ Feature files written after the code. If tests are reverse-engineered from the implementation, they prove the code, not the business rule — BDD without the B.
  • ✅ "Scenarios are written in the three-amigo session, before the code, in the same PR." That ordering is the whole discipline.
✅ Reporting BDD wins upward

When the PM asks "is PAY-330 tested?", the old answer is "QA is 70% through the test cases".

The new answer: "11 scenarios green on the last CI run — here's the report, readable in English, including the cumulative-limit rule the business flagged."

Executives can't read unit test coverage, but they can read a scenario. That is the day quality stops being a black box.

7. Practice 练习题

✍️ Practice Drills
Q1. Translate the three-amigo kickoff into English:

"这是 three-amigo 会议。目标：把 PAY-330 的验收标准转成 Gherkin。规则：步骤保持业务可读——不写按钮名、不写选择器；Gherkin 是契约，实现是管道；公共前置条件放 Background；scenario 打 @smoke 和 @regression 标签，每次合并跑 smoke，每夜跑全量。feature 文件、step definition 和代码进同一个 PR。"

"This is a three-amigo session. Goal: turn the PAY-330 acceptance criteria into Gherkin.

Ground rules: keep the steps business-readable — no button names, no selectors; Gherkin is the contract, implementation is the plumbing; shared preconditions go in a Background section; scenarios get @smoke and @regression tags — @smoke on every merge, the full tagged suite nightly.

The feature file, the step definitions and the code land in the same PR."

Q2. Write the Gherkin — cumulative limit edge case:

Business rule: a customer with a 50,000 HKD daily limit pays 30,000, then 25,000. The second payment must be rejected even though each payment alone is under the limit. Write the scenario in Given / When / Then English, using a Background precondition.

Background:
Given a verified customer with a daily limit of 50,000 HKD

Scenario: Cumulative payments exceeding the daily limit are rejected
Given the customer has already paid 30,000 HKD today
When the customer submits a payment of 25,000 HKD
Then the payment is rejected with a "daily limit exceeded" message
And the total paid today remains 30,000 HKD

(Note the final And-step: "the total paid today remains 30,000 HKD" — an observable outcome about money, exactly the precision a BA looks for.)

Q3. Role-play: QA reports a flaky step — respond as TL

Mei tells you: "The cumulative-limit step went red twice this week, green after re-running. It waits on the async ledger update." Respond: (a) your diagnosis, (b) your policy on retries, (c) the fix you expect, (d) how you'd track flakiness going forward.

"Thanks for flagging it early — flaky tests rot trust in the report, so let's fix the wait, not mask it.

Diagnosis: the step is racing the backend — the ledger update is async, and the assertion fires before the event lands.

Policy: a retry policy is a monitoring tool, not a repair — if a scenario is red, red must mean broken; we don't paper over it with infinite retries.

Fix: the step definition should wait on the ledger event, or poll the account balance with a timeout and a clear failure message — no sleep-and-hope.

Tracking: we tag the scenario @flaky-candidate, watch the Cucumber report trend, and review flakiness weekly — if it stays red for reasons we can't explain, it gets fixed before we merge anything else. Green means something, or the whole suite means nothing."

8. Key Takeaways 本篇要点

🎓 What you can now run as TL
  1. Run a three-amigo session — BA reads the ACs, QA drafts scenarios, dev checks feasibility, all before code.
  2. Keep Gherkin at the business layer — contract vs plumbing; Background for shared preconditions.
  3. Make the pipeline read the tags — @smoke on merge, @regression nightly, reports the BA can actually read.
  4. Defend report credibility — fix waits, don't mask them; red must mean broken.
  5. Ship it as one PR — feature file, step definitions, implementation, together.

Practice tip: In your next story kickoff, open with "Let's turn these acceptance criteria into scenarios — business-readable ones." One sentence, and the BA knows this sprint will be different.
