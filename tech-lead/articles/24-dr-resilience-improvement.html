DR Design & Resilience Improvements

Article 24 · Module 6: Reliability & Delivery · Tech Lead English

⏱ 10 min read 🔴 Senior 🎯 Warm standby review, RTO/RPO gaps, automated failover, runbook rehearsal, resilience backlog

1. Scenario 场景背景

It is Wednesday, 2:00 PM HKT, three weeks after the annual DR drill. You are Wei, the Tech Lead for the Payments Platform squad. In the room (and on Teams) are Michael, your Senior Manager, and Steven, the Group Architect who owns the resilience standard.

周三下午 2 点，年度灾备演练结束三周后。你是 Wei，支付平台squad 的 Tech Lead。会议室里（Teams 上）有高级经理 Michael 和负责韧性标准的集团架构师 Steven。

The current DR setup for the payments service is a warm standby environment in a second region: the application stack runs at reduced capacity, the ledger database uses asynchronous replication, and switching traffic means a manual DNS change executed from the runbook. The mandate today: review the design honestly after the drill, quantify the gaps, and agree a prioritised resilience backlog.

支付服务目前的灾备架构是第二地区的温备环境：应用以降配方式常驻，账本数据库使用异步复制，切流量靠 runbook 里的手动 DNS 变更。今天的任务：演练后诚实评审设计、量化差距、敲定一份按优先级排序的韧性待办。

As the TL, you own the assessment. Your job is not to defend the old design — it is to name the gaps, propose the fixes, and give management a decision-ready plan.

作为 TL，评审由你负责。你的工作不是为旧设计辩护，而是指出差距、提出改进方案、给管理层一份可直接决策的计划。

2. Dialogue 英文对话

You (TL): Happy to. Current state first: warm standby in the second region, asynchronous DB replication for the ledger, and a manual DNS switch driven by the runbook. Three gaps came out of the drill.没问题。先说现状：第二地区温备，账本库异步复制，切流靠 runbook 里的手动 DNS 切换。演练暴露了三个差距。
You (TL): Gap one — RTO. Our target is two hours; the drill took four hours and ten minutes. The runbook is stale — it references a jump host we decommissioned last year — and there are fourteen manual, swivel-chair steps between detection and traffic switch.差距一——RTO。目标是 2 小时，演练花了 4 小时 10 分。runbook 过期了——里面还引用去年就下线的堡垒机——从发现故障到切流之间有 14 步手动"转椅操作"。
Steven (Architect): Gap two is mine to confirm — RPO. Async replication lag peaked at fifteen minutes during the drill. If the primary had died at the peak, we would have lost fifteen minutes of ledger writes against a five-minute RPO target.差距二我来确认——RPO。演练中异步复制延迟峰值达到 15 分钟。如果主库恰好在峰值挂掉，按 5 分钟 RPO 目标，我们会丢 15 分钟的账本写入。
You (TL): Agreed. My proposal: move the ledger to semi-synchronous replication — the commit waits for at least one standby ack — plus replication-lag alerting at five minutes. Cost is roughly 5 to 8 percent extra write latency, which our budget absorbs.同意。我的方案：账本库改半同步复制——提交至少要等一个备库确认——再加 复制延迟 5 分钟告警。代价是写延迟增加约 5%–8%，预算内可以吸收。
You (TL): Gap three — the failover itself is manual. I want automated failover: health checks on the primary, three consecutive failures triggers promotion of the standby, and the orchestrator holds a quorum so we never end up with split-brain — two primaries both accepting writes.差距三——切换本身靠人工。我要自动故障切换：对主库做健康检查，连续 3 次失败就触发备库提升，编排器持有仲裁多数，避免脑裂——两个主库同时收写。
Steven (Architect): Good — but automation without fencing is how you cause the outage you were preventing. Fence the old primary before promotion, and keep the manual switch as a break-glass fallback the on-call can execute.好——但没有隔离机制的自动化，反而会制造你要防的故障。提升前先隔离旧主库，手动切换保留为值班同学可执行的紧急兜底。
You (TL): Exactly. Application side, we also need retry with exponential backoff and a circuit breaker — when the primary is half-dead, we fail fast instead of hammering it with ten thousand timeouts per minute.正是。应用侧还要加指数退避重试和熔断器——主库半死不活时，快速失败，而不是每分钟上万次超时去捶它。
You (TL): And the cultural fix: runbook as code — version-controlled, reviewed at every release, plus quarterly game days where we break things on purpose in staging and rehearse the switch. Resilience is a habit, not a project.还有文化层面的修复：runbook 即代码——进版本库、每次发版评审，外加季度 game day——在预发环境故意制造故障、演练切换。韧性是习惯，不是一次性项目。
Michael (Senior Manager): What does this mean in business minutes? If we do nothing, and we have a real event?换算成业务分钟数是什么概念？如果我们什么都不做，真出一次事故呢？
You (TL): Doing nothing: up to four hours of payment outage and fifteen minutes of lost ledger writes to reconstruct by hand. With the backlog: RTO back inside two hours by Q3, RPO within target, and every drill generates evidence for the regulator instead of surprises.不做：最多 4 小时支付中断，外加 15 分钟账本写入要靠人工重建。做了这套待办：Q3 前 RTO 回到 2 小时以内，RPO 达标，每次演练都能给监管交证据，而不是交惊吓。
Michael (Senior Manager): Prioritise it for me. What lands this quarter?排个优先级。这个季度落地什么？
You (TL): Prioritised resilience backlog: P0 — runbook refresh plus a supervised dry run this month. P1 — health checks and automated failover with fencing. P2 — semi-sync replication. P3 — quarterly game days. Nothing here needs a new team; it needs protected capacity in this squad.按优先级排的韧性待办：P0——本月完成 runbook 重写加一次有人监督的干跑。P1——健康检查、带隔离的自动切换。P2——半同步复制。P3——季度 game day。不需要新增团队，只需要本 squad 被保住的容量。
Michael (Senior Manager): Agreed. Bring the cost estimate Thursday, and Steven — sign off the failover design before Wei starts P1.同意。周四给我成本估算。Steven——在 Wei 启动 P1 之前把切换设计签了。
Steven (Architect): Will do. Wei, good assessment — you quantified the gaps instead of dramatising them. That's exactly how resilience should be sold.没问题。Wei，评审很到位——你把差距量化了，而不是渲染它。韧性就该这么卖。

4. Key Phrases 关键句型

  • CORE Current state: warm standby, asynchronous DB replication, manual DNS switch. 现状：温备、数据库异步复制、手动 DNS 切换 Open a DR review with the current state in one sentence — three nouns, no adjectives. 用一句话、三个名词开场说清现状，不要形容词。
  • CORE Our RTO target is two hours; the drill took four hours and ten minutes. RTO 目标 2 小时，演练花了 4 小时 10 分 State target and actual in the same sentence — the gap becomes undeniable. 目标和实测放在同一句里，差距无法被否认。
  • CORE The runbook is stale — it references a jump host we decommissioned last year. runbook 过期了——还引用着去年下线的堡垒机 Stale is the professional word for outdated docs. Concretize it with one wrong detail. Stale 是"文档过期"的专业说法，用一个具体错误细节坐实它。
  • CORE Fourteen manual, swivel-chair steps between detection and traffic switch. 从发现故障到切流之间有 14 步手动"转椅操作" Swivel-chair steps = manual copy-paste style work between systems. Automate these first. Swivel-chair steps 指在系统间人工搬数式的操作，是自动化的首选目标。
  • FORMAL I recommend semi-synchronous replication for the ledger database. 我建议账本库改用半同步复制 I recommend + noun is the cleanest way to propose a design change to an architect. 对架构师提设计变更，用 I recommend + 名词 最干净。
  • CORE Three consecutive failed health checks trigger promotion of the standby. 连续 3 次健康检查失败触发备库提升 Quantify the trigger — "three consecutive failures" prevents flapping on one blip. 触发条件要量化，"连续 3 次"避免一次抖动就乱切换。
  • CORE Retry with exponential backoff, plus a circuit breaker so we fail fast. 指数退避重试，加熔断器快速失败 Fail fast is the justification — retries without a breaker amplify the outage. Fail fast 是理由：只有重试没有熔断会放大故障。
  • CASUAL We run quarterly game days — break things on purpose, in staging. 我们做季度 game day——在预发环境故意搞坏东西 Break things on purpose sounds casual but lands well with engineers; say "fault-injection exercise" with auditors. 对工程师说 break things on purpose 很自然；对审计要说"故障注入演练"。
  • FORMAL Here is the prioritised resilience backlog: P0 runbook, P1 automated failover, P2 semi-sync, P3 game days. 这是排好优先级的韧性待办：P0 runbook、P1 自动切换、P2 半同步、P3 演练 P0–P3 numbering turns a wish list into a decision-ready plan. P0–P3 编号让愿望清单变成可决策的计划。
  • CORE Resilience is a habit, not a project. 韧性是习惯，不是一次性项目 A memorable closing line — it justifies recurring drills instead of a one-off fix. 收尾金句：为常态化演练争取理由，而不是一次性修复。

5. Vocabulary 词汇表

  • failover n. 故障切换（自动切到备用系统） Automatic transfer of traffic/workload to the standby when the primary fails. Opposite action planned between data centres is a switchover. 主库故障时自动把流量切到备库；计划内双向切换叫 switchover。
  • warm standby n. 温备环境 Standby runs at reduced capacity with data replicated; scale-up is needed on failover. Hot = fully scaled and live; cold = powered off until needed. 备端降配常驻、数据已复制，切换时需扩容；hot 是全量在线，cold 是平时不开机。
  • RTO (Recovery Time Objective) n. 恢复时间目标 Maximum acceptable time to restore service after a disruption. Measured from detection to service restored. 中断后恢复服务的最长可接受时间，从发现故障算到服务恢复。
  • RPO (Recovery Point Objective) n. 恢复点目标（可容忍的数据丢失窗口） Maximum acceptable data loss, expressed in time. With async replication, your RPO equals your replication lag. 用时间表达的可接受数据丢失量；异步复制下 RPO 等于复制延迟。
  • replication lag n. 复制延迟 The delay between a write on the primary and the same write landing on the standby. Monitor and alert on it — it is your live RPO. 主库写入到备库落库的延迟；要监控告警，它就是实时的 RPO。
  • runbook n. 运维手册 / 操作手册 Step-by-step operational document for procedures like failover. Best practice: runbook as code — version-controlled and reviewed each release. 像 failover 这类操作的逐步手册；最佳实践是 runbook 即代码，进版本库、随发版评审。
  • health check n. 健康检查 A periodic probe that decides whether a component is alive and ready. Automations usually require N consecutive failures before acting. 定期探测组件是否存活可用；自动化通常要求连续 N 次失败才动作。
  • circuit breaker n. 熔断器 Pattern that stops calling a failing dependency after a threshold (closed → open → half-open), letting it recover. 连续失败达到阈值后停止调用故障依赖（闭合→断开→半开），给对方恢复时间。
  • exponential backoff n. 指数退避（重试间隔指数增长） Retry pattern where each wait doubles (1s, 2s, 4s…), ideally with jitter, to avoid a retry storm. 重试间隔翻倍（1s、2s、4s…），最好加随机抖动，避免重试风暴。
  • split-brain n. 脑裂（双主同时写入） Failure mode where two nodes both believe they are primary and accept writes. Prevented by quorum and fencing. 两个节点都认为自己是主库并接收写入；用仲裁（quorum）和隔离（fencing）预防。
  • game day n. 故障演练日 A scheduled exercise where the team injects failures and rehearses responses — the friendly name for chaos engineering in production-like environments. 团队按计划注入故障并演练响应；在类生产环境做混沌工程的友好叫法。
  • single point of failure (SPOF) n. 单点故障 Any component whose failure takes down the whole service. In DR reviews, hunt SPOFs before buying tools. 任何一个挂掉就拖垮整个服务的组件；做灾备评审时先找 SPOF，再谈买工具。

6. Grammar Notes 语法点

① "Target is X; the drill took Y" — quantifying the gap in one sentence

The strongest DR sentence pattern is target vs actual: "Our RTO target is two hours; the drill took four hours and ten minutes." Two numbers, one semicolon, no adjectives like "quite bad". The audience does the math themselves — which is far more convincing than your evaluation.

灾备汇报最强的句式是目标 vs 实测："Our RTO target is two hours; the drill took four hours and ten minutes." 两个数字、一个分号，不用"挺糟糕"这类形容词。让听众自己做减法，比你自己下结论更有说服力。

② "X triggers Y" — causative verbs for automation design

Automation is described with causative verbs: three consecutive failures trigger promotion, the orchestrator fences the old primary, the alert pages the on-call. Notice the present simple tense — automation rules are permanent truths, not one-off events.

描述自动化用使役动词：trigger（触发）、fence（隔离）、page（呼人）。注意用一般现在时——自动化规则是永久事实，不是一次性事件。

③ "P0… P3" — numbered priorities to negotiate scope

When management asks "what lands this quarter?", answer with a numbered list, not a paragraph: P0 runbook, P1 automated failover, P2 semi-sync, P3 game days. Numbers let the manager cut from the bottom ("drop P3") without touching your critical path. Vague lists get cut arbitrarily.

当管理层问"这个季度落地什么"，用编号清单回答，不要用一段话：P0…P3。编号让经理从尾部裁剪（"砍掉 P3"），而不会动你的关键路径。模糊清单会被任意裁。

7. Cultural Tips 文化 / 职场 Tips

💡 Presenting Resilience Risks Without Alarming Management
  • Gap → Plan → Date. Never present a gap alone. "RTO is 4h" is alarming; "RTO is 4h, P0–P1 land this quarter, back inside 2h by Q3" is reassuring. 灾备汇报三段式：差距→方案→日期。只说"RTO 4 小时"是惊吓，补上季度恢复计划才是安心。
  • Translate RTO/RPO into business minutes. Managers remember "four hours of payment outage" and "fifteen minutes of lost transactions" — not the acronyms. 把 RTO/RPO 翻译成业务分钟数：经理记得住"4 小时支付中断""丢了 15 分钟交易"，记不住缩写。
  • Ask for capacity, not headcount, first. "This needs protected capacity in this squad" is easier to approve than a new team request. 先要"保住的容量"，比开口要新 headcount 容易获批。
⚠️ Don't Blame the Last Drill Team
  • Say "the runbook went stale" — a system and process problem. Never say "the team who wrote it didn't maintain it" — that's a people attack and it makes future authors defensive. 说"runbook 过期了"——这是系统和流程问题；别说"写的人没维护"——那是攻击个人，会让以后没人敢写文档。
  • Stale runbooks are a design flaw, not a discipline failure: fix it with "runbook as code" reviewed at every release, not with a reminder email. runbook 过期是设计缺陷而不是纪律问题：用"随发版评审的 runbook 即代码"来修，而不是发一封提醒邮件。
✅ Credit-Sharing in Assessments
  • Name who found what. "Lin spotted the decommissioned jump host during the drill; Raj traced the replication-lag spike." An assessment that credits investigators builds a culture where people volunteer bad news early. 评审里点名发现者："Lin 在演练中发现堡垒机已下线，Raj 追到了复制延迟峰值。"肯分享功劳的评审，会让大家愿意早报坏消息。
  • Thank the architect's pushback publicly. Steven's fencing warning made your automation safer — agreeing openly with a correction costs you nothing and buys an ally. 公开感谢架构师的反驳：Steven 的隔离提醒让你的自动化更安全。公开认错零成本，换来盟友。

8. Practice 练习题

✍️ Practice Drills
Q1. Translate to English (DR review style):

"我们目前的灾备是温备加数据库异步复制，DNS 切换是手动的。RTO 目标是 2 小时，上次演练花了 4 小时——runbook 过期了，里面还有 14 步手动操作。我的建议是自动化故障切换，连续 3 次健康检查失败触发备库提升。"

"Our current DR is a warm standby with asynchronous DB replication, and the DNS switch is manual. Our RTO target is two hours; the last drill took four — the runbook is stale and still contains fourteen manual steps. My recommendation is automated failover: three consecutive failed health checks trigger promotion of the standby."
Q2. Spot the wrong statement — which line confuses RTO and RPO?
  1. "RPO is how much data we can afford to lose, measured in time."
  2. "RTO is how long it takes us to restore service after a disruption."
  3. "With async replication, our RPO equals our replication lag."
  4. "Our RTO is fifteen minutes, because replication lag peaked at fifteen minutes."
④ is wrong — replication lag is an RPO factor (data loss window), not an RTO factor (restore time). ①②③ are correct. Remember: RTO = time to recover the service; RPO = time window of data loss. ④ 错——复制延迟影响的是 RPO（丢数据窗口），不是 RTO（恢复时长）。①②③ 正确。记忆法：RTO = 恢复服务要多久；RPO = 会丢多久的数据。
Q3. Your turn — write your 60-second DR gap summary:

Scenario: your service has an active-active setup, target RTO 15 minutes, target RPO 0. Last game day: automated failover worked, but the DNS TTL (600 seconds) delayed user recovery by 10 minutes, and two services retried without a circuit breaker, nearly causing a retry storm. Write 5 English sentences: current state, two gaps, two fixes, one closing line. Use at least 3 phrases from this article.

"Current state: active-active across two regions, automated failover, target RTO fifteen minutes and RPO zero. Gap one: the DNS TTL is six hundred seconds, so users took ten extra minutes to reach the surviving region. Gap two: two services retried without a circuit breaker and nearly caused a retry storm. Fix one: cut the TTL to sixty seconds before the next drill. Fix two: enforce the circuit-breaker library in our shared middleware, with retries on exponential backoff. Resilience is a habit, not a project — we rehearse again next quarter."
